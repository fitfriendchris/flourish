Prep as Worship

For men

Proverbs thirty-one is not a job description for every woman, but it is a picture of stewardship. Someone in the home has to think ahead so that the people under the roof are fed with order instead of chaos.

For women

She rises while it is still dark so that the people she loves will eat with order. That kind of foresight is not about being a perfect wife; it is about stewarding the plate so that the household can function in peace.

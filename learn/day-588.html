Love Abound

For men

Love that does not think becomes harm dressed in kindness. Warmth without wisdom can become the very harm you were trying to prevent.

For women

A daughter's love grows wiser when she lets knowledge shape her tenderness. Let wisdom shape your tenderness so that love lasts longer than exhaustion.

The Berean Habit

For men

Noble minds do not choose between eagerness and discernment; they bring both. Eagerness plus discernment is the mark of a noble mind.

For women

A woman who checks spiritual claims against Scripture is not rebellious; she is noble. A Berean heart is both open and careful.

Live With Understanding

Day 296 of 1,095

Husbands, in the same way, treat your wives with consideration as a delicate vessel, and with honor as fellow heirs of the gracious gift of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered.

1 Peter 3:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A wife is not a problem to manage. She is an heir of the gracious gift of life, and God says the way you treat her can either open or close the door to your own prayers.

For women

Being understood is not a luxury in marriage; it is the air a relationship breathes. The call to live with understanding runs both ways, but Peter aims this command at men because God knows where the gap usually starts.

Leave Margin

Day 123 of 1,095

When you reap the harvest of your land, you are not to reap to the very edges of your field or gather the gleanings of your harvest. You must not strip your vineyard bare or gather its fallen grapes. Leave them for the poor and the foreigner. I am the LORD your God.

Leviticus 19:9-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God does not ask farmers to give everything to the poor. He asks them to stop maximizing so there is something left for the hungry.

For women

You do not have to empty your pantry to be faithful. You only have to stop cleaning it out so completely that nothing remains for the hungry.

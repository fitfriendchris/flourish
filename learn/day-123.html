Day 123 of 1,095Year 1 · Week 18Biblical Economics — God's Design vs Consumerism

Leave Margin

For men

God does not ask farmers to give everything to the poor. He asks them to stop maximizing so there is something left for the hungry.

For women

You do not have to empty your pantry to be faithful. You only have to stop cleaning it out so completely that nothing remains for the hungry.

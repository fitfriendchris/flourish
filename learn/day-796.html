Balance Sleep

Day 796 of 1,095

Do not love sleep, or you will grow poor; open your eyes, and you will have plenty of food.

Proverbs 20:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The object of the verb is love, not sleep. Solomon forbids an affection, not a quantity — and Psalm 127 says God gives sleep to His beloved.

For women

The remedy in the second clause is not work harder. It is open your eyes — the proverb is about attention, and its opposite is not wakefulness but noticing.

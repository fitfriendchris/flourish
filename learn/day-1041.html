Day 1041 of 1,095

Join me in suffering, like a good soldier of Christ Jesus.

2 Timothy 2:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A soldier on active duty does not get tangled in civilian affairs. A Timothy must learn what to leave behind.

For women

Share in suffering is a together-word. Paul did not ask Timothy to suffer alone; he asked him to suffer with him.

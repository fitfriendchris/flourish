Boundaries with Draining People

Day 250 of 1,095

Seldom set foot in your neighbor’s house, lest he grow weary and hate you.

Proverbs 25:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Even good neighbors can grow weary of too much presence; boundaries preserve friendship by preventing exhaustion.

For women

Even the dearest neighbor grows weary of constant presence; boundaries are the guardrails that keep love from collapsing under its own weight.

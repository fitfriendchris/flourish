Reap in Due Season

For men

Weariness is not a sign that the work is wrong. It is a sign that the work is hard and the harvest is still coming. A man who gives up in seedtime forfeits the very crop he planted.

For women

A woman often grows weary because she is doing good in seasons no one else sees. Galatians 6:9 is God's whisper to keep going: in due season you will reap if you do not give up. The unseen labor matters.

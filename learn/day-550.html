Day 550 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 79The Man in the Arena — Character Under Pressure

Advice Feels Like Delay

For men

Counsel is hardest to receive at exactly the moment you most need it, because under pressure listening feels like losing time you do not have.

For women

Cease to hear instruction and you will stray. Straying does not begin with leaving. It begins with no longer listening.

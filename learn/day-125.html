The One Thing

Day 125 of 1,095

Jesus looked at him, loved him, and said to him, “There is one thing you lack: Go, sell everything you own and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow Me.” But the man was saddened by these words and went away in sorrow, because he had great wealth.

Mark 10:21-22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus looked at a man who had kept every command and loved him enough to name the one thing still in the way.

For women

Jesus did not ask the man to give away his money because He needed it. He asked because the man needed to stop needing it.

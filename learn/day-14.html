Day 14 of 1,095

Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

James 4:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

James puts the two verbs in an order. Submit first, then resist. Reverse them and you are just fighting on your own.

For women

Resistance without submission is willpower, and willpower has a shorter battery than you think.

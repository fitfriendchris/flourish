No Place to Lay His Head

Day 201 of 1,095

Jesus replied, “Foxes have dens and birds of the air have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay His head.”

Luke 9:58 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A would-be disciple promises to follow Jesus anywhere. Jesus replies by telling him the truth about his living situation: He has no house. The Son of God was a renter, a guest, a traveler. Following Him does not require a mortgage.

For women

A woman can feel the weight of housing status more sharply than a man. The questions about where you live, whether you own, and what school district you are in can sound like judgments on your whole life. Jesus' answer is relief: the Son of Man had no place to lay His head, and He was enough.

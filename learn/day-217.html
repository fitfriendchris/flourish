When the Wall Falls

For men

A man can own every right policy and still lose everything if the Lord is not building his house. Preparation is wise; trusting preparation is idolatry.

For women

Unless the LORD protects the city, its watchmen stand guard in vain. A woman who has done all she can must finally rest in the One who does what she cannot; her plans are not her God.

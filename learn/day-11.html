Shield of Faith

Day 11 of 1,095

In addition to all this, take up the shield of faith, with which you can extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one.

Ephesians 6:16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Roman shields were soaked in water before battle. Faith does not deflect the dart; it puts it out.

For women

The accusation will arrive. Faith is not pretending it did not — it is what stops it catching light.

Leave, Cleave, Become One Flesh

For men

Marriage requires a leaving, a cleaving, and a becoming. Skip any of the three and the union grows thin.

For women

Becoming one flesh is not automatic. It is the result of leaving old loyalties and cleaving with your whole heart.

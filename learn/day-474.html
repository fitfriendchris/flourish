Test Me in This

Day 474 of 1,095

Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house. Test Me in this,” says the LORD of Hosts. “See if I will not open the windows of heaven and pour out for you blessing without measure.

Malachi 3:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Malachi's audience were not too poor to tithe. They were bringing the blind and lame animals — the problem was contempt, and a new venture is where it starts.

For women

The tithe fed actual people — Levites, widows, the foreigner. Giving in Scripture almost always has a person on the other end.

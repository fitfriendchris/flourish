Rightly Handling

Day 22 of 1,095

Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman who accurately handles the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul's word for handling Scripture is a craftsman's word. It can be done badly, and usually is by people who are sincere.

For women

Sincerity is not the same as accuracy. You can mean well and still cut the line crooked.

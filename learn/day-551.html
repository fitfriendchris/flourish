Abide Means Do Not Leave

Day 551 of 1,095

As the Father has loved Me, so have I loved you. Remain in My love. If you keep My commandments, you will remain in My love, just as I have kept My Father’s commandments and remain in His love. I have told you these things so that My joy may be in you and your joy may be complete.

John 15:9-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Menō is not a mystical word. It means stay, remain, do not depart — and it is an instruction for conditions that make you want to go.

For women

As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you. Kathos — in the same manner and the same degree. Not a lesser grade.

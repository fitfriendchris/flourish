The Daughters Asked

Day 826 of 1,095

Why should the name of our father disappear from his clan because he had no sons? Give us property among our father’s brothers.” So Moses brought their case before the LORD, and the LORD answered him, “The daughters of Zelophehad speak correctly. You certainly must give them property as an inheritance among their father’s brothers and transfer their father’s inheritance to them.

Numbers 27:4-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Five women found a gap in inheritance law, said so in public, and the law was amended. Moses did not know the answer and said so.

For women

Moses did not decide. He said he did not know and took it to God in front of everyone, which is the rarest thing in the chapter.

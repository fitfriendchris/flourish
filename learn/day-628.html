Pure Heart Love

Day 628 of 1,095

Since you have purified your souls by obedience to the truth so that you have a genuine love for your brothers, love one another deeply, from a pure heart. For you have been born again, not of perishable seed, but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.

1 Peter 1:22-23 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Peter says that the goal of obedience to the truth is sincere brotherly love. A pure heart is not a private achievement. It is the source from which a man loves the brothers around him.

For women

Peter calls the whole church to sincere brotherly love from a pure heart. Women often carry the emotional work of community, and this verse reminds them that the work must flow from a soul that has been purified by obedience to the truth.

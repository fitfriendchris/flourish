Day 746 of 1,095

Hold on to the pattern of sound teaching you have heard from me, with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. Guard the treasure entrusted to you, with the help of the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

2 Timothy 1:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Two years of teaching have given you a shape of truth. Year three is not about finding a better gospel. It is about keeping the pattern and handing it to the next man.

For women

After two years you carry an imprint of the faith. Mastery means guarding that shape in faith and love, and then passing it to another woman.

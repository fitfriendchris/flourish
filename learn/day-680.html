Thanks Good

Day 680 of 1,095

Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever.

Psalm 107:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The long road back does not end with arrival; it ends with thanks. Gratitude is the proof that the failure has been handed over to a good God whose love outlasts the journey.

For women

A woman often serves as the thanksgiving center of a household. Her choice to give thanks to the LORD when the road is still long teaches everyone under her roof that God's goodness is not waiting for the finish line.

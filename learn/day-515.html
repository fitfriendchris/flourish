Roll It Onto Him

Day 515 of 1,095

Commit your works to the LORD and your plans will be achieved.

Proverbs 16:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Commit is literally roll — the word for rolling a load off your own back. And the neighbouring verses make clear that what gets established may not be the plan you brought.

For women

The word for plans is machashavah — thoughts, devisings. Roll the work, and the thought-life is what gets settled.

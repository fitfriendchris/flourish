Walls Rebuilt

Day 805 of 1,095

Like a city whose walls are broken down is a man who does not control his temper.

Proverbs 25:28 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Solomon's city is not open ground. It is broken through — which means it had walls, and something took them down.

For women

The simile is a city, not a house and not a man. Other people live inside the thing that got opened.

Borrower Slave

For men

Debt is not just a number. It is a change of master. The lender rules; the borrower serves. That is the plain Hebrew meaning of Proverbs 22:7, and it is why the Bible treats debt as a sovereignty issue before it is a math problem.

For women

The woman who borrows quietly often believes she is protecting someone else. Proverbs 22:7 asks a harder question: who is now ruling the peace you were trying to keep?

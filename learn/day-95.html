Table as Altar

For men

The early church did not think of meals as interruptions to doctrine. meals with gladness and sincerity of heart because the table was one of the places where the gospel was practiced out loud.

For women

The first Christians did not separate worship from supper. They broke bread from house to house, sharing their meals every day with gladness and sincerity of heart, which means the kitchen table can be as holy as the temple courtyard.

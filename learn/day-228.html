Abraham Gave All He Had to Isaac

Day 228 of 1,095

Abraham left everything he owned to Isaac. But while he was still alive, Abraham gave gifts to the sons of his concubines and sent them away from his son Isaac to the land of the east.

Genesis 25:5-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Abraham transferred wealth before he died. Isaac received the promise; the others received gifts and distance. Clarity protected both the heir and the wider family.

For women

A woman often sees the emotional currents in a family that legal documents ignore. Abraham's careful distribution honored both the promise and the people on the edges of it.

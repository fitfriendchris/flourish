Day 16 of 1,095Year 1 · Week 3The Mind Battlefield — Taking Every Thought Captive

Pure Focus

For men

Paul does not tell you to stop thinking about the wrong thing. He tells you what to think about instead. Attention moves; it does not switch off.

For women

You will not empty your mind. You can only fill it with something better.

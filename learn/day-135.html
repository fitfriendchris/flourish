Owe Nothing But Love

For men

Paul does not forbid all borrowing. He forbids every obligation that competes with love. If you are so loaded with payments that you cannot help your brother, the debt has already broken the command.

For women

A woman is often told that love means sacrifice, and it does. But Romans 13:8 says the only permanent debt is love — not the guilt, not the endless yes, and not the silent loan you made to keep someone happy.

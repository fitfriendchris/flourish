Early Rising

Day 39 of 1,095

Early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up and went out to a solitary place to pray.

Mark 1:35 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus had the busiest ministry in history and the response was to get up earlier, not to sleep in.

For women

He went somewhere alone before the day could make its claims on Him.

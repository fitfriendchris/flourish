Bear Much Fruit

Day 1089 of 1,095

This is to My Father’s glory, that you bear much fruit, proving yourselves to be My disciples.

John 15:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

This is to My Father’s glory, that you bear much fruit. The aim of three years of growth is fruit that shows whose disciple you are.

For women

Much fruit comes from abiding. The graduation is not the end of staying close; it is the beginning of bearing.

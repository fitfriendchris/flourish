Perfectly One

For men

Jesus prays for the future church. His deepest desire is not success, protection, or comfort, but that his people would be one as the Father and Son are one.

For women

Jesus prays for every believer who will come through the apostles' word, and that includes women. His longing for unity is especially urgent for sisters who are often tempted to compare, compete, or withdraw.

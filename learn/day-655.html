Day 655 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 94Leading from the Second Chair — Faithfulness Before Title

Account God

For men

The second-chair man cannot hide behind the first chair at the judgment seat. Each of us will give an account of himself to God.

For women

A woman who knows she will give an account of herself to God stops measuring her life by comparison and starts stewarding the part that is only hers.

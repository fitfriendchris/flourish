Gluttony Warning

Day 93 of 1,095

Do not join those who drink too much wine or gorge themselves on meat. For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe them in rags.

Proverbs 23:20-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The church will warn a man about his drinking before it warns him about his eating, but Proverbs puts them in the same sentence. Gluttony is the sin nobody preaches, and that silence has made it easy to hide in plain sight.

For women

We preach against drunkenness and stay silent about the second dessert, the secret drive-thru, and the plate we clean before our body has said it is full. Proverbs does not stay silent, and neither should you.

Work as for the Lord

Day 244 of 1,095

Slaves, obey your earthly masters in everything, not only to please them while they are watching, but with sincerity of heart and fear of the Lord. Whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, as for the Lord and not for men, because you know that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as your reward. It is the Lord Christ you are serving. Whoever does wrong will be repaid for his wrong, and there is no favoritism.

Colossians 3:22-25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Your boss is not your god, but your work is an act of worship. Colossians teaches a workplace ethic that honors Christ without endorsing oppression.

For women

A woman's work at home, in an office, or in service to others is not invisible to God. Whatever you do, you are serving. Whoever does wrong.

Work as for the Lord

For men

Your boss is not your god, but your work is an act of worship. Colossians teaches a workplace ethic that honors Christ without endorsing oppression.

For women

A woman's work at home, in an office, or in service to others is not invisible to God. Whatever you do, you are serving. Whoever does wrong.

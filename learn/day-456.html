Increase Decrease

Day 456 of 1,095

He must increase; I must decrease.

John 3:30 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

John says it while watching his own crowd leave for someone else. His joy is not despite the decrease — it is complete because of it.

For women

His disciples brought him a complaint about losing the crowd. He answered by calling his joy complete.

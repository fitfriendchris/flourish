Not of Blood

Day 770 of 1,095

But to all who did receive Him, to those who believed in His name, He gave the right to become children of God— children born not of blood, nor of the desire or will of man, but born of God.

John 1:12-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Three negatives before the positive, and the third one is the will of man. That is the sentence every parent eventually has to read carefully.

For women

He gave the right to become. Receiving is instant and becoming is not, and the verse puts both in the same sentence without apology.

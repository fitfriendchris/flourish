Pay careful attention, then, to how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

Ephesians 5:15-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Look carefully is not a call to be busy. It is a call to know what your time is actually being spent on.

For women

redeeming the time is not the same as being productive every minute. It means knowing whether your leisure is feeding you or hiding you.

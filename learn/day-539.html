Sworn to Your Fathers

For men

The wealth is given to confirm a covenant sworn to previous generations. You are in the middle of a chain, not at the start of a fortune.

For women

Your clothing did not wear out and your foot did not swell, these forty years. Nobody writes a testimony about their shoes.

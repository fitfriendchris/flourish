Eye On You

For men

Year one taught you that God sees. Year two asks what it means to live as if that seeing matters when the resources run low and the soul feels empty. The eyes of the LORD are not passive surveillance. It is active deliverance.

For women

Year one taught you that God sees you. Year two asks whether you will let that seeing steady you when the pantry is bare and the heart is anxious. The eyes of the LORD are not merely observation. It is covenant care.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

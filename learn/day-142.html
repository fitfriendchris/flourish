Hedonic Treadmill

For men

Qoheleth saw the treadmill three thousand years before psychologists named it. Loving money is not a financial problem; it is a satisfaction problem, and the cure is not more money.

For women

The woman who says 'I'll be happy when...' is already on the treadmill. Ecclesiastes 5:10 says the finish line will move the moment she reaches it.

Rest is the program

For men

Your muscles do not grow in the gym. They grow while you sleep. Your faith does not grow only in effort; it grows when you stop trying to earn what has already been given.

For women

You have probably been told that rest is what you earn after everything else is done. The gospel says rest is where you start, because Christ has already finished what you could never finish.

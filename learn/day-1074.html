A Crown of Life

Day 1074 of 1,095

Blessed is the man who perseveres under trial, because when he has stood the test, he will receive the crown of life that God has promised to those who love Him.

James 1:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The crown of life is promised to those who love Him. Endurance is the evidence; love is the root.

For women

Love is what makes endurance possible. A woman who remains under trial because she loves God is already living the crown.

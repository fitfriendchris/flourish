When Your Son Asks

Day 879 of 1,095

In the future, when your son asks, “What is the meaning of the decrees and statutes and ordinances that the LORD our God has commanded you?” then you are to tell him, “We were slaves of Pharaoh in Egypt, but the LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand.

Deuteronomy 6:20-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The answer Moses scripts is not a definition. It is a story about slaves, and the child asked a question about rules.

For women

Most of the people told to say we were slaves had been born in the wilderness and never seen Egypt.

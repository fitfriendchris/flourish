Glory Revealed

For men

Your present sufferings — in body, money, and relationships — are not comparable to the glory that will going will be revealed in you. Steward today in light of that future.

For women

A woman's present sufferings are often unseen — the miscarriage, the estrangement, the financial squeeze, the weary body. Romans 8:18 does not deny them; it gives them a scale small enough that glory can outweigh them.

Grace Trains

For men

Grace is not permission to coast into next year. It is the teacher who trains you to renounce what kills you and to live with your eyes on the coming King.

For women

Grace is not a blanket excuse for a messy life. It is the patient teacher who is training you for the next chapter and the coming King.

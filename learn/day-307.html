Eli: The Failure to Restrain

For men

Eli knew what his sons were doing. God says so. His failure was not ignorance; it was a refusal to use his authority to stop them.

For women

A mother who sees her child sinning and does nothing is not being loving. She is being Eli. Love restrains, even when restraint is costly.

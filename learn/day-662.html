Suffering Produces

Day 662 of 1,095

Not only that, but we also rejoice in our sufferings, because we know that suffering produces perseverance; perseverance, character; and character, hope. And hope does not disappoint us, because God has poured out His love into our hearts through the Holy Spirit, whom He has given us.

Romans 5:3-5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Year two is not a detour from the curriculum; it is the curriculum. Suffering produces endurance, perseverance; perseverance, character; and character, hope that will not embarrass you.

For women

The second year proves what the first year only promised. Suffering is not a sign that something has gone wrong; it is the furnace where endurance, character, and hope are formed.

Day 662 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 95The Discipline of Endurance — Year Two Perseverance

Suffering Produces

For men

Year two is not a detour from the curriculum; it is the curriculum. Suffering produces endurance, perseverance; perseverance, character; and character, hope that will not embarrass you.

For women

The second year proves what the first year only promised. Suffering is not a sign that something has gone wrong; it is the furnace where endurance, character, and hope are formed.

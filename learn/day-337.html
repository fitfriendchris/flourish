Moses at Eighty

For men

God's call does not wait for your résumé to feel ready. Eighty years of life had not made Moses too old; they had made him ready.

For women

A woman's later seasons can be her most fruitful when she stops performing and starts listening. The same desert that broke Moses also prepared him to hear God when the bush burned.

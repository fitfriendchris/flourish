Why decisions feel heavy

Day 78 of 1,095

A horse is prepared for the day of battle, but victory is of the LORD.

Proverbs 21:31 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You can prepare, budget, interview, pray, and still wake up unsure if you chose right. That weight is not always lack of faith; sometimes it is the honest feel of responsibility in a world you do not control.

For women

Some mornings the weight is not the decision itself but the fear that one wrong move will unravel everything. The question underneath is usually this: will I still be loved, safe, and held if I choose wrong?

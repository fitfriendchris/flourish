A Dry Morsel

Day 935 of 1,095

Better a dry morsel in quietness than a house full of feasting with strife.

Proverbs 17:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A feast can be a place of strife. The Hebrew calls it a house full of sacrifices, and quarrels can happen at religious tables too.

For women

A dry morsel is a choice. Some households need less abundance and more peace, and a woman often sees the trade first.

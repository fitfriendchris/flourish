Crucified With

Day 782 of 1,095

I have been crucified with Christ, and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself up for me.

Galatians 2:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The sentence does not stop where it usually gets quoted. Paul says he no longer lives, and then says the life he now lives in the flesh — in consecutive clauses.

For women

Galatians is a corporate argument about law and grace from start to finish, and here, once, Paul drops into the singular. Who loved me and gave himself for me.

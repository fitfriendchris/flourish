Spirit-Filled Craft

For men

The first person in the Bible described as filled with the Spirit was not a prophet or a king. He was an artist.

For women

Ability, intelligence, knowledge and craftsmanship are all described as gifts of the Spirit. Your making is not separate from your devotion.

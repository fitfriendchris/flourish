Day 988 of 1,095

because I rescued the poor who cried out and the fatherless who had no helper. The dying man blessed me, and I made the widow’s heart sing for joy.

For men

Job's influence in the gate was measured by the people he rescued. Public honour followed public advocacy.

For women

I made the widow’s heart sing. Advocacy can do more than rescue; it can restore joy.

Vanity vs stewardship

Day 104 of 1,095

But the LORD said to Samuel, “Do not consider his appearance or height, for I have rejected him; the LORD does not see as man does. For man sees the outward appearance, but the LORD sees the heart.”

1 Samuel 16:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Samuel was picking a king by the only standard he had - how the man looked. God stopped him cold: appearance is not the metric, and never was.

For women

The world trains women to believe that appearance is currency. God says it is not even the measure he uses when he chooses a king. That is not an invitation to neglect yourself; it is an invitation to stop financing your self-worth with your looks.

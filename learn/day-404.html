Faith Hearing

Day 404 of 1,095

Consequently, faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of Christ.

Romans 10:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul quotes Isaiah before he draws the conclusion, and the word he uses means both the hearing and the thing heard. Faith comes from a report.

For women

You did not write most of the running commentary you carry. It came in by ear, in sentences, from people with standing.

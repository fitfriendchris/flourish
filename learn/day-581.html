Bear Weak

Day 581 of 1,095

We who are strong ought to bear with the shortcomings of the weak and not to please ourselves. Each of us should please his neighbor for his good, to build him up.

Romans 15:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Today, as a father, the obligation is immediate: the strong members of the family must carry the weak ones.

For women

Today a mother learns that her strength is not for her own ease but for building up the people in her home who cannot yet carry themselves.

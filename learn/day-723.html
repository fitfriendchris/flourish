A man’s heart plans his course, but the LORD determines his steps.

Proverbs 16:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A builder makes plans, but the ground beneath the plan belongs to God. As year two ends, the commencement posture is this: hold your blueprint loosely and your obedience tightly.

For women

A woman plans her days around people she cannot control. Year two's commencement lesson is that her planning and her surrender must learn to breathe together; the Lord establishes every step she takes.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

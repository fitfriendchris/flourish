Day 88 of 1,095Year 1 · Week 13Temple Theology — Why Your Body Matters to God

Incarnation Argument

For men

God did not send a message about bodies. He became one. That is the argument against every philosophy that treats the body as secondary.

For women

If God was willing to become a body, then your body is not a mistake to rise above. The Word did not hover above us. He became one of us.

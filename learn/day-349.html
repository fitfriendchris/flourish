Morning Mercies

For men

Mercy is not a one-time rescue. It is a daily renewal. The same God who judged Jerusalem still opened the door of compassion every single morning. A man who knows this stops living from yesterday's failure.

For women

A woman's heart often rehearses yesterday's failures before the day begins. Lamentations gives you a better morning ritual: His mercies are new every morning. You do not have to earn the sunrise; it comes because He is faithful.

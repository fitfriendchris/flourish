The Land Shall Keep

Day 1066 of 1,095

But in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath of complete rest for the land—a Sabbath to the LORD. You are not to sow your field or prune your vineyard.

Leviticus 25:4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The land's Sabbath year was a business Sabbath. A man trusts God enough to let his income field lie fallow.

For women

The Sabbath of the land shall provide food for you, your servants, the hired worker, and the sojourner. Rest feeds the vulnerable too.

Peacemakers, Not Peacekeepers

Day 260 of 1,095

Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called sons of God.

Matthew 5:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus does not bless the peacekeepers who keep everyone comfortable. He blesses the peacemakers who step into conflict to restore what is broken.

For women

True peace is not the absence of conflict. It is the presence of righteousness and restored relationship, and the woman who builds it reflects the family of God.

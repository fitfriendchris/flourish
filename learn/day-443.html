Practice Sinning

Day 443 of 1,095

Everyone who practices sin practices lawlessness as well. Indeed, sin is lawlessness.

1 John 3:4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

John's whole argument rests on tense. Every verb in the passage is continuous — he is describing a practice, not an incident.

For women

Your real theology is not what you affirm. It is what you do when you are not paying attention.

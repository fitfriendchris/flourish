Day 652 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 93Calling Confirmed — Gifts, Burdens, and Open Doors

Finish Race

For men

Paul is at the finish line. He does not say he was famous or comfortable. He says he finished. Calling is confirmed at the end, not at the beginning. The crown is for the one who has loved Christ's appearing enough to keep going.

For women

A woman who loves the appearing of Jesus will run the race differently. She will not waste her energy on performances that fade. She will fight, finish, and keep the faith in the hidden places where crowns are won.

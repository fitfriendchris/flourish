But you, be sober in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry. For I am already being poured out like a drink offering, and the time of my departure is at hand. I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith. From now on there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day—and not only to me, but to all who crave His appearing.

2 Timothy 4:5-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul is at the finish line. He does not say he was famous or comfortable. He says he finished. Calling is confirmed at the end, not at the beginning. The crown is for the one who has loved Christ's appearing enough to keep going.

For women

A woman who loves the appearing of Jesus will run the race differently. She will not waste her energy on performances that fade. She will fight, finish, and keep the faith in the hidden places where crowns are won.

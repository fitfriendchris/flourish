When Work Becomes Identity

For men

Babel was not a building project that got out of hand. It was an identity project. The people wanted to make a name for themselves, and they were willing to build a tower to do it.

For women

When your work becomes your name, every success is a high and every failure is a fall. Babel is the warning; the gospel is the exit.

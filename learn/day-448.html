Help My Unbelief

Day 448 of 1,095

“If You can?” echoed Jesus. “All things are possible to him who believes!” Immediately the boy’s father cried out, “I do believe; help my unbelief!”

Mark 9:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

I believe; help my unbelief. He does not resolve the contradiction before asking, and he gets the answer anyway.

For women

He is not asking for his doubt to be excused. He is asking for help with it, which is a different and better request.

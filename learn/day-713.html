Day 713 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 102Strengthening the Weak Pillar — Honest Assessment

Worthy Gospel

For men

Honest assessment is not private navel-gazing. It is a check on whether your life looks like the gospel you claim to believe.

For women

Honest assessment asks whether your private life matches the gospel you say you believe. The weak pillar is often the gap between what you profess and what you practice.

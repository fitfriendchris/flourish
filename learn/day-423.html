Not Shaken

Day 423 of 1,095

I have set the LORD always before me. Because He is at my right hand, I will not be shaken.

Psalm 16:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

I have SET the LORD before me. It is a placing verb — something done deliberately, and done again.

For women

Strength is what you can move. Stability is whether you hold when the load arrives from an angle you did not pick.

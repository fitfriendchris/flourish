Day 732 of 1,095Year 3 · Week 105The Shepherd's Own Soul — Guarding the Guide

Filled Spirit

For men

Year three is not about discovering the Spirit. It is about letting him govern the spaces you have been filling with lesser things. Let the Spirit govern the spaces you have filled with lesser masters.

For women

A woman in year three does not need more information about the Spirit. She needs the Spirit to have the first place in her crowded heart. Give the Holy Spirit the first place in the crowded center of your life.

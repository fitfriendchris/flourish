Sincere Faith in Three Generations

Day 354 of 1,095

I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced is in you as well.

2 Timothy 1:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul names the women who made Timothy. A man's faith does not appear from nowhere; it is passed through living examples.

For women

Paul names the women who passed faith to Timothy. Their gender is not the point. The point is that their faith was real enough to dwell in and real enough to hand down.

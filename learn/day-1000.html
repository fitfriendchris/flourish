Sincere Faith Dwelt

Day 1000 of 1,095

I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first dwelt in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am convinced is in you as well.

2 Timothy 1:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Timothy's faith came through his mother and grandmother while his father was an unbeliever. A man's faith can take root even where his own father did not plant it.

For women

Dwelt is the word for living in a house. Faith took up residence in Lois, then Eunice, then Timothy.

Let Your Yes Be Yes

For men

A man's word is either a currency people can trade on or a decoration they learn to ignore. This is the quiet work of character in a world that prefers image.

For women

A woman's word becomes powerful when it is scarce and true; every unnecessary vow is a confession that her ordinary yes was not enough.

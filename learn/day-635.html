Search Me

Day 635 of 1,095

Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my concerns. See if there is any offensive way in me; lead me in the way everlasting.

Psalm 139:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You cannot review a quarter honestly if you will not let God review you first. The Psalmist invites the searchlight because he wants the way everlasting more than he wants to be right.

For women

A woman who asks God to search her is not weak; she is brave. The same God who sees the hidden things also loves the hidden you, and He will not leave you in what He finds.

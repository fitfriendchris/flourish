Sabbath as Resistance

For men

The Sabbath command is a weekly vote against going back to slavery. The machine does not own you unless you keep acting like it does.

For women

The Sabbath command in Deuteronomy is for the whole household, not just the person with the best schedule. A woman who rests while others still serve has kept part of it; a household that rests together has kept the whole.

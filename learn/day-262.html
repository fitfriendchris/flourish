A Soft Answer Turns Away Wrath

For men

The first response in a heated moment sets the trajectory. A soft answer does not mean a weak answer; it means a wise one that lowers the temperature.

For women

A woman's response in a tense moment can either pour gasoline or water on the fire. The Proverb invites her to become the one who turns wrath away.

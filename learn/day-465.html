Firstfruits Presume a Harvest

Day 465 of 1,095

Honor the LORD with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all your crops; then your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will overflow with new wine.

Proverbs 3:9-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You cannot give the first of something you have never valued. Firstfruits assumes a man who knows what his crop is worth.

For women

The offering had to be the first and the unblemished — which presumes a woman who knows exactly what her harvest contains.

He May Be a Fool

Day 824 of 1,095

I hated all for which I had toiled under the sun, because I must leave it to the man who comes after me. And who knows whether that man will be wise or foolish? Yet he will take over all the labor at which I have worked skillfully under the sun. This too is futile. So my heart began to despair over all the labor that I had done under the sun. When there is a man who has labored with wisdom, knowledge, and skill, and he must give his portion to a man who has not worked for it, this too is futile and a great evil.

Ecclesiastes 2:18-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

This is the honest objection to everything the last two days said, and Scripture put it in the canon rather than answering it too quickly.

For women

Under the sun appears nine times in this chapter. He is telling you exactly where he is standing when he does the arithmetic.

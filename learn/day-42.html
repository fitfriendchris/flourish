Perfect Peace

Day 42 of 1,095

You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind, because he trusts in You.

Isaiah 26:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Isaiah gives a mechanism, not a mood: peace is kept for the mind that is stayed. The staying is the part you do.

For women

Peace here is not something you feel your way into. It follows where your mind has settled.

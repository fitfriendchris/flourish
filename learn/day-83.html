When Scripture is silent

For men

Paul wanted to go east. The Spirit said no, twice. Then a vision redirected him west. But remember 2 Corinthians 2:12: Paul later walked away from an open door because the people he cared about were not ready. Open doors are not always orders from God.

For women

Not every question has a verse waiting for you. Some decisions are lawful, wise, and free. The silence of Scripture on your spouse or your city is not neglect. It is room for you to walk with God as a daughter, not a puppet.

Delighted Meditation

Day 314 of 1,095

But his delight is in the Law of the LORD, and on His law he meditates day and night. He is like a tree planted by streams of water, yielding its fruit in season, whose leaf does not wither, and who prospers in all he does.

Psalm 1:2-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The godly man is not defined by what he avoids. He is defined by what he delights in. Meditation is the discipline of sustained delight.

For women

A flourishing woman is not defined by her schedule. She is defined by what she lets her mind return to again and again.

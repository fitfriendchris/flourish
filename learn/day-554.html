Paul Quotes a Playwright

Day 554 of 1,095

Do not be deceived: “Bad company corrupts good character.” Sober up as you ought, and stop sinning; for some of you are ignorant of God. I say this to your shame.

1 Corinthians 15:33-34 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Bad company corrupts good character.” Sober is a line from a Greek comedy, and Paul aims it at people whose beliefs — not whose behaviour — were hollowing out the church.

For women

Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die is itself a quotation — the words of a city partying while its walls were being breached.

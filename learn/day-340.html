Tentmakers Teach Apollos

For men

The best teachers are often the most teachable. Pride refuses correction; growth invites it. The most gifted teacher still needs a tentmaker to keep him accurate.

For women

A teachable woman knows that the Spirit often speaks through ordinary people with accurate understanding.

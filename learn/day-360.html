Stones of Remembrance

For men

A man who forgets the crossings will fear every river he meets. Memorials keep courage portable. Stack the stones so your future self remembers who carried you through.

For women

A woman who marks the crossings gives her future self a place to stand when fear comes flooding back.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

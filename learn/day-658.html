Fear Not Suffer

For men

Faithfulness in the second chair may cost you something. Jesus tells the church at Smyrna to stop fearing the suffering that is coming and to remain faithful even to death.

For women

A woman in the second chair often suffers quietly. Jesus sees it, limits it, and promises that faithfulness unto death will receive the crown of life.

And the Fullness Thereof

For men

David grounds God's ownership in creation, and then asks who may come near the Owner. The answer is about hands — which is where a stewardship week has to look.

For women

The psalm is a processional. It ends at a gate, with a crowd calling to have it opened — and the one who ascends receives rather than brings.

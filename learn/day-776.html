Act Like Men

Day 776 of 1,095

Be on the alert. Stand firm in the faith. Be men of courage. Be strong. Do everything in love.

1 Corinthians 16:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Five imperatives, and the fifth one governs the other four. Paul builds a wall and then puts a door in it.

For women

The verb is andrizesthe — literally act like men — and Paul writes it to a whole congregation. In the Greek Old Testament it is what Joshua is told.

Fruit Spirit

For men

Nine words, one noun. Paul writes fruit, singular — against works of the flesh, plural. You manufacture works. You do not manufacture fruit.

For women

Read as nine items it is a report card. Read as one fruit it is a diagnosis, and a kinder one.

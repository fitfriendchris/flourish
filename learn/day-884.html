But as for you, continue in the things you have learned and firmly believed, since you know from whom you have learned them. From infancy you have known the Holy Scriptures, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.

2 Timothy 3:14-15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

since you know from whom you have learned them. Paul grounds the reliability of the content in the reliability of the people it came through.

For women

Apo brephous — from infancy. Paul dates it to before Timothy could evaluate anything, and treats that as the foundation rather than a lesser start.

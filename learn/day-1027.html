Thirty Sixty Hundred

Day 1027 of 1,095

Still others are like the seeds sown on good soil. They hear the word, receive it, and produce a crop—thirtyfold, sixtyfold, or a hundredfold.”

Mark 4:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Hear, accept, bear fruit. The good soil welcomes the word, and welcoming is a choice before it is a harvest.

For women

Thirtyfold is not failure. The good soil produces different yields, and all of them are good soil.

Love One Another

Day 631 of 1,095

A new commandment I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you also must love one another. By this everyone will know that you are My disciples, if you love one another.”

John 13:34-35 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

On the night before his death, Jesus gives a new commandment. His people are to love each other the way he has loved them. This love is the distinguishing mark of a disciple.

For women

Jesus gives this command to all his disciples, including every woman who follows him. A sister council is Christian to the extent that its members love one another the way Jesus has loved them.

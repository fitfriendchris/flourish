The Race Is Not to the Swift

For men

Your plan was not wrong because you failed. It was wrong because you thought the outcome belonged to your speed, strength, or intelligence.

For women

Your plan was not wrong because life was unfair. It was wrong because it assumed you could see the finish line from the starting blocks.

Set Your House in Order

For men

Hezekiah was dying, and the prophet did not offer a miracle first. He offered a command: says: ‘Put your house in order. The order was love, not panic.

For women

A woman often holds the household together invisibly. Setting the house in order means making that hidden care visible, written, and reachable by the people who will need it.

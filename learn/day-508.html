The Leaf That Falls and the Leaf That Sprouts

Day 508 of 1,095

He who trusts in his riches will fall, but the righteous will thrive like foliage.

Proverbs 11:28 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Both halves of the proverb are plant images. The man who trusts his riches falls like a leaf; the righteous sprouts like one. Same object, opposite direction.

For women

Falling and flourishing are both what leaves do. Same object, opposite direction — and the difference is what it is attached to.

Day 704 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 101The Quiet Victories — Faithfulness No One Sees

Remember Fallen

For men

The church at Ephesus had a long resume and a cooling heart. The quiet victory today is remembering where you fell and returning to first love before anyone had to confront you.

For women

A woman can maintain an impressive spiritual household and still lose the warmth of first love. The quiet victory is stopping long enough to remember where she fell and returning to Jesus before the cold becomes normal.

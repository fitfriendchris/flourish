When It Fails

Day 877 of 1,095

I tell you, use worldly wealth to make friends for yourselves so that when it is gone, they will welcome you into eternal dwellings.

Luke 16:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Hotan eklipe — when it fails, not if. The whole strategy is built on the assumption that the money runs out.

For women

The only thing that survives the failure of the instrument is the people it was spent on.

Entrusted Gospel

For men

The gospel is not your private opinion; it has been entrusted to you by God. Audit your calendar and your bank statement, because those two ledgers reveal what you actually believe is worth carrying to the world.

For women

The gospel entrusted to you is not a performance; it is a life laid down. A woman's calendar and bank statement also preach, and kingdom economics asks whether they tell the same story as her lips.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

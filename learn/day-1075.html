An Imperishable Wreath

Day 1075 of 1,095

Everyone who competes in the games trains with strict discipline. They do it for a crown that is perishable, but we do it for a crown that is imperishable.

1 Corinthians 9:25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Everyone who competes in the games trains with strict discipline. The discipline that built a man's body can train his soul for a crown that lasts.

For women

A perishable wreath at Corinth was made of withered celery or pine. The world's crowns are dry before the celebration ends.

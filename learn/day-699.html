Cloud Witnesses

For men

Faithfulness is not a solo sport. A great cloud of witnesses has already run this race, and their endurance calls you to throw off every encumbrance and keep running toward the integrated life Christ has set before you.

For women

A woman's race is often invisible, but the cloud of witnesses sees it. The same faith that carried Sarah, Rahab, and Mary can carry her through the ordinary marathons of love and duty.

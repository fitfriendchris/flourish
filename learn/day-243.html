Honor Up, Nurture Down

Day 243 of 1,095

Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. “Honor your father and mother” (which is the first commandment with a promise), “that it may go well with you and that you may have a long life on the earth.” Fathers, do not provoke your children to wrath; instead, bring them up in the discipline and instruction of the Lord.

Ephesians 6:1-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Authority in the home flows downward, but honor flows upward. Children obey. Parents discipline without crushing. Both are forms of love.

For women

A mother's honor flows upward to parents and downward to children. She teaches the next generation how to obey and how to be nurtured without being crushed.

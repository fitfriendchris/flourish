Gold Does Not Corrode

For men

James says their gold and silver have rusted. He knows precious metals do not rust. The corrosion is the point — it is evidence of money that sat still.

For women

You have fattened your hearts in a day of slaughter — livestock eating well, on the morning they are to be killed, noticing nothing.

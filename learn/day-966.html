Day 966 of 1,095Year 3 · Week 138The Restorer's Path — Bringing Back the Wanderer

Seek the Strayed

For men

God's promise to seek the lost is the exact reversal of what Israel's shepherds had failed to do. Leaders who do not restore are named in the chapter.

For women

Bind up the broken. Many wanderers left because they were hurt, and restoration begins with the wound.

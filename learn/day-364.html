The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy. I have come that they may have life, and have it in all its fullness.

John 10:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Abundant does not mean more. It means full — and Jesus set it against a thief who offers the counterfeit.

For women

He did not promise you a bigger life. He promised you a full one, which is a different thing entirely.

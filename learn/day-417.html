Temptation Common

Day 417 of 1,095

No temptation has seized you except what is common to man. And God is faithful; He will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear. But when you are tempted, He will also provide an escape, so that you can stand up under it.

1 Corinthians 10:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Read the end of the sentence: it is a way out that you may be able to ENDURE it. The exit is often through.

For women

The escape is described as a way to endure it, not a way out of it. That is harder, and it is honest.

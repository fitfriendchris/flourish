The Night Watch

For men

David found God through the watches of the night. A 3am wake-up can be a meeting, not just a malfunction.

For women

The women in 1 Samuel also woke in the night. Hannah prayed until Eli thought she was drunk, and the boy Samuel heard his name in the dark.

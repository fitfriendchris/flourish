The Night Watch

Day 110 of 1,095

My soul is satisfied as with the richest of foods; with joyful lips my mouth will praise You. When I remember You on my bed, I think of You through the watches of the night.

Psalm 63:5-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

David found God through the watches of the night. A 3am wake-up can be a meeting, not just a malfunction.

For women

The women in 1 Samuel also woke in the night. Hannah prayed until Eli thought she was drunk, and the boy Samuel heard his name in the dark.

Our Peace

Day 625 of 1,095

For He Himself is our peace, who has made the two one and has torn down the dividing wall of hostility by abolishing in His flesh the law of commandments and decrees. He did this to create in Himself one new man out of the two, thus making peace and reconciling both of them to God in one body through the cross, by which He put to death their hostility.

Ephesians 2:14-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul tells the Ephesian church that Christ is not merely a teacher of peace; he is our peace. He demolished the wall that divided Jew and Gentile and made one new humanity by the cross.

For women

Paul writes to the whole church, and women are often the ones who maintain the relational fabric that keeps a community from splintering. The cross gives women a basis for sisterhood that is stronger than shared history or similar stage of life.

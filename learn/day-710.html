Vain Labor

For men

A weak pillar often signals vain labor: effort that looks impressive but lacks God's blessing. Honest assessment asks whether the Lord is building your house.

For women

A weak pillar is often a sign that the labor has been vain: hard work without the Lord's building and watching.

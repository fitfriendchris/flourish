But a time is coming and has now come when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and in truth, for the Father is seeking such as these to worship Him. God is Spirit, and His worshipers must worship Him in spirit and in truth.”

John 4:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Father is seeking such people to worship him. In a chapter that opened with Jesus asking a woman for water, the seeking keeps running the wrong direction.

For women

She asked a question about location — this mountain or Jerusalem. He answered by removing location from the conversation entirely.

The Sons of Rechab

For men

The Rechabites kept their ancestor's command for two hundred and fifty years. A father's instructions can shape a family long after he is gone.

For women

Neither you nor your descendants are ever to drink wine. The Rechabite command shaped a whole household culture, and the wives and daughters kept it too.

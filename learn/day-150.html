Skilled Before Kings

For men

A skill so useful that kings send for the person who has it is not fame; it is public usefulness. The proverb is about being sought, not about seeking attention.

For women

Skill is a magnet. Mediocrity has to push itself forward; excellence is pulled into the room where it is needed.

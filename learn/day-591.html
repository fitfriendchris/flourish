Love Neighbor

Day 591 of 1,095

Do not seek revenge or bear a grudge against any of your people, but love your neighbor as yourself. I am the LORD.

Leviticus 19:18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The command to love your neighbor does not appear first in the New Testament. It is woven into the fabric of Israel's covenant life, and it comes with a warning against the vengeance that poisons community.

For women

The command to love your neighbor is not a feeling command. It is a justice command, placed in the middle of Israel's daily life so that the community would not dissolve into grudges.

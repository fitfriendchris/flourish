Be Steadfast Immovable

Day 1093 of 1,095

Therefore, my beloved brothers, be steadfast and immovable. Always excel in the work of the Lord, because you know that your labor in the Lord is not in vain.

1 Corinthians 15:58 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Your labour in the Lord is not in vain. The resurrection is the reason three years of work, and a lifetime more, will count.

For women

In the Lord. The labour that is not in vain is labour done in union with Christ.

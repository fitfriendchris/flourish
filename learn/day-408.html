Called by Name

Day 408 of 1,095

But now, this is what the LORD says—He who created you, O Jacob, and He who formed you, O Israel: “Do not fear, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; you are Mine! When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and when you go through the rivers, they will not overwhelm you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be scorched; the flames will not set you ablaze.

Isaiah 43:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Year one taught you who owns you. Year two puts that ownership to the test when the waters rise and the fire heats up. You are called by name before the trial begins, and the name does not expire in the middle of it.

For women

Year one taught you that you belong to God. Year two tests that belonging when the emotions are loud and the circumstances are dark. You are not abandoned in the river. You are called by name inside it.

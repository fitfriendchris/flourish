Not Too Hastily

Day 1069 of 1,095

Do not be too quick in the laying on of hands and thereby share in the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.

1 Timothy 5:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Laying hands too hastily makes you a partner in another's sins. Commissioning carries shared responsibility.

For women

Some sins are conspicuous; some good works are hidden for a time. Patience lets both come to light.

Day 271 of 1,095

Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail.

For men

Secrets stay sick. Confession is where healing begins because sin dies in the light and prayer invites God's power.

For women

The wound you hide grows infected. The wound you confess begins to heal because God meets honesty with mercy and power.

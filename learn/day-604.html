Day 604 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 87Forgiveness as Warfare — Cutting the Root of Bitterness

Walk Truth

For men

Bitterness hides behind polite silence. John calls love 'walking in the truth,' which means the first step out of resentment is usually a truthful sentence you have been avoiding.

For women

The lie 'I am fine' is often bitterness in disguise. John ties love to walking in truth, and that means a woman who wants to forgive must first stop hiding behind a smile.

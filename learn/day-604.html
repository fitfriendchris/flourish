Walk Truth

Day 604 of 1,095

I was overjoyed to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as the Father has commanded us. And now I urge you, dear lady—not as a new commandment to you, but one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another. And this is love, that we walk according to His commandments. This is the very commandment you have heard from the beginning, that you must walk in love.

2 John 4-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Bitterness hides behind polite silence. John calls love 'walking in the truth,' which means the first step out of resentment is usually a truthful sentence you have been avoiding.

For women

The lie 'I am fine' is often bitterness in disguise. John ties love to walking in truth, and that means a woman who wants to forgive must first stop hiding behind a smile.

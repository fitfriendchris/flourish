Ambassadors Appealing

Day 1005 of 1,095

Therefore we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were making His appeal through us. We implore you on behalf of Christ: Be reconciled to God.

2 Corinthians 5:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

An ambassador speaks for the one who sent him. Paul says God makes His appeal through us, and the tone is begging, not lecturing.

For women

Be reconciled to God. The ambassador's message is not a list of rules; it is an invitation back into relationship.

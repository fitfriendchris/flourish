Day 618 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 89The Open Table — Hospitality as Strategy

Father Instruction

For men

A father's instruction is not a lecture. It is an invitation to live. Solomon calls his sons to listen so that they may gain insight, get wisdom, and receive the garland and crown that wisdom gives.

For women

A father's instruction is the start of wisdom, but mothers often create the atmosphere in which that instruction is received. The open table depends on a woman who prepares space for truth to be heard.

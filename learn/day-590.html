Day 590 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 85The Healing Fellowship — Confession and Restoration

Chosen Race

For men

You were not made to believe alone. Peter writes to scattered Christians and tells them they have become a people, a priesthood, and a nation. Your individual calling is real, but it is exercised inside a body.

For women

You were not made to belong to yourself alone. Peter tells scattered women that they have become a people, a priesthood, and a nation held by mercy. Your place in the body is not an afterthought; it is part of your calling.

No Air in the Measure

Day 478 of 1,095

Give, and it will be given to you. A good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over will be poured into your lap. For with the measure you use, it will be measured back to you.”

Luke 6:38 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Pressed down, shaken together, running over is what an honest grain seller does. Jesus takes a merchant's measure and makes it the standard you will be judged by.

For women

An honest seller settles the grain so no air is sold as wheat. That is the picture behind the promise.

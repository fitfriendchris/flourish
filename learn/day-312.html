Fasting as a Weapon

For men

Ezra needed protection on a dangerous road. Instead of asking the king for soldiers, he asked God through fasting. The weapon was hunger paired with humility.

For women

Fasting is not a diet with a spiritual excuse. It is a way of telling your body, your fears, and your plans that God is more necessary than any of them.

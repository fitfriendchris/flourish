Mentors, Peers, and Protégés

Day 251 of 1,095

And the things that you have heard me say among many witnesses, entrust these to faithful men who will be qualified to teach others as well.

2 Timothy 2:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul's command to Timothy is the model: receive truth, entrust it to faithful people, and keep the chain unbroken across generations.

For women

Paul's chain of truth — older to younger, teacher to faithful learner — gives every woman a three-directional map for friendship: mentor, peer, and protégé.

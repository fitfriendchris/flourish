Desire at War

Day 124 of 1,095

What causes conflicts and quarrels among you? Don’t they come from the passions at war within you? You crave what you do not have; you kill and covet, but are unable to obtain it. You quarrel and fight. You do not have, because you do not ask. And when you do ask, you do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, that you may squander it on your pleasures.

James 4:1-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

James says the conflict in your home, team, or country did not start out there. It started as desire inside someone and grew legs.

For women

The quarrel in your kitchen or group chat did not begin with the other person. It began with a desire inside you that learned to demand instead of ask.

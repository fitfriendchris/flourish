No Partiality

Day 667 of 1,095

Then Peter began to speak: “I now truly understand that God does not show favoritism, but welcomes those from every nation who fear Him and do what is right.

Acts 10:34-35 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God does not play favorites with platforms. The same Lord who accepted Cornelius assesses your feed, your boardroom, and your living room by one standard: do you fear him and do what is right?

For women

Your platform is a pulpit, and your people are watching before they ever hear a Bible verse. A woman's fear of the Lord and doing of right will preach louder than any caption.

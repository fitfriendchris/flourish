He Would Withdraw

Day 313 of 1,095

Yet He frequently withdrew to the wilderness to pray.

Luke 5:16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The busiest man in history made withdrawal a habit. Solitude was not a luxury for Jesus. It was the source of His clarity.

For women

You do not have to earn the right to rest with God. Even Jesus stepped away from good things to be with the Father.

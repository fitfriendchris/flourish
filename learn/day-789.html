Continue Learned

For men

Knowing from whom you learned it. Paul grounds Timothy's confidence partly in the people who taught him, which is a claim worth examining.

For women

From childhood you have been acquainted with the sacred writings. The word is brephos — infancy. Timothy was taught before he could possibly assess it, and Paul calls that an advantage.

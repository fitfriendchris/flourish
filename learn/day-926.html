I Will Come In

Day 926 of 1,095

Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in and dine with him, and he with Me.

Revelation 3:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Christ knocks and asks to eat with a lukewarm church. The mentor's table is finally His table, and He is the one asking to come in.

For women

If anyone hears my voice. Christ's invitation to a lukewarm church is personal, and it begins with listening.

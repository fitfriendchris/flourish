Finish the Course

Day 1035 of 1,095

But I consider my life of no value to me, if only I may finish my course and complete the ministry I have received from the Lord Jesus—the ministry of testifying to the good news of God’s grace.

Acts 20:24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The ministry was received, not chosen. Finishing the course means completing an assignment someone else gave.

For women

The ministry of testifying to the good news of God’s grace. Paul's whole course was about grace, and a woman can finish on the same note.

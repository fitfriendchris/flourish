Fear L O R D Blessed

Day 615 of 1,095

A song of ascents. Blessed are all who fear the LORD, who walk in His ways! For when you eat the fruit of your labor, blessings and prosperity will be yours. Your wife will be like a fruitful vine flourishing within your house, your sons like olive shoots sitting around your table. In this way indeed shall blessing come to the man who fears the LORD.

Psalm 128:1-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The psalm connects the fear of the Lord with a household that flourishes. A man who fears God does not treat his wife, his work, or his children as possessions. He walks in God's ways, and the result is a home that produces life.

For women

Psalm 128 describes a household that flourishes because the people in it fear the Lord and walk in his ways. A woman is part of that flourishing, and her purity contributes to the fruitfulness of the whole home.

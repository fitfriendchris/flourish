Day 660 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 95The Discipline of Endurance — Year Two Perseverance

Sabbath

For men

Year two asks: does the rhythm hold when the year has already been hard? Endurance is not a new resolve; it is the same commandment obeyed again after the resolve faded.

For women

Year two asks whether you will still stop when stopping costs more than it did the first time.

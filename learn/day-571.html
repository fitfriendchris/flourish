Two Different Words for Burden

For men

Carry one another’s burdens, and each will bear his own load — two different Greek words, three verses apart. Confusing them wrecks a marriage in both directions.

For women

watch yourself is singular, dropped into a plural sentence. The person doing the restoring is the one Paul warns.

Do Not Despise Her

Day 977 of 1,095

Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old.

Proverbs 23:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The father gave you life. That fact alone earns him a hearing, even when you are grown and disagree.

For women

When she is old. The command anticipates the season when a mother's slowness, forgetfulness, or repetition could invite contempt.

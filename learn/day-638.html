Day 638 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 91Q3 Review — Love with Calloused Hands

Love First

For men

You cannot claim to love God while withholding love from the brother you can see. The vertical always tests itself in the horizontal.

For women

A woman cannot love God in the abstract while despising the sister in the flesh. The love that first came down from heaven must now travel across the room.

Filled Knowledge

For men

The long war is not won by guessing. A soldier needs to know the Commander’s will with clarity enough to walk worthy of him.

For women

A woman who knows God’s will walks differently through the long war. Her steps are not perfect, but they are worthy of the Lord she serves.

Remember the Sabbath Day

Day 346 of 1,095

Remember the Sabbath day by keeping it holy. Six days you shall labor and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the LORD your God, on which you must not do any work—neither you, nor your son or daughter, nor your manservant or maidservant or livestock, nor the foreigner within your gates. For in six days the LORD made the heavens and the earth and the sea and all that is in them, but on the seventh day He rested. Therefore the LORD blessed the Sabbath day and set it apart as holy.

Exodus 20:8-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Sabbath is not a suggestion for the spiritually advanced; it is a command rooted in creation. God worked six days and then stopped. A man who cannot stop has forgotten who is actually running the world.

For women

A woman often carries the emotional labor of a household, and rest can feel impossible. The Sabbath command is for you too: your son, daughter, servant, and even the sojourner are included. God sees the work you do and commands the pause you need.

Remember the Sabbath Day

For men

Sabbath is not a suggestion for the spiritually advanced; it is a command rooted in creation. God worked six days and then stopped. A man who cannot stop has forgotten who is actually running the world.

For women

A woman often carries the emotional labor of a household, and rest can feel impossible. The Sabbath command is for you too: your son, daughter, servant, and even the sojourner are included. God sees the work you do and commands the pause you need.

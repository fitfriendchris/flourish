Signs, peace, and fleece tests

For men

Gideon asked God for a sign, twice. God gave it to him, twice. That does not mean you should copy him. The story is describing what happened, not prescribing what you should do every time you are unsure.

For women

You may have laid out your own fleece: if this happens, it is a sign; if that happens, it means no. Gideon did the same thing. The surprising part is not that God answered. It is what the rest of Scripture says about whether you should keep doing it.

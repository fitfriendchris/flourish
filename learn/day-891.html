Hidden in Darkness

Day 891 of 1,095

Therefore judge nothing before the appointed time; wait until the Lord comes. He will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and will expose the motives of men’s hearts. At that time each will receive his praise from God.

1 Corinthians 4:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

judge nothing before the appointed time. Paul is talking about assessing ministers, and the first person he applies it to is himself.

For women

Each will receive his praise. Epainos — praise, not a verdict, and not everyone's the same.

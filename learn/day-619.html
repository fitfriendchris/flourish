Strive Peace

For men

Striving for peace is not passive niceness. It is active pursuit. The writer of Hebrews commands us to chase peace with everyone, as well as to guard as well as holiness, without which no one will see the Lord.

For women

Striving for peace falls heavily on women because women often sense tension before it is named. A woman's discernment is meant to guard grace and uproot bitterness before it spreads.

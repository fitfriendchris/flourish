Open Doors

Day 1016 of 1,095

as you pray also for us, that God may open to us a door for the word, so that we may proclaim the mystery of Christ, for which I am in chains.

Colossians 4:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul was in chains and asked for an open door for the word, not a door out of prison.

For women

Pray also for us. Paul asked others to pray for his doors, and intercession opens doors for people far away.

According to What

Day 870 of 1,095

For if the eagerness is there, the gift is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what he does not have.

2 Corinthians 8:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Prothumia — the readiness. Paul makes eagerness the qualifying condition and then caps the amount at what she actually has.

For women

Isotes — equality. Paul expects the direction to reverse, which makes it something other than patronage.

Day 725 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 104Year Two Commencement — The Builder Becomes a Pillar

Entrust Faithful

For men

Now the builder becomes a pillar by becoming a link. The end of year two is not graduation from discipleship; it is commissioning into it.

For women

A woman becomes a pillar when she stops only receiving and begins entrusting what she has received to the next faithful person in the chain.

Honest Ledger

For men

God does not merely prefer honesty in trade. He calls a false balance an abomination — the strongest word Scripture uses for what offends Him.

For women

An accurate weight is not the absence of crime. It is the active choice to make your numbers match reality, because God is watching the match.

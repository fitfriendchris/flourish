Train Up a Child in His Way

For men

The proverb is about bending, not breaking. A child has a way — a unique shape — and the parent is called to train him along it, not against it.

For women

Your child is not raw material to be shaped into your image. He or she is a specific person, and your job is to train along the grain of who God made them.

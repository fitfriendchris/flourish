while he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness. He sat down under a broom tree and prayed that he might die. “I have had enough, LORD,” he said. “Take my life, for I am no better than my fathers.” Then he lay down under the broom tree and fell asleep. Suddenly an angel touched him and said, “Get up and eat.” And he looked around, and there by his head was a cake of bread baked over hot coals, and a jar of water. So he ate and drank and lay down again. A second time the angel of the LORD returned and touched him, saying, “Get up and eat, or the journey will be too much for you.” So he got up and ate and drank. And strengthened by that food, he walked forty days and forty nights until he reached Horeb, the mountain of God.

1 Kings 19:4-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Elijah's collapse came after his biggest win. The angel did not rebuke him; he rebuilt him with food, water, and two rounds of sleep.

For women

Elijah did not fall apart because he lacked faith. He fell apart because he was human. God's first response was not a sermon but a meal.

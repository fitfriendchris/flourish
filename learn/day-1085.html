He Will Wipe Away

Day 1085 of 1,095

‘He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,’ and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the former things have passed away.”

Revelation 21:4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The former things have passed away. A man carries losses and failures that will not follow him into the city.

For women

He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. The comfort is personal — God's own hand on each face.

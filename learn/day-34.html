Kingdom Prayer

Day 34 of 1,095

So then, this is how you should pray: ‘Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven.

Matthew 6:9-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The opening lines read as an order are hard. Read as surrender they are much harder, and that is the reading the psalm asks for.

For women

Your will be done is easy to pray in general and almost impossible to pray about one specific thing.

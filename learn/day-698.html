Possible With God

For men

The integration you cannot manufacture is possible with God. The same Lord who can save a rich man can also align your calendar, your body, your money, and your relationships.

For women

A woman often carries the impossible quietly — the child who will not turn back, the marriage that feels finished, the budget that will not stretch. Jesus looks at her and says the same thing: with God, all things are possible.

Day 644 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 92The Integrated Life — One Lord Over Every Ledger

New Heaven Earth

For men

Integration only makes sense if the future is certain. A man who knows that God will wipe every tear and end every death can steward his body, money, and relationships with hope instead of desperation.

For women

A woman often carries the tears of a household. The promise that God will wipe them away gives her courage to keep loving, keep giving, and keep hoping even when the present is heavy.

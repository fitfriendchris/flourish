Day 654 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 94Leading from the Second Chair — Faithfulness Before Title

Subject Honor

For men

Subjection is not weakness; it is a strategic act of worship. Peter tells free men to place themselves under authority for the Lord's sake, and to honor the emperor while fearing God.

For women

Honor is the atmosphere a woman creates when she decides that no title is needed before she treats another person as valuable.

Christ and the Church

For men

Marriage is not a social contract dressed in church clothes. It is a living picture of Christ and the church, and the way you love your wife or honor your husband today either preaches that gospel or contradicts it.

For women

Your marriage is the closest thing many people will ever see to the way Jesus loves his people. The way you receive your husband's love and respect his leadership matters far more than your wedding photos ever will.

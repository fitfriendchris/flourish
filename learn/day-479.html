Turn Aside From Evil

Day 479 of 1,095

How much better to acquire wisdom than gold! To gain understanding is more desirable than silver. The highway of the upright leads away from evil; he who guards his way protects his life.

Proverbs 16:16-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Solomon prices wisdom against gold on purpose. And the next line is a road: the upright highway is defined by what it turns away from.

For women

The highway of the upright is defined by what it turns away from — small corrections, repeated, over a long road.

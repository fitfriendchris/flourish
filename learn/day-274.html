The Gift of Singleness

For men

Paul calls his unmarried state a gift, not a defect. The single life is not a holding pattern; it is a particular grace from God.

For women

Paul calls singleness a gift, not a season to endure. Your unmarried years are not a gap in your story; they are a grace for a specific calling.

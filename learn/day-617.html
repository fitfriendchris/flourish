Love Your Enemies

For men

Jesus commands his people to love, bless, pray for, and give to their enemies. The standard is not reciprocity. It is mercy, because God is kind to the ungrateful and wicked.

For women

Jesus commands his people to love, bless, and pray for their enemies. For a woman, this is a call to mercy that protects her own heart from the bitterness that masquerades as justice.

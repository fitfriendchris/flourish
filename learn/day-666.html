Day 666 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 95The Discipline of Endurance — Year Two Perseverance

Draw All

For men

The endurance of year two is not endless self-improvement. It is a long look at the lifted-up Christ, who draws all people to himself through the scandal of the cross.

For women

Year two endurance does not depend on finding a new technique. It depends on looking again at Christ lifted up. His cross is the magnet that draws people — including you — back to God.

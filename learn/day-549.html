Day 549 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 79The Man in the Arena — Character Under Pressure

Said Hours Before They All Ran

For men

He called them friends on the night they would all abandon Him. The commandment to love was issued under maximum pressure, to people about to fail Him.

For women

You did not choose me, but I chose you. For anyone who has spent a life being picked or not picked, the direction is the point.

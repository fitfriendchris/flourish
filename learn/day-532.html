Haste Produces Lack

For men

The second half is the one this week needs. Haste does not merely lose money — it produces machsor, lack, and the first thing it takes is quality.

For women

'Ats does not mean fast. It means pressed — squeezed by what is urgent. And what pressure produces is machsor, lack.

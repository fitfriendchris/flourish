“They do not need to go away,” Jesus replied. “You give them something to eat.”

Matthew 14:16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus hands the problem back. The disciples wanted the crowd sent away; He told them to feed it from what they had.

For women

Only is the word of a disciple who counted before he offered. Christ asked for the offering, not the count.

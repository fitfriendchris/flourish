“Be angry, yet do not sin.” Do not let the sun set upon your anger, and do not give the devil a foothold.

Ephesians 4:26-27 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul's reason for the sunset deadline is tactical: do not give the devil an opportunity. Delay is the opportunity.

For women

The danger is not the anger. It is the gap you leave between the anger and dealing with it.

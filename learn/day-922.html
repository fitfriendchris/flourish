Come and Have Breakfast

Day 922 of 1,095

“Come, have breakfast,” Jesus said to them. None of the disciples dared to ask Him, “Who are You?” They knew it was the Lord.

John 21:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus restored a failed disciple with breakfast before He said a word about the failure.

For women

None of them dared ask, Who are you? They knew. A shared meal can say what nobody is ready to say aloud yet.

The Name Is a Strong Tower

Day 213 of 1,095

The name of the LORD is a strong tower; the righteous run to it and are safe.

Proverbs 18:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A strong tower was the place you ran to when raiders came. The righteous run to. Faith is a direction, not a feeling.

For women

The name of the Lord is not a feeling of safety; it is a tower strong enough to lift a woman above the battle. Her peace does not depend on the policy paying out.

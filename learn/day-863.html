Moses Is Dead

Day 863 of 1,095

“Moses My servant is dead. Now therefore arise, you and all these people, and cross over the Jordan into the land that I am giving to the children of Israel. … No one shall stand against you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so will I be with you; I will never leave you nor forsake you. Be strong and courageous, for you shall give these people the inheritance of the land that I swore to their fathers I would give them. … Have I not commanded you to be strong and courageous? Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”

Joshua 1:2; 1:5-6; 1:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God says Moses is dead as the premise of the sentence, not as news. Joshua had been at the funeral.

For women

Be strong and courageous, three times in nine verses, to a man who had already heard it from Moses. Scripture is not embarrassed that he was frightened.

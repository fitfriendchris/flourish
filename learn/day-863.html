Moses Is Dead

For men

God says Moses is dead as the premise of the sentence, not as news. Joshua had been at the funeral.

For women

Be strong and courageous, three times in nine verses, to a man who had already heard it from Moses. Scripture is not embarrassed that he was frightened.

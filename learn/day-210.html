Set Aside on the First Day

Day 210 of 1,095

Now about the collection for the saints, you are to do as I directed the churches of Galatia: On the first day of every week, each of you should set aside a portion of his income, saving it up, so that when I come no collections will be needed.

1 Corinthians 16:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Spontaneous generosity is good, but planned generosity is faithful. Paul wants the Corinthians to set something aside regularly so they are ready when the need arrives.

For women

A woman who plans her giving is not less spiritual. She is preparing her heart and her hands to respond when God opens a door.

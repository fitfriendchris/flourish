Paul Mediates a Reconciled Future

For men

Paul does not cancel Philemon or command him. He appeals on the basis of love, and he renames Onesimus from slave to beloved brother.

For women

Reconciliation across deep wounds needs a mediator who sees both people as God sees them: not as roles, but as beloved.

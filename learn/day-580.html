Forgive

Day 580 of 1,095

Watch yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him. Even if he sins against you seven times in a day, and seven times returns to say, ‘I repent,’ you must forgive him.”

Luke 17:3-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Today return to it as a father: forgiveness is not optional, and it must be practiced at home before it can be preached.

For women

Today return to it as a mother: your willingness to forgive, and to ask for forgiveness, shapes the spiritual atmosphere of your home.

Wisdom Is Bought

Day 543 of 1,095

How much better to acquire wisdom than gold! To gain understanding is more desirable than silver. The highway of the upright leads away from evil; he who guards his way protects his life.

Proverbs 16:16-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Get wisdom is a purchase verb. The question a review asks is what currency you paid this quarter — and the usual answer is a mistake.

For women

In Proverbs, wisdom is a woman who builds a house, sets a table and calls from the street. Getting her is what is better than gold.

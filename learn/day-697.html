No Other Gods

For men

The first commandment is the first audit: there is room for only one God on the throne. Your calendar and your bank statement will reveal whether you actually believe that.

For women

A woman's idols are often disguised as love, competence, or responsibility. The first commandment asks her to be honest about what receives her first energy, her first anxiety, and her first devotion.

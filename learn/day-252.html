Tychicus will tell you all the news about me. He is a beloved brother, a faithful minister, and a fellow servant in the Lord. … My fellow prisoner Aristarchus sends you greetings, as does Mark the cousin of Barnabas. You have already received instructions about him: If he comes to you, welcome him. Jesus, who is called Justus, also sends greetings. These are the only Jews among my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me.

Colossians 4:7; 4:10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul's closing greetings read like a relational resume: dozens of named co-laborers, each trusted with different parts of the mission.

For women

Paul's letters end with long lists of named co-laborers — a reminder that the Christian life is a network of shared mission, not a private spirituality.

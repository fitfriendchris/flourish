Seek First the Kingdom

Day 642 of 1,095

But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you.

Matthew 6:33 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Integration is impossible without priority. Jesus does not promise that every ledger will manage itself. He promises that when the kingdom comes first, the rest falls into proper order.

For women

A woman often sets the kingdom priorities of a home. The meals, the schedule, the budget, and the conversation all announce what is first in the household she stewards.

Ambassadors

Day 665 of 1,095

Therefore we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were making His appeal through us. We implore you on behalf of Christ: Be reconciled to God. God made Him who knew no sin to be sin on our behalf, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God.

2 Corinthians 5:20-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

An ambassador does not represent himself. Year two endurance means staying on mission as Christ's representative even when you feel like representing your own grievances.

For women

A woman who endures in year two remembers that she represents Christ, not herself. She carries the appeal of reconciliation into every room, even when she feels more like complaining than inviting people to peace with God.

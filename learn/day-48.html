Stand Firm

For men

Take the limit today: God sets a ceiling on what you can be handed.

For women

He will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear. But when you are tempted, He will also provide an escape, so that you can stand up under it — which means what you are facing is survivable, however it feels.

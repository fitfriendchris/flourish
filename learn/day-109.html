Sleep as Gift

Day 109 of 1,095

In vain you rise early and stay up late, toiling for bread to eat—for He gives sleep to His beloved.

Psalm 127:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The hours you shave off sleep are not buying what you think they are. Sleep is a gift to the beloved, not a wage for the diligent.

For women

The beloved in this psalm is not the one who has everything handled. It is the one God loves while she sleeps.

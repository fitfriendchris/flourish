Do Not Neglect

For men

The gift was given when the elders laid hands on Timothy. A commissioned man carries the community's recognition and must not treat it carelessly.

For women

Given by prophecy and the elders' hands. A woman's gift is often first seen by others, and their recognition is worth remembering.

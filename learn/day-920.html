No Leisure to Eat

For men

Part of discipling a man is taking him away from the work. Jesus' first move after the apostles reported back was a meal and rest, not a debrief.

For women

The rest was interrupted, and Jesus fed the crowd anyway. A crowded table is still a table where disciples learn.

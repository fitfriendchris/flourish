Honorable Pursuit Within Community

For men

Boaz noticed Ruth's character before he pursued her, and he honored Naomi, the law, and the community before he claimed a wife.

For women

Boaz honored Ruth's character, her mother-in-law, and the law before he ever asked her to belong to him. That is the kind of pursuit a woman can trust.

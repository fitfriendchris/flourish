My True Child

Day 917 of 1,095

To Timothy, my true child in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

1 Timothy 1:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A true child is a legitimate one. Paul gave Timothy, with his mixed background, a legitimacy the synagogue would have questioned.

For women

Grace, mercy, and peace — mercy added only for Timothy. A good mother adjusts her blessing to the child.

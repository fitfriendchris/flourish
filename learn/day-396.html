Living Sacrifice

For men

Paul puts the body first and the mind second. Most thought-warfare advice reverses that order and then wonders why it keeps failing.

For women

A living sacrifice has one obvious problem, and Paul knew it. It can get down off the altar. That is the arrangement, not the failure.

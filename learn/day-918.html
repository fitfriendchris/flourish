Where You Go

For men

Ruth attached herself to a bitter woman. Sometimes a younger believer chooses you as family at the moment you are least fit to lead.

For women

Your God will be my God. Ruth did not adopt Naomi's theology first. She adopted Naomi's people, and the God came with them.

He who walks with the wise will become wise, but the companion of fools will be destroyed.

Proverbs 13:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The men you walk with will either sharpen you or dull you; choose your circle like you choose your weapons.

For women

The women beside you are either calling out your best self or training you to shrink; friendship is a forming power, not a neutral accessory.

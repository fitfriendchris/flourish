Until I Come

Day 838 of 1,095

Beforehand, he called ten of his servants and gave them ten minas. ‘Conduct business with this until I return,’ he said. … Then another servant came and said, ‘Master, here is your mina, which I have laid away in a piece of cloth. For I was afraid of you, because you are a harsh man. You withdraw what you did not deposit and reap what you did not sow.’

Luke 19:13; 19:20-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Ten servants, one mina each — identical capital. The talents parable gave different amounts. Luke is asking a different question and it is not about capacity.

For women

His theory of the master was wrong and his logic from it was sound. That is how a wrong picture of God produces caution rather than rebellion.

Day 661 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 95The Discipline of Endurance — Year Two Perseverance

Patient Farmer

For men

Year two perseverance is mostly waiting. The farmer does not dig up the seed to check progress. He waits for the early and late rains because the crop has its own calendar.

For women

Year two is the long stretch between the early rain of resolve and the late rain of harvest. A woman's endurance is shown by how she treats the people around her while she waits.

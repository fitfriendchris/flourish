Peace Gift

Day 736 of 1,095

But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, will teach you all things and will remind you of everything I have told you. Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled; do not be afraid.

John 14:26-27 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Helper is not a substitute for Jesus. He is the presence of Jesus, sent to teach, remind, and give the peace that does not depend on circumstances. The Helper teaches, reminds, and gives the peace of Christ.

For women

The Helper teaches what you need for the moment and reminds you of what Jesus has already said. Peace is the gift he carries with him. Carry the Helper's peace into the rooms that need it most.

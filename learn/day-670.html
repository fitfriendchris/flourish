Speed Ahead

For men

The best use of your platform is to speed the word of the Lord ahead. Pray for it, speak it, live it, and protect it from the evil that wants to slow it down.

For women

A woman's prayers, conversations, and ordinary courage can make the word of the Lord run faster in her home, her neighborhood, and her network than any campaign could.

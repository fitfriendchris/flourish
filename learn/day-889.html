Who Cannot Repay

For men

Otherwise, they may invite you in return, and you will be repaid. Jesus names ordinary hospitality as a transaction that settles, and settling is the problem.

For women

You will be repaid at the resurrection of the righteous. He names the settlement date, and it is not in this life.

A City on a Hill

Day 987 of 1,095

You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its savor, how can it be made salty again? It is no longer good for anything, except to be thrown out and trampled by men. You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden.

Matthew 5:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Salt that loses its taste has become foolish. The Greek word for tasteless is the word for a fool.

For women

A city on a hill is plural people in one place. The light Jesus describes is a community, not a lone lamp.

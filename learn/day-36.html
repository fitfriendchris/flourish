Fasting Discipline

Day 36 of 1,095

When you fast, do not be somber like the hypocrites, for they disfigure their faces to show men they are fasting. Truly I tell you, they already have their full reward. But when you fast, anoint your head and wash your face,

Matthew 6:16-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus says when you fast, not if. He assumes a practice most of us have quietly retired.

For women

Jesus assumed His followers would fast. He spent His instruction on how to hide it, not whether to do it.

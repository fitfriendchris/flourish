Shema Parenting: Teach at Every Moment

For men

The shema is not a classroom lesson. It is a whole-life environment. God expects parents to teach while sitting, walking, lying down, and rising up.

For women

Your children will not love God because you told them to. They will love Him because they saw Him loved in the ordinary hours of your home.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

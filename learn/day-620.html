Day 620 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 89The Open Table — Hospitality as Strategy

Sun and Rain

For men

Jesus does not soften the law. He intensifies it. The old saying allowed love for neighbors and hate for enemies. Jesus commands love for enemies and prayer for persecutors, because that is how the children of the Father live.

For women

Jesus commands love for enemies, and women often face enemies in hidden forms: the critic, the excluder, the one who undermines quietly. The command lands with the same force in those smaller wars.

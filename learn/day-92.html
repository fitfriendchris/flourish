Food Is Gift

Day 92 of 1,095

For every creation of God is good, and nothing that is received with thanksgiving should be rejected, because it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.

1 Timothy 4:4-5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Your body does not need another enemy, and the plate in front of you is not the problem. The lie is older than the diet industry: that some foods are clean and others are dirty, and that your worth is measured by what you refused today.

For women

The bread on your table is not guilty. The whispers that call some foods bad and your hunger shameful are the ones that need to be silenced, because Paul says everything God created is good when that is received with thanks.

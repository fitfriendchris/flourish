Moral, wisdom, preference

Day 80 of 1,095

“Everything is permissible,” but not everything is beneficial. “Everything is permissible,” but not everything is edifying. No one should seek his own good, but the good of others.

1 Corinthians 10:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Freedom in Christ is real, but it is not the same as doing whatever you want. Some choices are right or wrong. Some are wise or foolish. Some are simply preference. Learning the difference is the work of maturity.

For women

You probably know what you are technically allowed to do. The harder question is whether your choice helps anyone besides you, and whether it builds up the people who will live with the consequences.

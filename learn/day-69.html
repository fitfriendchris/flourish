Cognitive Defense

Day 69 of 1,095

See to it that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deception, which are based on human tradition and the spiritual forces of the world rather than on Christ.

Colossians 2:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul names the standard at the end: not according to Christ. Defence is not suspicion — it is having a measure.

For women

You do not spot a counterfeit by studying counterfeits. You spot it by knowing the real thing.

Love Not World

Day 385 of 1,095

Do not love the world or anything in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in him. For all that is in the world—the desires of the flesh, the desires of the eyes, and the pride of life—is not from the Father but from the world. The world is passing away, along with its desires; but whoever does the will of God remains forever.

1 John 2:15-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

John is not talking about the planet. The word is the arrangement — a system of wanting, with its own logic and its own promises.

For women

Two of the three are named as temptations. The third gets called ambition and goes unexamined for years.

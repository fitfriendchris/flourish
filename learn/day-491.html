Know the Condition of the Flock

Day 491 of 1,095

Be sure to know the state of your flocks, and pay close attention to your herds; for riches are not forever, nor does a crown endure to every generation.

Proverbs 27:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Riches vanish, says Solomon — and then he describes a flock that renews itself. The distinction is between a pile and a productive asset, and it turns on knowing what you own.

For women

A crown is impressive and static. A flock renews — and the whole promise depends on someone knowing its condition.

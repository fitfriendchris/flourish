Practicing Contentment

Day 147 of 1,095

Keep your lives free from the love of money and be content with what you have, for God has said: “Never will I leave you, never will I forsake you.”

Hebrews 13:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Contentment is a command, not a feeling, and the reason for it is not your circumstances but the promise that God leave you.

For women

Contentment is not something you feel; it is something you practice because God has promised He leave you.

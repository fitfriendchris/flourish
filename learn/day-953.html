Fathers Tell Children

For men

Hezekiah vowed to tell his children of God's faithfulness. His son became Judah's worst king. A promise to tell must become a practice.

For women

The living, only the living. Hezekiah's thanks came from someone who had nearly died, and testimony is strongest when it is specific.

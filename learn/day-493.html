The Asset Nobody Can Seize

For men

Markets fall, currencies weaken, employers close. The one holding that cannot be confiscated is a skill — but only while it is still being maintained.

For women

A skill cannot be seized, does not fall with the market, and travels in your head — but it decays if it is not tended.

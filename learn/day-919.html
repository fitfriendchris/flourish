Until Christ Is Formed

Day 919 of 1,095

My children, for whom I am again in the pains of childbirth until Christ is formed in you,

Galatians 4:19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul said he was in labour again. Discipleship sometimes has to begin a second time with the same person.

For women

Until Christ is formed in you — not until you look like me. A spiritual mother's goal is a likeness that is not her own.

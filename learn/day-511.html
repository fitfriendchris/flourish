Day 511 of 1,095

The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is slave to the lender.

Proverbs 22:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The first half of this proverb is not an endorsement. Solomon is describing how power actually flows, and Scripture's remedies for it are aimed at the lender.

For women

The verse sits inside a chapter of commands about the poor — and the slavery it describes is often domestic long before it is ever a bank.

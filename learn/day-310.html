So then, this is how you should pray: ‘Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.’

Matthew 6:9-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Lord's Prayer is not a chant. It is a skeleton for every prayer you will ever pray. It gives you a structure that keeps your prayers honest, humble, and aligned with what actually matters.

For women

The Lord's Prayer is not poetry to recite. It is a pattern that teaches your heart what to want first.

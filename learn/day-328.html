2 Samuel 12:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Nathan confronted the most powerful man in Israel with a story, not a sword, because truth told well disarms the defenses that authority builds around itself.

For women

The bravest words are often told as a story first, so the heart can hear what the pride would otherwise refuse.

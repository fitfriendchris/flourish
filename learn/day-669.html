Take Up Your Cross

Day 669 of 1,095

Then Jesus told His disciples, “If anyone wants to come after Me, he must deny himself and take up his cross and follow Me. For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake will find it. What will it profit a man if he gains the whole world, yet forfeits his soul? Or what can a man give in exchange for his soul?

Matthew 16:24-26 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A platform that costs you your soul is a bad bargain. Jesus calls you to lose your life for his sake, and in that losing you find the only influence that outlives you.

For women

A woman's influence is most powerful when she is willing to lose it for Christ. The world teaches you to protect your platform; Jesus teaches you to lay it down and find it again in him.

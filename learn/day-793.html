Sacrifice Praise

Day 793 of 1,095

Through Jesus, therefore, let us continually offer to God a sacrifice of praise, the fruit of lips that confess His name. And do not neglect to do good and to share with others, for with such sacrifices God is pleased.

Hebrews 13:15-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Two sacrifices are named in two verses, and the second one is doing good and sharing what you have. The writer puts them in the same category.

For women

The thanksgiving offering in Leviticus had to be eaten the same day. Which meant you could not finish it alone, and had to invite people to help.

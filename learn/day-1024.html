Let Us Test Our Ways

Day 1024 of 1,095

Let us examine and test our ways, and turn back to the LORD.

Lamentations 3:40 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Let us test our ways. The audit is plural, and a family or church that reviews together returns to God together.

For women

The audit sits in a chapter that says His mercies are new every morning. Reviewing her ways happens inside that mercy.

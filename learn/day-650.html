Divine Power

For men

Calling is not a request for God to give you power. It is a response to the power He has already granted. His divine power has already given you everything you need for life and godliness, which means you already have what you need for the next step.

For women

A woman does not need more power. She needs to believe the power she already has in Christ. His promises are the bridge from the life she is living to the life she is called to live.

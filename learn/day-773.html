Peace With God

For men

Peace with God and the peace of God are two different things, and a man who measures the first by the second will conclude he has lost what he cannot lose.

For women

Access is a court word — being formally presented by somebody with standing. You did not slip in, and nobody who is brought in that way worries about whether they belong.

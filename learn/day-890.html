A Cup of Water

Day 890 of 1,095

Indeed, if anyone gives you even a cup of water because you bear the name of Christ, truly I tell you, he will never lose his reward.

Mark 9:41 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A cup of water is the smallest unit of help a person can give, and the promise attached to it is the most emphatic negation Greek can build.

For women

Because you bear the name of Christ. The reason named is the receiver's identity, not the receiver's need.

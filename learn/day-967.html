Comfort and Forgive

Day 967 of 1,095

So instead, you ought to forgive and comfort him, so that he will not be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. Therefore I urge you to reaffirm your love for him.

2 Corinthians 2:7-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The goal of church discipline is restoration, and Paul asked for a formal reaffirmation of love once the man had repented.

For women

Overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. The word means swallowed up, and a repentant person can drown in shame if no one reaches in.

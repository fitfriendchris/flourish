Day 192 of 1,095

For lack of guidance, a nation falls, but with many counselors comes deliverance.

Proverbs 11:14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A single hot tip can wreck a portfolio. A network of wise advisors, combined with simple diversified vehicles, is the modern application of Solomon's warning.

For women

A woman is often the one who gathers counsel for the family: the doctor, the teacher, the therapist, the pastor. Solomon says that gathering is not weakness; it is the very thing that keeps a people from falling.

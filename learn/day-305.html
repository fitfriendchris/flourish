Discipline Is Love

Day 305 of 1,095

He who spares the rod hates his son, but he who loves him disciplines him diligently.

Proverbs 13:24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The parent who refuses to discipline is not being kind. He is being cruel. Loving discipline gives the child rest and gives the parent peace.

For women

A mother who disciplines well is not being harsh. She is being faithful. Children who learn self-control early become adults who can govern themselves.

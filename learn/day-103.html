Training partners

For men

The lone-wolf fitness story is a lie. Strong men do not train alone forever; they train with someone who knows when they are slacking, when they are hurt, and when they are about to quit.

For women

Women are often better at relationships than men, but that can hide a different loneliness - the kind where you are surrounded by people but no one knows the real struggle behind your goals.

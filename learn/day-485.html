Great Gain Is a Business Word

For men

Paul calls contentment great gain using a commercial term for profit-making, then describes the want to be rich as a trap — which is why contentment is an investing skill.

For women

A snare works because the animal never sees it. Paul's warning is not about being rich — it is about desiring to be.

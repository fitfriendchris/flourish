Index Theology

Day 191 of 1,095

For God is not a God of disorder, but of peace—as in all the churches of the saints.

1 Corinthians 14:33 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul writes this to a church whose worship had become chaotic. The stock market can feel the same way unless you choose vehicles designed for order, simplicity, and peace.

For women

A woman often carries the mental load of the household, and a chaotic portfolio adds to that load. An index-based strategy is a way of removing decision fatigue so you can focus on people instead of tickers.

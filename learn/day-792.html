Live By Faith

Day 792 of 1,095

Now it is clear that no one is justified before God by the law, because, “The righteous will live by faith.”

Galatians 3:11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul is quoting Habakkuk, and the sentence was first spoken to a man who had just been told the Babylonians were coming and had not been told why.

For women

The Hebrew is emunah, which means steadiness and faithfulness as readily as it means belief. Both readings are ancient and they ask different things of you.

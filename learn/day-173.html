De-escalation Before Persuasion

Day 173 of 1,095

When Abigail saw David, she quickly got off the donkey, fell facedown, and bowed before him. She fell at his feet and said, “My lord, may the blame be on me alone, but please let your servant speak to you; hear the words of your servant.

1 Samuel 25:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Abigail did not argue with David in his rage. She absorbed the offense, honored his position, and gave him a reason to turn back.

For women

Abigail met an army with bread, humility, and vision. Her persuasion saved lives because she lowered the temperature first.

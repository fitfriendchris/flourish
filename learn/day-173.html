De-escalation Before Persuasion

For men

Abigail did not argue with David in his rage. She absorbed the offense, honored his position, and gave him a reason to turn back.

For women

Abigail met an army with bread, humility, and vision. Her persuasion saved lives because she lowered the temperature first.

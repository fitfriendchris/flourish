Salt and Light

Day 730 of 1,095

You are the salt of the earth. But if the salt loses its savor, how can it be made salty again? It is no longer good for anything, except to be thrown out and trampled by men. You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden. Neither do people light a lamp and put it under a basket. Instead, they set it on a stand, and it gives light to everyone in the house. In the same way, let your light shine before men, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Year two ends where Jesus puts his people: in the world as salt and light. The builder becomes a pillar by becoming visible, flavorful, and luminous for the glory of the Father as year three begins.

For women

Year two ends with the same commission: you are the salt of the earth and the light of the world. A woman becomes a pillar when she stops hiding her faith and lets her ordinary life glorify the Father as year three begins.

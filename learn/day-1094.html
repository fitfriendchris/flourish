Now to Him Who Is Able

Day 1094 of 1,095

Now to Him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you unblemished in His glorious presence, with great joy— to the only God our Savior be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority through Jesus Christ our Lord before all time, and now, and for all eternity. Amen.

Jude 24-25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

To the only God be glory before all time, now, and forever. The curriculum ends by giving the glory where it belongs.

For women

With great joy. God presents His people blameless, and the joy at that presentation is His as much as theirs.

Commit Way

Day 434 of 1,095

Commit your way to the LORD; trust in Him, and He will do it. He will bring forth your righteousness like the dawn, your justice like the noonday sun.

Psalm 37:5-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Commit is literally roll. The same verb is used of rolling a stone off the mouth of a well — a weight moved by putting your shoulder into it once.

For women

You can hand something over and still stand over it, checking. That is picking it up again in a different posture.

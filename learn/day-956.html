Abigail Met Him

Day 956 of 1,095

Then David said to Abigail, “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who sent you to meet me this day! Blessed is your discernment, and blessed are you, because today you kept me from bloodshed and from avenging myself by my own hand.

1 Samuel 25:32-33 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man stopped from a sinful decision by a wise mediator should say thank you. David blessed the woman who kept him from bloodguilt.

For women

On me alone be the guilt. Abigail's mediation began by stepping between the angry man and the fool and taking the weight herself.

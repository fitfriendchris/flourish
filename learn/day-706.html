Worker Approved

Day 706 of 1,095

Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman who accurately handles the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The quiet victory now is not knowing it better but doing it better — presenting yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman in the unseen moments.

For women

A woman often works hardest where no one grades her. The quiet victory is doing that hidden work as if presenting it to God, because that is exactly what she is doing.

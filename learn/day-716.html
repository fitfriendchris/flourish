Rejoice Glad

Day 716 of 1,095

This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Rejoicing in the day the LORD made is not denial; it is the discipline that turns a man's private growth into a public blessing for everyone around him.

For women

A woman who can be glad in an ordinary, hard day gives the people around her a stable place to stand, because her joy is anchored in the LORD, not in circumstances.

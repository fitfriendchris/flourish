“Everything is permissible for me,” but not everything is beneficial. “Everything is permissible for me,” but I will not be mastered by anything.

1 Corinthians 6:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul makes a pun the English loses: all things are within my authority, but I will not be brought under the authority of anything.

For women

The test is not is this allowed. It is what has authority here — and that one is answerable honestly.

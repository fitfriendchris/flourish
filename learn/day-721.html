Reap Sow

For men

Whatever a man sows, he will reap; year two is the season when the seeds of obedience begin to show, and a faithful harvest becomes the supply he uses to bless others.

For women

A woman who sows to please the Spirit, from the Spirit in the long middle of year two will reap a harvest that feeds not only her soul but also the people God has placed around her.

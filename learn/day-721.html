Reap Sow

Day 721 of 1,095

Do not be deceived: God is not to be mocked. Whatever a man sows, he will reap in return. The one who sows to please his flesh, from the flesh will reap destruction; but the one who sows to please the Spirit, from the Spirit will reap eternal life. Let us not grow weary in well-doing, for in due time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.

Galatians 6:7-9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Whatever a man sows, he will reap; year two is the season when the seeds of obedience begin to show, and a faithful harvest becomes the supply he uses to bless others.

For women

A woman who sows to please the Spirit, from the Spirit in the long middle of year two will reap a harvest that feeds not only her soul but also the people God has placed around her.

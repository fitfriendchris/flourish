Comparison Thief

For men

Comparison is not a competitive sport in Paul's world; it is a category error. Measuring yourself by someone else's tape means you are already without understanding.

For women

The woman who compares her home, her body, her marriage, or her children to another woman's life is using a ruler God never handed her.

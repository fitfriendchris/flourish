Cover a Multitude

Day 965 of 1,095

My brothers, if one of you should wander from the truth and someone should bring him back, consider this: Whoever turns a sinner from the error of his way will save his soul from death and cover over a multitude of sins.

James 5:19-20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The wanderer in James is one of us. Restoration begins inside the church, with the brother who drifted from the truth.

For women

Let him know. James tells the restorer the stakes, because the work is easier to begin when you know it saves a life.

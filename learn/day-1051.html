Sell and Give

Day 1051 of 1,095

Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Provide yourselves with purses that will not wear out, an inexhaustible treasure in heaven, where no thief approaches and no moth destroys.

Luke 12:33 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Sell and give follows fear not. Radical generosity grows from knowing the kingdom has already been given.

For women

Little flock. Jesus spoke tenderly to a small, vulnerable group before asking them to give generously.

The Field and the Treasure

For men

Jesus says the kingdom is worth selling everything to obtain. Applied to investing, that single-minded focus is called concentration. The question is whether your field is actually treasure, or just the only thing you have ever looked at.

For women

A woman is often told that safety means keeping her options open and her money spread out. The hidden treasure says that real security sometimes looks like a bold, informed commitment to one thing you have truly seen.

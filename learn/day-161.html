“Is it a time for you yourselves to live in your paneled houses, while this house lies in ruins?” Now this is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Consider carefully your ways. You have planted much but harvested little. You eat but never have enough. You drink but never have your fill. You put on clothes but never get warm. You earn wages to put into a bag pierced through.”

Haggai 1:4-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You can build the side income and still lose everything if God is not first in the budget. Haggai says the reason money disappears is not the economy; it is misplaced priorities.

For women

A woman can decorate her house, grow her income, and still feel empty. Haggai says the emptiness is not a mystery; it is the consequence of putting your own comfort ahead of God's house.

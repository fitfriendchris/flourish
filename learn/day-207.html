Bring the Full Tithe

For men

God does not ask for a tip or a bonus. He asks for the portion that declares the rest belongs to him, and he invites you to test whether he will keep his promise.

For women

The tithe is not a tax on your income or a payment for God’s favor. It is a declaration that your income is not finally yours, and that the One who provided it can be trusted with the first portion.

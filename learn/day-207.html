Bring the Full Tithe

Day 207 of 1,095

Will a man rob God? Yet you are robbing Me! But you ask, ‘How do we rob You?’ In tithes and offerings. You are cursed with a curse, yet you—the whole nation—are still robbing Me. Bring the full tithe into the storehouse, so that there may be food in My house. Test Me in this,” says the LORD of Hosts. “See if I will not open the windows of heaven and pour out for you blessing without measure.

Malachi 3:8-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God does not ask for a tip or a bonus. He asks for the portion that declares the rest belongs to him, and he invites you to test whether he will keep his promise.

For women

The tithe is not a tax on your income or a payment for God’s favor. It is a declaration that your income is not finally yours, and that the One who provided it can be trusted with the first portion.

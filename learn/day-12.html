Helmet of Salvation

Day 12 of 1,095

Put on the full armor of God, so that you can make your stand against the devil’s schemes.

Ephesians 6:11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The helmet guards the place where you decide what is true. Attack it and the rest of the armour becomes irrelevant.

For women

If the enemy can change what you believe about your salvation, he does not need to touch anything else.

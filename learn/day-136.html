Surety Trap

For men

Cosigning is not kindness. It is a trap that uses your word as collateral. Proverbs 6 does not say 'be careful.' It says run.

For women

Women are often asked to sign for love: a boyfriend's car, a son's apartment, a sister's loan. Proverbs 6 calls that a snare, not a favor, and the escape plan is the same for her as for any man.

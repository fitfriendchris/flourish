Family-Involved Courtship

For men

Isaac did not find Rebekah by accident. His father sent a servant with a covenant oath, clear criteria, and a prayer, because marriage begins in spiritual discernment long before attraction.

For women

Rebekah was chosen because of character shown in ordinary service, and she entered marriage through family, prayer, and covenant—not through private infatuation.

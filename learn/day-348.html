Number Our Days

For men

Wisdom does not come from pretending you have unlimited time. It comes from numbering your days. A man who knows his days are limited stops wasting them on trivial things.

For women

A woman's heart often carries the weight of everyone else's days. Psalm 90:12 invites you to number your own days first, not to add anxiety but to add wisdom to every yes and no you give.

Peace That Is Kept

Day 419 of 1,095

You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind, because he trusts in You.

Isaiah 26:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Keep is a guarding word. The peace is not something you hold on to — it is something posted around you.

For women

You are being kept. That is a different sentence from you are keeping it together.

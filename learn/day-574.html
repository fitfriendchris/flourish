The Only One That Does Not Expire

Day 574 of 1,095

And now these three remain: faith, hope, and love; but the greatest of these is love.

1 Corinthians 13:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Love is greatest because the other two are temporary by definition. Faith becomes sight and hope becomes possession; love needs no deficiency to work.

For women

Now I know in part; then I shall know fully, even as I have been fully known. The knowing of you is already complete.

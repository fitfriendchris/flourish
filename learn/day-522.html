Kind to the Ungrateful

For men

The engine is three verses earlier. Give like this, Jesus says, and you will be sons of the Most High — for He is kind to the ungrateful and the evil.

For women

The same sermon that promises a lap full of grain has already said: woe to you who are rich, for you have received your consolation.

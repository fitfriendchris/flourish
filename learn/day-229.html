Trust the Next Generation

For men

David's last charge to Solomon began with God, not with gold. A legacy that skips faith is just a transaction waiting to divide the family.

For women

A mother or grandmother often worries whether the next generation will carry the faith. David's charge is the answer: tell them who God is before you tell them what they will receive.

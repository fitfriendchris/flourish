Trust the Next Generation

Day 229 of 1,095

As for you, Solomon my son, know the God of your father and serve Him wholeheartedly and with a willing mind, for the LORD searches every heart and understands the intent of every thought. If you seek Him, He will be found by you; but if you forsake Him, He will reject you forever. Consider now that the LORD has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary. Be strong and do it.”

1 Chronicles 28:9-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

David's last charge to Solomon began with God, not with gold. A legacy that skips faith is just a transaction waiting to divide the family.

For women

A mother or grandmother often worries whether the next generation will carry the faith. David's charge is the answer: tell them who God is before you tell them what they will receive.

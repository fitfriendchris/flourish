Fountain and Cistern

Day 299 of 1,095

Drink water from your own cistern, and running water from your own well. Why should your springs flow in the streets, your streams of water in the public squares? Let them be yours alone, never to be shared with strangers. May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth: A loving doe, a graceful fawn—may her breasts satisfy you always; may you be captivated by her love forever.

Proverbs 5:15-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Proverbs compares sexual faithfulness to drinking from your own cistern. The alternative is letting your springs run in the streets, wasting what was meant to satisfy you at home.

For women

Rejoicing in the wife of your youth is not nostalgia for the honeymoon phase. It is a command to keep choosing, delighting in, and returning to the one you promised yourself to for life.

Seek the Welfare

Day 984 of 1,095

Seek the prosperity of the city to which I have sent you as exiles. Pray to the LORD on its behalf, for if it prospers, you too will prosper.”

Jeremiah 29:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Seek the peace of the city you did not choose. God told the exiles that their welfare was bound up with Babylon's.

For women

Plant gardens and eat their produce. The exiles' mission began with ordinary, rooted life.

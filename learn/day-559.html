Bless Means Speak Well

For men

Reviling is abusive speech; blessing is speaking well. Peter sets an exchange rate — they speak badly of you, you speak well of them.

For women

Seek peace and pursue it — dioko, the hunting verb. Peace is chased down, not waited for.

When the Budget Fails

Day 133 of 1,095

And my God will supply all your needs according to His glorious riches in Christ Jesus.

Philippians 4:19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The budget is a tool; it is not God. When it fails, the promise still stands, but the promise is not a blank check. God supplies, and he often supplies through the hands he has already placed around you.

For women

A failed budget can feel like a failed faith, but it is not. The same God who called you to steward money also calls you to come back when the plan breaks. His supply is not limited to your spreadsheet.

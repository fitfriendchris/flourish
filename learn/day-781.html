Prepare Minds

For men

Prepare your minds for action is literally gird up the loins of your mind — tucking a long robe into a belt so you can move. It is done before you need to.

For women

There is only one imperative in the sentence. Gird and be sober are participles; the command is set your hope fully, and the rest tells you how.

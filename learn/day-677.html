Finishing What You Started

Day 677 of 1,095

being confident of this, that He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.

Philippians 1:6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The title is wrong on purpose. You are not finishing what you started; God is finishing what he started, and the completion date is the day of Christ Jesus.

For women

A woman often carries the private ledger of unfinished things: habits that stalled, relationships she meant to mend, dreams she set down. The good news is that completion is not her job description; it is God's promise.

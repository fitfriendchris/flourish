He Rested

Day 1060 of 1,095

And by the seventh day God had finished the work He had been doing; so on that day He rested from all His work. Then God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it, because on that day He rested from all the work of creation that He had accomplished.

Genesis 2:2-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The first thing God made holy was not a place or a person. It was a day. Rest is sacred time before it is anything else.

For women

The seventh day has no evening and morning. God's rest was left open, an invitation rather than a closed door.

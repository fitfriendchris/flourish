Jesus Rose to Pray

Day 393 of 1,095

Early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up and went out to a solitary place to pray.

Mark 1:35 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The word for desolate place is the same word Mark used for the wilderness the Spirit drove him into to be tested. He went back on purpose.

For women

An eremos is a place with nothing in it. That is not the drawback of the location. It is the whole specification.

The Righteous Rise Again

For men

Righteousness is not the absence of falling; it is the habit of rising. The long road back is not a straight line; it is a series of falls followed by deliberate getting up.

For women

A woman often feels that one fall undoes everything. The proverb says the opposite: the righteous fall seven times, and they rise again. Her rising is what makes her righteous, not her perfect record.

Day 686 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 98The Thankful Builder — Gratitude in the Grind

Wings Like Eagles

For men

Waiting on the Lord is not passive. It is the strength-training of the soul, and it produces endurance for the long build.

For women

A woman often waits in hidden ways — for a child, a husband, a healing, a dream. God promises that waiting itself becomes the place where strength is renewed.

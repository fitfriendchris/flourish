Who May Dwell

For men

Integrity is not a single act. It is a life shaped enough to dwell with God. It is the slow accumulation of small honest choices in every hidden room of life.

For women

The woman who may dwell with God is not perfect. She is whole — the same in private as she hopes to be in public.

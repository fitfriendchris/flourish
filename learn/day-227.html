Who Made Me a Divider?

For men

Jesus would not be the judge in a family money fight. His refusal shows that the right question is not who gets what, but who the heirs are becoming.

For women

A woman caught in a family inheritance fight is often expected to keep peace while sacrificing fairness. Jesus says the first issue is not the account balance. It is the heart.

He Gave Them a Plan

For men

Pharaoh asked for a dream interpretation. Joseph gave him the interpretation and then, unasked, a fully specified operating plan with a rate, a structure and a purpose.

For women

He named his second son Fruitful and called Egypt the land of my affliction in the same breath — while running the country.

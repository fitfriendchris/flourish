My Child Onesimus

Day 915 of 1,095

I appeal to you for my child Onesimus, whose father I became while I was in chains. Formerly he was useless to you, but now he has become useful both to you and to me.

Philemon 10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Useless became useful. A spiritual father speaks about a younger man's future while his past is still on the record.

For women

Whose father I became while I was in chains. Paul's most fruitful spiritual parenting happened in chains.

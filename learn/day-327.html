Exhort Every Day

For men

Sin hardens slowly, which is why exhortation cannot wait for a convenient tomorrow. Tell one brother this week what you have been carrying alone — let the wound of a friend become the door to healing.

For women

The kindest thing a friend can do is speak the truth while your heart is still able to bend. Bring one wound to the light this week — not as a confession record, but as proof you trust the cross to cover it.

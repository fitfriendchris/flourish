Be Still

Day 37 of 1,095

“Be still and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted over the earth.”

Psalm 46:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Be still is not a mood. In the psalm it is a command shouted over a battlefield.

For women

The stillness God asks for is not calm. It is stopping, and letting go of what is in your hands.

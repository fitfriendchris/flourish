Day 1063 of 1,095Year 3 · Week 152Sabbath of the Third Year — Rest as Testimony

Call It a Delight

For men

Not going your own way, not seeking your own pleasure. Sabbath delight comes from turning away from self-direction for a day.

For women

Or talking idly. Sabbath rest includes a rest of the tongue from ordinary chatter, worry, and business.

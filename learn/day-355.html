Blessed to Be a Blessing

Day 355 of 1,095

I will bless those who bless you and curse those who curse you; and all the families of the earth will be blessed through you.”

Genesis 12:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Abraham's legacy was not a family photo album. It was a covenant promise that would reach every family on earth.

For women

A woman inherits Abraham's promise too. Her legacy grows when her life becomes a channel of blessing to families outside her own.

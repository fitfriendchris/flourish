Love Manifest

For men

John does not define love in the abstract. He points to the sending of the Son. is how God’s love was revealed among us, and because God so loved us, we ought to love one another.

For women

John's declaration that love was made manifest lands especially on women, because women are often the ones through whom love becomes tangible in a household. A woman's welcome is a small manifestation of God's great welcome.

Boast Cross

Day 727 of 1,095

But as for me, may I never boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything. What counts is a new creation. Peace and mercy to all who walk by this rule, even to the Israel of God.

Galatians 6:14-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The builder becomes a pillar when his only boast is the cross. Circumstances, achievements, and identities lose their weight, and a new creation becomes the only credential that matters.

For women

A woman is bombarded with reasons to boast: appearance, children, home, success, spirituality. Year two's commencement call is to find her only boast in the cross, where the world is crucified to her and she to the world.

Never Satisfied

Day 910 of 1,095

He who loves money is never satisfied by money, and he who loves wealth is never satisfied by income. This too is futile.

Ecclesiastes 5:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Hevel means breath. Wealth loved for itself is not evil so much as insubstantial — there is nothing there to hold.

For women

When goods increase, so do those who eat them. A bigger income rarely stays with the person who earned it.

Day 72 of 1,095

Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11:28 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus does not say put the load down first and then come. He says come while you are still carrying it.

For women

The invitation is not for the sorted version of you. It is for the heavy laden one.

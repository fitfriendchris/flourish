The First-Fruits Line

For men

Leftovers say, “If there is anything left, you can have it.” Firstfruits say, “You are the source of everything I have.” The line in your budget reveals the order.

For women

The woman who gives first is not more wealthy; she is more free. She has placed God at the top of her budget, and that order reshapes every other dollar.

Comfort Is Not Consumed

Day 575 of 1,095

Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of compassion and the God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our troubles, so that we can comfort those in any trouble with the comfort we ourselves have received from God.

2 Corinthians 1:3-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paraklesis appears ten times in five verses, and the structure is a chain: comforted, so that you may comfort. The affliction is the qualification.

For women

We despaired of life itself. Paul says it plainly, and says what it was for — to stop him relying on himself.

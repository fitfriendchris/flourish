Moved to the Loss Column

Day 471 of 1,095

But whatever was gain to me I count as loss for the sake of Christ. More than that, I count all things as loss compared to the surpassing excellence of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord, for whom I have lost all things. I consider them rubbish, that I may gain Christ

Philippians 3:7-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul writes his own résumé and then moves the whole thing into the loss column. That entry is the first line in any kingdom venture's books.

For women

Paul lists his credentials — real ones — and then writes the whole list off. What you are willing to lose is the first entry in any venture.

Day 991 of 1,095

You are to imitate me, just as I imitate Christ.

1 Corinthians 11:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Imitate me as I imitate Christ. The invitation is conditional, and a leader must be willing to be copied only where he is copying Christ.

For women

Imitation is learning by watching. A woman teaches more by what younger women see than by what they hear.

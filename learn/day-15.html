Day 15 of 1,095

We demolish arguments and every presumption set up against the knowledge of God; and we take captive every thought to make it obedient to Christ.

2 Corinthians 10:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You cannot stop a thought arriving. You can decide whether it gets to sit down.

For women

A thought is a visitor. You are not responsible for who knocks — only for who you let stay.

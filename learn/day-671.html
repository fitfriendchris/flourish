Fear God and Keep

For men

The whole duty of man is not to build a platform; it is to fear God and keep his commandments. Every deed — public and secret — will be judged. Live accordingly.

For women

A woman's whole duty is not to be perfect, admired, or influential. It is to fear God and keep his commandments. The judgment seat sees every secret thing, and that truth sets her free from performance.

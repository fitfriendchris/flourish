A Deed in a Jar

For men

Jeremiah is in prison while the Babylonian army besieges Jerusalem, and God tells him to buy a field. It is the worst possible time to invest in real estate, and that is exactly the point. Faith in God's future sometimes looks like signing a deed while the world is burning.

For women

A woman often plans for everyone else's future and forgets that she is allowed to plant flags too. Jeremiah's deed in a jar says your long-horizon faith is worth documenting, even when the world around you is unstable.

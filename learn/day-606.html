While Sinners

Day 606 of 1,095

But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.

Romans 5:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You were not worthy of forgiveness when you received it. That is why you can extend it to someone who does not deserve it either.

For women

You did nothing to earn the mercy that saved you. That same mercy is the only reason you can forgive someone who has given you no reason to trust them again.

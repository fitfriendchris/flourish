Day 705 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 101The Quiet Victories — Faithfulness No One Sees

Last Days

For men

Paul does not describe the future to make you paranoid. He describes it so you will recognize the pressure to conform and quietly refuse it.

For women

A woman feels the pressure of the last days in her social feed, her workplace, her parenting groups, and her own mirror. The quiet victory is refusing to let the culture name what is good and what is godly for her.

Biting and Devouring

Day 561 of 1,095

For you, brothers, were called to freedom; but do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh. Rather, serve one another in love. The entire law is fulfilled in a single decree: “Love your neighbor as yourself.”

Galatians 5:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul's verbs are animal ones: bite and eat down. A church consuming itself, and the freedom he had just fought for is the base of operations it launches from.

For women

Works of the flesh is plural. The fruit of the Spirit is singular — one fruit, nine facets. You cannot grow patience on its own.

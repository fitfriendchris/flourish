One Master Only

Day 121 of 1,095

No one can serve two masters: Either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve both God and money.

Matthew 6:24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus does not say money is dangerous. He says it is a master, and He is not willing to share the title.

For women

You cannot love God with all your heart and keep another god in your wallet. Jesus says the arrangement will not last.

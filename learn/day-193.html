Day Trading Is Gambling

For men

The phrase 'one eager to be rich' is a perfect description of day trading. It promises speed, excitement, and wealth without work. Proverbs says the punishment is built in.

For women

A woman often sees the damage of haste before the man living it does. Proverbs 28:20 gives her a way to name the danger without shaming the person: haste to be rich carries its own punishment.

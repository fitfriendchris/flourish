Shepherd Flock

For men

Peter writes to elders as one of them. His charge is not to rule but to God’s flock willingly, eagerly, and by example.

For women

Peter writes to elders, but the whole church lives under shepherding. Women are often the first to notice who is being overlooked, who is being dominated, and who needs to be carried.

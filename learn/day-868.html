Not Tomorrow

Day 868 of 1,095

Do not withhold good from the deserving when it is within your power to act. Do not tell your neighbor, “Come back tomorrow and I will provide”—when you already have the means.

Proverbs 3:27-28 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Hebrew is do not withhold good from its owners. The good you are holding already belongs to them.

For women

Come back tomorrow. Solomon records the exact sentence, which means someone said it to him or he had said it.

Two Requests That Are One

For men

Agur asks for two things and they are the same thing. Both wealth and poverty are named as pressures toward a particular lie.

For women

Agur opens his chapter by calling himself too stupid to be a man — and then asks for an income small enough that he cannot do damage with it.

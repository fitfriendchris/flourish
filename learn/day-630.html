Day 630 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 90Iron Council — Building Your Band of Brothers and Sisters

One Body

For men

Paul compares the church to a human body. One body, many members, all connected by the same Spirit. Unity does not require uniformity.

For women

Paul says the church is one body with many members. A woman's iron council of sisters should reflect that diversity. Different ages, stages, and gifts make the council stronger, not weaker.

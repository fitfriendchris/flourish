The Harvest of Your Righteousness

Day 535 of 1,095

Now He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your store of seed and will increase the harvest of your righteousness. You will be enriched in every way to be generous on every occasion, and through us your generosity will produce thanksgiving to God.

2 Corinthians 9:10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Follow the chain to its end. The output of the whole process is not a larger balance — it is other people's thanksgiving, which is the only thing that outlives you.

For women

The word Paul uses for generosity is haplotes — singleness. Giving with one motive and no second one folded in.

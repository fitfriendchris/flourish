Day 728 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 104Year Two Commencement — The Builder Becomes a Pillar

Contend Faith

For men

The builder becomes a pillar when he is willing to contend earnestly for the faith. Commencement is not a polite exit; it is a call to stand guard over the gospel that was delivered once for all.

For women

A woman becomes a pillar when she learns to contend earnestly for the faith in the spaces where distortion creeps in quietly: the group chat, the podcast, the parenting advice, the casual theology of friends.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

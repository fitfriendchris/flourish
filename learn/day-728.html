Contend Faith

Day 728 of 1,095

Beloved, although I made every effort to write to you about the salvation we share, I felt it necessary to write and urge you to contend earnestly for the faith entrusted once for all to the saints. For certain men have crept in among you unnoticed—ungodly ones who were designated long ago for condemnation. They turn the grace of our God into a license for immorality, and they deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ.

Jude 3-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The builder becomes a pillar when he is willing to contend earnestly for the faith. Commencement is not a polite exit; it is a call to stand guard over the gospel that was delivered once for all.

For women

A woman becomes a pillar when she learns to contend earnestly for the faith in the spaces where distortion creeps in quietly: the group chat, the podcast, the parenting advice, the casual theology of friends.

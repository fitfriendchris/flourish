Love Discipline

Day 786 of 1,095

Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge, but he who hates correction is stupid.

Proverbs 12:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Solomon's word for the alternative is not unwise. It is the word for an animal — brutish, unable to reason.

For women

Reproof is tokachat — correction spoken to you by a person. Not self-discipline. The verse is about receiving, which is the harder half.

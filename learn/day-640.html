Equipping Saints

Day 640 of 1,095

And it was He who gave some to be apostles, some to be prophets, some to be evangelists, and some to be pastors and teachers, to equip the saints for works of ministry and to build up the body of Christ, until we all reach unity in the faith and in the knowledge of the Son of God, as we mature to the full measure of the stature of Christ. Then we will no longer be infants, tossed about by the waves and carried around by every wind of teaching and by the clever cunning of men in their deceitful scheming. Instead, speaking the truth in love, we will in all things grow up into Christ Himself, who is the head. From Him the whole body, fitted and held together by every supporting ligament, grows and builds itself up in love through the work of each individual part.

Ephesians 4:11-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Integration is not a solo project. Christ gives leaders to equip the saints, and every saint has ministry work that builds the body. Your marriage, your money, and your muscles all serve the same building project.

For women

A woman is often the quiet equipping center of a household or a community. She teaches younger women, strengthens friends, and holds the body together with the truth spoken in love.

Half My Goods

Day 867 of 1,095

But Zacchaeus stood up and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord, half of my possessions I give to the poor, and if I have cheated anyone, I will repay it fourfold.” Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because this man too is a son of Abraham.

Luke 19:8-9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Fourfold is the Exodus penalty for a stolen sheep. He applies the harshest restitution rate in the Law to himself, unprompted.

For women

He could not see because of the crowd. The obstacle was other people, and he solved it by climbing above them.

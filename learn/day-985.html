Justice in the Gate

Day 985 of 1,095

Hate evil and love good; establish justice in the gate. Perhaps the LORD, the God of Hosts, will be gracious to the remnant of Joseph.”

Amos 5:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Justice in the gate means fair courts and honest dealing. Amos put it after hate evil and love good, because justice starts in affection.

For women

It may be. Amos offers hope without guarantee, and justice is pursued because it is right, not because success is certain.

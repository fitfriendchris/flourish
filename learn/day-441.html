Rock Fortress

For men

The verb David uses for love here is unusual — a tender, womb-deep word, and this is the only place in the Psalms it is aimed at God.

For women

The images are all fortifications — rock, fortress, stronghold. You do not attack with a fortress. You sleep in one.

Test Own Work

Day 608 of 1,095

Each one should test his own work. Then he will have reason to boast in himself alone, and not in someone else. For each one should carry his own load.

Galatians 6:4-5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Before you demand that someone else fix what they broke, test your own work. Paul says each man must one should carry his own load, and that includes the load of your own contribution to the conflict.

For women

A woman often replays a conflict looking for proof that she was right. Paul tells her to test her own work instead, because bearing her own load is work enough without carrying someone else's guilt.

Due Diligence

Day 163 of 1,095

He who answers a matter before he hears it—this is folly and disgrace to him.

Proverbs 18:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The fastest way to make a bad business decision is to decide while the other person is still speaking. Hearing the matter out is where diligence starts.

For women

You do not have to have an opinion immediately. The person who hears first is rarely the one who is ashamed later.

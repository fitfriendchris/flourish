The twenty-year view

Day 105 of 1,095

Brothers, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead, I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly calling in Christ Jesus.

Philippians 3:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul is not pretending he has arrived, and he is not wallowing in every past failure. He is running a long race, and the only thing that matters is the next faithful step.

For women

Women are often sold the lie that youth is the only season worth having. Paul says the goal is the upward call, which means the most important body is the one you are building for the long haul, not the one you are mourning from ten years ago.

The prudent see danger and take cover, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty.

Proverbs 27:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Now read the rest of the proverb: the simple keep going and pay the penalty. Hiding is not pessimism; it is the mercy that spares the people behind you from your arrogance.

For women

Today walk through it: the same Hebrew word for hiding can also describe the refuge God gives. A woman who prepares is not living in fear; she is building the shelter her household may one day need.

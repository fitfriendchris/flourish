A Good Name Chosen

Day 330 of 1,095

A good name is more desirable than great riches; favor is better than silver and gold.

Proverbs 22:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man's name is not built by public relations; it is built by private choices repeated until they become character. Solomon pairs Proverbs 22:1 with Ecclesiastes 7:1 to say that a good name is both chosen and better than celebration.

For women

A woman's good name is not a performance for an audience; it is the fruit of a thousand quiet decisions to be true. Both Proverbs 22:1 and Ecclesiastes 7:1 say the same thing in different accents: a good name is chosen, and it outlasts perfume.

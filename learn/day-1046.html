Treasures in Heaven

Day 1046 of 1,095

But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moth and rust do not destroy, and where thieves do not break in and steal.

Matthew 6:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

store up for yourselves. Jesus does not ask a man to stop caring about his own future; He tells him where to invest it.

For women

Moth and rust — the Greek word for rust means eating. Earthly treasure is consumed, often slowly and from within.

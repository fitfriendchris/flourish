He Does Not Overlook

For men

The writer makes a claim about God's justice, not His kindness: it would be unjust of Him to forget what you did for people when nobody was recording it.

For women

Forgetting your work would be unjust of God — that is a stronger claim than saying He is kind enough to remember.

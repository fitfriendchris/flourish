Faithful Unto Death

For men

Ten days. Christ told Smyrna their suffering would be real and limited. Faithfulness to death is sustained by knowing trials have an end.

For women

I know your tribulation and your poverty — but you are rich. Christ sees the suffering and names a wealth no one else sees.

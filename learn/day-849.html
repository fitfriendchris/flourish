Nailed to the Cross

For men

Cheirographon — a handwritten certificate of debt, signed by the debtor. Paul says it was not torn up but nailed up, in public, where the amount could be read.

For women

God made you alive with Christ. He forgave — before the cancelling clause, not after it. The order in the sentence is the order of the thing.

Learning How to Abound

For men

Paul says he learned the secret of abundance as well as of need. Plenty is a skill too, and most people never learn it because they assume it needs no learning.

For women

Learned the secret is the word for initiation into a mystery. Paul had to be taught how to have plenty.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

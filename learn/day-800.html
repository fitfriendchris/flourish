Put Off Old

Day 800 of 1,095

to put off your former way of life, your old self, which is being corrupted by its deceitful desires; to be renewed in the spirit of your minds; and to put on the new self, created to be like God in true righteousness and holiness.

Ephesians 4:22-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Three infinitives, and the middle one is passive. You do the putting off and the putting on. The renewing is done to you.

For women

Put off and put on are clothing verbs, and the metaphor has a gap in it. Paul does not describe a swap.

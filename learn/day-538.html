Forty Years at That Gate

For men

Luke tells us later that the man was over forty. He had been carried to that gate daily for decades — and one gift, given once, reached thousands.

For women

He had been carried to the temple gate his whole life and had never once been inside. The healing got him through a door, not just onto his feet.

Covenant Friendship vs. Transactional Connection

For men

Jonathan loved David at the cost of his own throne; covenant friendship binds souls, not schedules or social utility.

For women

Jonathan loved David more than his own crown; covenant friendship gives itself away, while transactional friendship keeps a running total.

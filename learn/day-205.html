The Widow’s Two Coins

For men

Jesus does not count coins the way the treasury does. He measures what is left behind, and a widow who gives everything she has gives more than the rich give from their surplus.

For women

A woman with little is not disqualified from generosity. Her small gift, given in trust, outweighs the noise of larger offerings.

Move for Mission

For men

Paul does not move to Corinth for the weather or the market. He moves because the Spirit sends him, and he finds a home and a job through the network God has already placed there.

For women

A woman usually bears the hidden cost of a move: finding the school, the doctor, the friend, the grocery store, the new rhythm. Acts 18 says that God prepares people in the new place before we arrive, and sometimes a tent shop becomes a home.

If Hearing You Hear

For men

The Hebrew doubles the verb — if hearing you hear. The condition on the whole chapter is attention, and attention is what excellence is made of.

For women

Read the blessings themselves — basket and kneading bowl, coming in and going out. They land in the places an ordinary day actually happens.

Pursue Love

For men

Paul does not begin with a technique for better romance. He tells the church to pursue love the way a hunter pursues game, and to want the gifts that build others up.

For women

Paul tells the church to pursue love with intention, not to wait for it as a feeling. For a woman, this means love is a direction, not a rescue fantasy, and purity grows from what you chase.

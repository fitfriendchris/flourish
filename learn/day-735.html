Come, All Who Thirst

For men

Come thirsty. Even after 730 days, the invitation still stands, and the waters are still free. Thirst is the only currency; the waters are still free after 730 days.

For women

Thirst is not a weakness. It is the only currency that buys what God is offering without money and without cost. Let your thirst invite others to come and drink without money and without cost.

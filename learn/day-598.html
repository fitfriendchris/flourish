Fathers Provoke

For men

Then the focus was the danger of provoking children. Year two returns to ask how fathers show up in the lives of their children with discipline and instruction that does not wound.

For women

Ephesians 6:4 addresses fathers, but mothers are the ones who often translate a father's authority into daily care. The same warning against provocation and the same command to nourish apply to every parent who shows up.

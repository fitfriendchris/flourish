She Set Her Table

Day 924 of 1,095

Wisdom has built her house; she has carved out her seven pillars. She has prepared her meat and mixed her wine; she has also set her table.

Proverbs 9:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Wisdom does not only teach. She builds, prepares, and sets a table before she calls anyone to it.

For women

Wisdom sets her table at the same time Folly sets hers. The chapter holds two invitations, and the voices sound alike.

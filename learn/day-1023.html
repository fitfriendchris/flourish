What the Fire Tests

Day 1023 of 1,095

his workmanship will be evident, because the Day will bring it to light. It will be revealed with fire, and the fire will prove the quality of each man’s work.

1 Corinthians 3:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The fire tests prove the quality of each man’s work, not how much. Three years of activity will be measured by quality.

For women

Saved, but only as through fire. The audit ends in grace: even a builder whose work burns is rescued.

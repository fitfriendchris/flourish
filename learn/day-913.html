Not Many Fathers

Day 913 of 1,095

Even if you have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers; for in Christ Jesus I became your father through the gospel.

1 Corinthians 4:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Instructors can be hired. Fathers cannot. Spiritual fatherhood is measured by responsibility carried, not information passed on.

For women

Countless guides, not many fathers. The shortage in the church is not teaching. It is people who will stay.

Buying the Time at Market

Day 489 of 1,095

Pay careful attention, then, to how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

Ephesians 5:15-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

redeeming the time is a market verb — buying up, the same root as ransom. Time is the asset compounding converts into money, and it is the one you cannot purchase more of.

For women

Buy the time, Paul says, as a merchant buys a commodity he can see running out.

The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy. I have come that they may have life, and have it in all its fullness. I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down His life for the sheep.

John 10:10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus does not say the good shepherd is skilled. He says he stays — and then names exactly why the paid man does not.

For women

The hired hand does not run because he is a coward. He runs because the sheep are not his, and Jesus says so outright.

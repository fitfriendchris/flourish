Redeem at the Gate

For men

Real estate in Israel was never just dirt. It was tied to family, name, and inheritance. Boaz handles the transfer publicly because land and legacy are inseparable.

For women

A woman in crisis is often invisible until someone with resources chooses to see her. Boaz uses his position at the gate to make Ruth and Naomi secure. Property becomes redemptive when it is used to protect the vulnerable.

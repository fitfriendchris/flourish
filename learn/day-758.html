Rend Your Heart

Day 758 of 1,095

“Yet even now,” declares the LORD, “return to Me with all your heart, with fasting, weeping, and mourning.” So rend your hearts and not your garments, and return to the LORD your God. For He is gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion. And He relents from sending disaster.

Joel 2:12-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Yet even now. Two words, after a chapter of total devastation, and they are the whole argument of the passage.

For women

Tearing your clothes was the visible, expensive-looking act. He asks for the invisible one instead, which is much harder to do well and much easier to fake.

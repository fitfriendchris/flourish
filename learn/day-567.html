A Woman of Valour

For men

Eshet chayil is military language — chayil is used of armies. And the poem is a mother's instruction to her son, whose one job in it is to praise her publicly.

For women

She laughs at the time to come. That is the emotional centre of the poem, and it is not a productivity line.

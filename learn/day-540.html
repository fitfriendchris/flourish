A Sentence No Gospel Recorded

For men

Paul quotes words of Jesus that appear in no gospel. They survived because a church remembered them — which is what transmission actually looks like.

For women

The handover ends with grown men weeping on a beach, holding on to him. A legacy passage that closes in tears and embraces, not a document.

Day 114 of 1,095

There the ships pass, and Leviathan, which You formed to frolic there.

Psalm 104:26 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God made a sea monster with no purpose except to play. If He can do that, your hobby does not need a business plan.

For women

A creature of the deep was made for play. That is not a side note; it is part of what the psalm says about God's wisdom.

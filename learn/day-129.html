Envelope Theology

For men

A shepherd cannot lead sheep from a throne. Your money lives in categories; the envelope system is the fence that keeps each one safe. Take this truth into today’s decisions.

For women

Money that moves unseen eventually controls what you value. The envelope system is not deprivation; it is the fence that lets each part of your life graze in its proper pasture.

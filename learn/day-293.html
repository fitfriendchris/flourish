Quick to Hear, Slow to Speak

Day 293 of 1,095

My beloved brothers, understand this: Everyone should be quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to anger,

James 1:19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Your ears are a bigger marriage tool than your mouth. Use them first. A man's first job in a hard conversation is not to fix the problem. It is to understand the person in front of him.

For women

Being heard is one of the deepest needs of the human heart. Marriage is supposed to be the place where that need is met first.

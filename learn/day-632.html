Discipline Love

Day 632 of 1,095

My son, do not reject the discipline of the LORD, and do not loathe His rebuke; for the LORD disciplines the one He loves, as does a father the son in whom he delights.

Proverbs 3:11-12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The same correction you resent today is the signature of a Father who has not given up on you. Q3 is a good moment to stop despising the process.

For women

God's discipline is not punishment for being unwanted; it is proof that you are cherished. A daughter He delights in is a daughter He refuses to leave as she is.

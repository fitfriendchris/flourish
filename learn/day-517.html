What a Day Gives Birth To

For men

Solomon forbids boasting, not planning. And the reason is a birth image — you do not know what a day will bring forth, which is the argument for more than one stream.

For women

Chapter twenty-seven is about being known by other people. You cannot see tomorrow, and verse nineteen says you cannot see your own face either.

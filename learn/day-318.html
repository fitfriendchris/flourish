Truth in the Inward Being

Day 318 of 1,095

Surely You desire truth in the inmost being; You teach me wisdom in the inmost place.

Psalm 51:6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God does not want your best performance. He wants truth in the place no one else can reach. He wants the truth beneath the performance, the reality behind the reputation.

For women

God is not asking you to be presentable. He is asking you to be real with Him in the secret places..

When the Land Prospers

For men

Moses warns Israel that the very prosperity they are praying for will become their greatest temptation. Good houses are a gift from God and a trap for the proud.

For women

A woman often builds the home, fills it, and then believes its beauty is a report on her worth. Deuteronomy 8 says the good land is a gift, and the woman who remembers that can rest without either guilt or vanity.

Unplugging in an Always-On Culture

For men

The disciples were doing kingdom work, and the crowd pressure was so relentless they could not eat. Jesus did not commend their dedication; He ordered them to leave.

For women

The disciples were doing kingdom work, and they still needed to be told to stop. Busyness is not proof that you matter; it is proof that your rhythm is broken.

Planted Tree

Day 772 of 1,095

Blessed is the man who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked, or set foot on the path of sinners, or sit in the seat of mockers. But his delight is in the Law of the LORD, and on His law he meditates day and night. He is like a tree planted by streams of water, yielding its fruit in season, whose leaf does not wither, and who prospers in all he does.

Psalm 1:1-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Planted is a passive participle. The tree did not find the stream — somebody carried it there and put it in the ground.

For women

Read the first verse before the tree. Walks, stands, sits — three postures, each more settled than the last, describing how a person ends up somewhere she never decided to go.

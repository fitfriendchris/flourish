Fear Trust

Day 59 of 1,095

When I am afraid, I put my trust in You. In God, whose word I praise—in God I trust. I will not be afraid. What can man do to me?

Psalm 56:3-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

David does not say if. He says when — and then tells you what he does in the moment rather than how he avoids it.

For women

David was afraid and trusted at the same time. Those are not opposites.

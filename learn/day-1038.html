Lay Aside the Weight

Day 1038 of 1,095

Therefore, since we are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, let us throw off every encumbrance and the sin that so easily entangles, and let us run with endurance the race set out for us.

Hebrews 12:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A weight is not a sin. Runners shed anything that slows them, even things that are harmless for spectators.

For women

Sin that so easily entangles is like a long robe that tangles the runner's legs. Some entanglements must be cut away.

A Question Used as a Defence

For men

The lawyer answers his own question correctly, and then asks a second one — not to learn, but to narrow what he owes. Luke tells you the motive outright.

For women

And your neighbour as yourself. The measure the verse assumes is that you have some regard for yourself to measure by.

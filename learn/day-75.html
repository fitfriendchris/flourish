I Am With You

Day 75 of 1,095

Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be afraid, for I am your God. I will strengthen you; I will surely help you; I will uphold you with My righteous right hand.

Isaiah 41:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God's answer to fear is not an explanation or a guarantee. It is His presence, stated twice.

For women

He does not say there is nothing to fear. He says I am with you.

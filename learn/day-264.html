Leave Your Gift at the Altar

For men

Jesus says worship waits when a relationship is broken. Reconciliation is not an extra-credit act; it is the prerequisite for an offering that pleases God.

For women

A woman cannot offer God a heart full of worship while holding onto a fractured relationship. Jesus calls her to stop, turn around, and reconcile before she returns to the altar.

Be Holy

Day 724 of 1,095

“Speak to the whole congregation of Israel and tell them: Be holy because I, the LORD your God, am holy.

Leviticus 19:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Holiness is not a mood or a summit experience. It is the character of God reproduced in the community of his people, and the commencement call is to become a pillar set apart for him.

For women

Holiness is a household command. A woman does not become holy in isolation; she becomes holy as she speaks truth, keeps boundaries, honors Sabbath, and loves mercy within the congregation God has placed around her.

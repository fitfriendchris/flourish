Fear Beginning

Day 672 of 1,095

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; all who follow His precepts gain rich understanding. His praise endures forever!

Psalm 111:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

True influence begins with the fear of the Lord. A platform built on any other foundation will eventually look foolish, no matter how successful it appears.

For women

A woman who fears the Lord has the beginning of wisdom, and that wisdom will shape every room she enters. Her platform is built to last because it rests on the only foundation that does not move.

Confession as Medicine

Day 324 of 1,095

Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail.

James 5:16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Confession is not punishment; it is the medicine that heals the infection secrecy keeps alive. This is how the body holds each other.

For women

The wound that heals often begins with the words 'I need to tell you something.' This is how the body holds each other.

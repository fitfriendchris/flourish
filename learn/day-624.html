Put Away Falsehood

For men

Paul moves from the old self to the new. The new self puts away falsehood and speaks the truth because the church is a body, not a collection of individuals. Anger is permitted but not allowed to linger, because lingering anger gives the devil an opening.

For women

Paul's commands about truth and anger apply to every member of the body, but women often carry the relational memory of a community. They notice when truth is missing and when anger is festering, and they have a strategic role in restoring honesty and peace.

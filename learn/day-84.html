Decide and move

Day 84 of 1,095

But Ruth replied: “Do not urge me to leave you or to turn from following you. For wherever you go, I will go, and wherever you live, I will live; your people will be my people, and your God will be my God. Where you die, I will die, and there I will be buried. May the LORD punish me, and ever so severely, if anything but death separates you and me.” When Naomi saw that Ruth was determined to go with her, she stopped trying to persuade her.

Ruth 1:16-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Ruth did not keep gathering options. She saw who Naomi was, saw what love required, and decided. Her words are not the poetry of hesitation. They are the speech of a woman who has made up her mind.

For women

Ruth had no guarantee that Bethlehem would welcome her. She only knew that Naomi was worth following. That is the heart of a good decision: not perfect information, but a settled love that is willing to move.

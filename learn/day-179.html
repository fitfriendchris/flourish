Purpose Over Popularity

Day 179 of 1,095

At daybreak, Jesus went out to a solitary place, and the crowds were looking for Him. They came to Him and tried to keep Him from leaving. But Jesus told them, “I must preach the good news of the kingdom of God to the other towns as well, because that is why I was sent.” And He continued to preach in the synagogues of Judea.

Luke 4:42-44 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The crowds wanted to keep Jesus. He left them because His purpose was bigger than their approval. You are not called to be available to everyone; you are called to be faithful to the purpose that sent you.

For women

The crowds tried to hold Jesus back, but He would not let popularity rewrite His calling. That is the brand test every woman faces.

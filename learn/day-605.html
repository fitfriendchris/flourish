Clothe Humility

Day 605 of 1,095

Young men, in the same way, submit yourselves to your elders. And all of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because, “God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble.” Humble yourselves, therefore, under God’s mighty hand, so that in due time He may exalt you. Cast all your anxiety on Him, because He cares for you.

1 Peter 5:5-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Humility is the garment that makes forgiveness possible. Peter says to put it on like an apron before you approach the people who have wounded you.

For women

A woman's bitterness often hides behind competence and quiet strength. Peter tells her to clothe herself with humility, which means she can stop carrying offenses as if they were proof of her value.

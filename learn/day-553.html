Bearing Up Under Evil

For men

One Greek compound: bearing-up-under-evil. Paul's instruction is about how you treat someone who is both wrong and against you.

For women

Paul's last word on the opponent is that he has been captured. Not an enemy — a prisoner, doing someone else's will.

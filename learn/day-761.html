Every Word Proves True

For men

Proves true is a smelting word. Agur is not saying God's words turn out accurate. He is saying they have been through the furnace and nothing burned off.

For women

The man who says every word of God is refined has just spent three verses saying he himself is too stupid to be a man and has not learned wisdom.

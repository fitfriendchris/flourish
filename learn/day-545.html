What Do You Have That You Did Not Receive

Day 545 of 1,095

So then, men ought to regard us as servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God. Now it is required of stewards that they be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul reviews himself and then refuses to pass verdict: do not judge before the time. A review that ends in a sentence on yourself has overstepped.

For women

When Paul actually reviews his own quarter, he lists hunger, poor clothing, homelessness and being called the scum of the world.

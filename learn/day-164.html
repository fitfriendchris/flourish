Paying Taxes

Day 164 of 1,095

This is also why you pay taxes. For the authorities are God’s servants, who devote themselves to their work. Pay everyone what you owe him: taxes to whom taxes are due, revenue to whom revenue is due, respect to whom respect is due, honor to whom honor is due.

Romans 13:6-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul treats tax-paying as part of worship, not as a secular inconvenience to minimise. The stakes are higher than they look.

For women

Your cheque to the government can be as much an act of faith as your cheque to the church — if you write it believing God is over both.

Every Dollar a Name

For men

A steward does not own the estate; he accounts for it. The zero-based budget is your monthly accounting before God. Take this truth into today’s decisions.

For women

Faithfulness is not about the size of your account; it is about whether you can account for it. Give every dollar a name, and you give God a life he can trust with more.

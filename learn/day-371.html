Shepherd

For men

He MAKES me lie down. It is a causative verb — because a sheep that is anxious, hungry, or at odds with the flock will not lie down on its own.

For women

Rest here is not a reward for finishing. It is evidence that the shepherd has already handled what was keeping you upright.

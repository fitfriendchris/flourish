Day 425 of 1,095

Rest in God alone, O my soul, for my hope comes from Him. He alone is my rock and my salvation; He is my fortress; I will not be shaken.

Psalm 62:5-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

David talks TO his soul rather than listening to it. And by the end of the psalm the word 'greatly' has dropped out of his refrain.

For women

He is not talking to God in this verse. He is talking to himself — and telling his soul what to do rather than asking it how it feels.

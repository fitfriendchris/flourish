Exercise has its place

For men

Paul does not insult your gym membership. He ranks it. physical exercise is of limited value - real value, present-tense value - but it is not the highest value, and treating it as though it were is the fast track to a smaller life.

For women

You have probably heard this verse used to shame anyone who cares about fitness. That is not what Paul is doing. He is protecting you from a life where the mirror decides your worth.

One Thing I Do

Day 1034 of 1,095

Brothers, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead, I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly calling in Christ Jesus.

Philippians 3:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

At the end of his ministry Paul still said I have not taken hold of it. Finishing strong requires humility until the last lap.

For women

Because Christ Jesus has made me His own. Paul pressed on because he was already held.

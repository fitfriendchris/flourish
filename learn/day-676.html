Faith in the Furnace

Day 676 of 1,095

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego replied to the king, “O Nebuchadnezzar, we have no need to answer you in this matter. If the God whom we serve exists, then He is able to deliver us from the blazing fiery furnace and from your hand, O king. But even if He does not, let it be known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the golden statue you have set up.”

Daniel 3:16-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The long road back requires furnace faith: full confidence in God's power, full resolve to obey even if the deliverance does not come on your schedule.

For women

A woman's furnace is often relational or private — the marriage that feels like fire, the hidden temptation, the slow loss of something precious. Faith in the furnace means she will not bow, even if no one sees her refusal.

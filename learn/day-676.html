Faith in the Furnace

For men

The long road back requires furnace faith: full confidence in God's power, full resolve to obey even if the deliverance does not come on your schedule.

For women

A woman's furnace is often relational or private — the marriage that feels like fire, the hidden temptation, the slow loss of something precious. Faith in the furnace means she will not bow, even if no one sees her refusal.

Widow's Oil

For men

Sometimes freedom costs the thing you love most. The widow's oil was not trash. It was her last comfort, and God asked her to sell it to buy her children back.

For women

A woman will sell her own needs before she sells what protects her children's routine. But the widow's oil teaches that sometimes the most loving thing a mother can do is part with the comfort so she can keep the children.

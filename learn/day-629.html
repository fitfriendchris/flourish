Put On Clothes

For men

Paul gives the church a wardrobe. The clothes are virtues, and the reason for wearing them is identity. You are chosen, holy, and beloved, so put on compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, patience, and love.

For women

Paul tells the whole church, including every woman in it, to put on a wardrobe of virtues. The iron council of sisters only works when each woman dresses herself in compassion, humility, patience, and love.

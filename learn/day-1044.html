Very Useful to Me

Day 1044 of 1,095

Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you, because he is useful to me in the ministry.

2 Timothy 4:11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul once refused to take Mark. At the end he asked for him by name. A Paul must be willing to change his assessment of a Timothy.

For women

Luke is with me. Faithful companionship at the end is rare, and a woman can be the one who stays.

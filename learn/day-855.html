Day 855 of 1,095

After this, the Lord appointed seventy-two others and sent them two by two ahead of Him to every town and place He was about to visit. And He told them, “The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few. Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into His harvest.

Luke 10:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

He sends them ahead into every place He was about to visit. They are not a substitute for Him. They are the advance work.

For women

He tells them to pray for labourers, and then sends them. The prayer and the answer are the same people.

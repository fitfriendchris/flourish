Day 678 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 97Failure, Forgiveness, and the Long Road Back

Hate Family

For men

The long road back costs everything. Jesus demands first place over every lesser love, including the family that failed you and the life you thought you were building.

For women

A woman often organizes her life around the people she loves. Jesus says that even these loves must be ordered under him, or they will become the very idols that keep her from coming back.

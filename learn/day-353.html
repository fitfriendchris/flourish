Teach Diligently Every Day

For men

Diligent teaching is not an event on the calendar. It is the atmosphere of an ordinary day, and a man who fails to teach in the small hours fails to build a legacy.

For women

A woman teaches her children most powerfully not in formal lessons but in the small hours she shares with them while living her own faith.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

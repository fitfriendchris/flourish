Moses Chose

Day 1052 of 1,095

He chose to suffer oppression with God’s people rather than to experience the fleeting enjoyment of sin. He valued disgrace for Christ above the treasures of Egypt, for he was looking ahead to his reward.

Hebrews 11:25-26 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Moses did the accounting. He valued disgrace for Christ above, and he chose accordingly.

For women

Fleeting pleasures. The Greek word means for a season — sin offers real pleasure, but it runs out.

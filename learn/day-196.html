Selling in Panic

For men

Jesus endured the cross because He saw the joy ahead. The investor endures a market downturn by keeping his eyes on the same horizon. Selling in panic trades a temporary feeling for a permanent loss.

For women

A woman often absorbs the household's anxiety during a crisis. Hebrews 12:2 calls her to look past the shame of the moment to the joy ahead, and to refuse the lie that selling in panic will restore her peace.

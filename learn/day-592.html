Discipline Sons

For men

The author of Hebrews does not let the church romanticize pain. Discipline is real, it hurts, and it is the proof that God treats you as a son rather than a stranger.

For women

The language of Hebrews is masculine, but the application belongs to every daughter of God. Discipline is the proof that you are inside the family, not the proof that you have been cast out.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

Red Flags When Dating Later

Day 280 of 1,095

There are three things too wonderful for me, four that I cannot understand: the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a snake on a rock, the way of a ship at sea, and the way of a man with a maiden.

Proverbs 30:18-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Attraction is a mystery, but character is visible. A wise man learns to enjoy the wonder of romance without ignoring the red flags that predict future pain.

For women

Romantic attraction is wonderful, but it is not wisdom. A discerning woman watches character, listens to counsel, and refuses to let feelings override red flags.

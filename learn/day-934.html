In Peace I Lie Down

Day 934 of 1,095

I will lie down and sleep in peace, for You alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety.

Psalm 4:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The bed is where anger is either settled or stored. Psalm 4 moves from be angry and do not sin to in peace I lie down.

For women

You alone make me dwell in safety. The word alone can also mean securely — a woman's rest does not depend on everything being handled.

Day 91 of 1,095

Therefore do not let sin reign in your mortal body so that you obey its desires. Do not present the parts of your body to sin as instruments of wickedness, but present yourselves to God as those who have been brought from death to life; and present the parts of your body to Him as instruments of righteousness.

Romans 6:12-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Sin does not just live in your head. It recruits your hands, your mouth, your eyes, and your body. You cannot be faithful in your mind while handing your body to sin.

For women

What your body does matters, because your body is not neutral ground. It is either an instrument of righteousness or an instrument of sin. Every day you choose who gets the use of your hands, mouth, eyes, and presence.

And Adds No Sorrow

Day 504 of 1,095

The blessing of the LORD enriches, and He adds no sorrow to it.

Proverbs 10:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The contrast is not rich against poor. It is wealth that arrives clean against wealth that arrives with grief attached — and the word for sorrow is the one from Eden.

For women

The word for sorrow here is the word from Genesis three. God can give increase without the curse riding along with it.

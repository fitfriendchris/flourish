Without Grumbling

Day 674 of 1,095

Do everything without complaining or arguing, so that you may be blameless and pure, children of God without fault in a crooked and perverse generation, in which you shine as lights in the world as you hold forth the word of life, in order that I may boast on the day of Christ that I did not run or labor in vain.

Philippians 2:14-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The long road back is paved with small obediences done without complaint. Grumbling turns a recovery into a second failure; silence before God keeps the first failure from becoming a habit.

For women

A woman's silence after failure can be more healing than her words. The long road back asks her to stop rehearsing the grievance and start rehearsing the grace she has received.

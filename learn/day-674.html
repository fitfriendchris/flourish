Without Grumbling

For men

The long road back is paved with small obediences done without complaint. Grumbling turns a recovery into a second failure; silence before God keeps the first failure from becoming a habit.

For women

A woman's silence after failure can be more healing than her words. The long road back asks her to stop rehearsing the grievance and start rehearsing the grace she has received.

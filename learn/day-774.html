Faith That Moves Mountains

Day 774 of 1,095

“Have faith in God,” Jesus said to them. “Truly I tell you that if anyone says to this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,’ and has no doubt in his heart but believes that it will happen, it will be done for him. Therefore I tell you, whatever you ask for in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours.

Mark 11:22-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

He says this walking down from Bethany, and there is a specific mountain in front of them with a specific institution standing on it.

For women

Read the next sentence. He attaches forgiveness to the mountain-moving promise immediately, in the same breath, and almost nobody quotes them together.

No Longer Knew

For men

Better a poor but wise youth than an old but foolish king who no longer knows how to take a warning. The disqualifier is not age. It is having stopped listening.

For women

Lehizzaher is to be warned. The lost capability is not judgment. It is the ability to receive a caution.

Boaz at the Gate

Day 986 of 1,095

Meanwhile, Boaz went to the gate and sat down there. Soon the kinsman-redeemer of whom he had spoken came along, and Boaz said, “Come over here, my friend, and sit down.” So he went over and sat down. Then Boaz took ten of the elders of the city and said, “Sit here,” and they did so.

Ruth 4:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Boaz went went to the gate and sat down. Influence exercised properly is patient, public, and witnessed.

For women

Sit still, my daughter. Ruth was not at the gate, and trusting a good advocate to act is its own kind of faith.

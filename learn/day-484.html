The LORD Was With Him in Both

For men

The Lord was with Joseph in the prosperous house. Eighteen verses later, after he refuses, the text says exactly the same thing about the prison.

For women

The same sentence covers the prosperous house and the prison. That is the promise — not the outcome, the presence.

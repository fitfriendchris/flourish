They Laid Hands

Day 1067 of 1,095

While they were worshiping the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, “Set apart for Me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.” And after they had fasted and prayed, they laid their hands on them and sent them off.

Acts 13:2-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Antioch sent its two best leaders. Commissioning is costly, and the Spirit often asks a church to give away what it most values.

For women

Sent them off is apelysan — released them. Commissioning is an act of letting go.

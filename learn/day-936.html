Proverbs 15:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Love is the ingredient Solomon adds to the cheaper meal. A household budget cannot buy the thing that makes dinner good.

For women

Where love is. The proverb does not say who brings it. A woman at the table can bring it even when others do not.

Some of Your Authority

Day 858 of 1,095

“May the LORD, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation who will go out and come in before them, and who will lead them out and bring them in, so that the congregation of the LORD will not be like sheep without a shepherd.” And the LORD replied to Moses, “Take Joshua son of Nun, a man with the Spirit in him, and lay your hands on him. … Confer on him some of your authority, so that the whole congregation of Israel will obey him.

Numbers 27:16-18; 27:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Some of your authority. Not all of it — mehodekha, from your splendour, a portion. The text is precise and the precision is the lesson.

For women

Moses asks the question the week he is told he will not enter the land. He raises it himself.

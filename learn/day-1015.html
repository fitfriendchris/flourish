Whom Shall I Send

Day 1015 of 1,095

Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying: “Whom shall I send? Who will go for Us?” And I said: “Here am I. Send me!”

Isaiah 6:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Here am I. Send me came after woe is me. The people God sends are people He has first cleansed.

For women

Who will go for us? God's question invites a volunteer, and the us hints at a conversation within the Godhead.

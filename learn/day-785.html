A Watered Garden

For men

What is promised to the one who pours out is not a fuller cup. It is becoming a spring, which is a different kind of thing entirely.

For women

Satisfy your desire in scorched places. Not out of them. The preposition is the whole promise, and it is smaller and better than the one we want.

Inner Renewed

Day 797 of 1,095

Therefore we do not lose heart. Though our outer self is wasting away, yet our inner self is being renewed day by day. For our light and momentary affliction is producing for us an eternal weight of glory that is far beyond comparison. So we fix our eyes not on what is seen, but on what is unseen. For what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.

2 Corinthians 4:16-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Day by day is a Hebraism — day and day. The renewal runs on exactly the same schedule as the wasting, in the same person, at the same time.

For women

Glory in Hebrew is a weight word. Paul is playing on it — light affliction producing heavy glory — and the verb says the affliction is manufacturing the weight, not merely preceding it.

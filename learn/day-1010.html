Act wisely toward outsiders, redeeming the time. Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how to answer everyone.

Colossians 4:5-6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Make redeeming the time means buying up the opportunity. A sent man treats conversations with outsiders as moments to redeem.

For women

Seasoned with salt, so that you may know how to answer everyone. Good speech is fitted to the individual.

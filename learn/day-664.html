Day 664 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 95The Discipline of Endurance — Year Two Perseverance

Finish Course

For men

Paul's life is disposable compared to his mission. Year two perseverance means caring more about finishing what God gave you than about preserving your comfort, reputation, or safety.

For women

A woman who endures in year two holds her own life loosely. She is not reckless; she is resolved. The mission God gave her matters more than her comfort, and finishing it matters more than protecting herself.

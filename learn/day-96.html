Daniel's Conviction

Day 96 of 1,095

But Daniel made up his mind that he would not defile himself with the king’s food or wine. So he asked the chief official for permission not to defile himself. Now God had granted Daniel favor and compassion from the chief official, but he said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has assigned your food and drink. For why should he see your faces looking thinner than those of the other young men your age? You would endanger my head before the king!” Then Daniel said to the steward whom the chief official had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, “Please test your servants for ten days. Let us be given only vegetables to eat and water to drink. Then compare our appearances with those of the young men who are eating the royal food, and deal with your servants according to what you see.” So he consented to this and tested them for ten days. And at the end of ten days, they looked healthier and better nourished than all the young men who were eating the king’s food. So the steward continued to withhold their choice food and the wine they were to drink, and he gave them vegetables instead.

Daniel 1:8-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Daniel did not refuse the king’s table because he had read a diet book. He had a conviction about food, and he was willing to look strange for it. That is a very different posture from hopping on the latest eating fad.

For women

The world will sell you a new eating plan every season, but Daniel refused the king’s table because his identity was settled, not because a celebrity had convinced him. Conviction looks like steadiness; faddishness looks like restlessness.

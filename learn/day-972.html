The Hired Hand

Day 972 of 1,095

The hired hand is not the shepherd, and the sheep are not his own. When he sees the wolf coming, he abandons the sheep and runs away. Then the wolf pounces on them and scatters the flock. The man runs away because he is a hired servant and is unconcerned for the sheep.

John 10:12-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The hired hand flees because the sheep are not his. Ownership is what keeps a guard at his post when the wolf comes.

For women

The wolf snatches and scatters. A woman who stays can help regather what was scattered.

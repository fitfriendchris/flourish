Their Deeds Follow

Day 1048 of 1,095

And I heard a voice from heaven telling me to write, “Blessed are the dead—those who die in the Lord from this moment on.” “Yes,” says the Spirit, “they will rest from their labors, for their deeds will follow them.”

Revelation 14:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Their deeds will follow them. Nothing done for Christ is left behind at death; it goes ahead into eternity with the one who did it.

For women

Rest from their labours. The word is kopos, wearisome toil, and the promise is rest for those who laboured hard.

Day 1091 of 1,095

Jesus asked a third time, “Simon son of John, do you love Me?” Peter was deeply hurt that Jesus had asked him a third time, “Do you love Me?” “Lord, You know all things,” he replied. “You know I love You.” Jesus said to him, “Feed My sheep.

John 21:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Peter was sent after being grieved. The commission to feed Christ's sheep comes to restored men, not flawless ones.

For women

Lord, You know all things,” he replied. Peter's final answer was not a boast but an appeal to Christ's knowledge of his heart.

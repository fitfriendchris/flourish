As the Father Sent Me

Day 1004 of 1,095

Again Jesus said to them, “Peace be with you. As the Father has sent Me, so also I am sending you.”

John 20:21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

As the Father sent Me describes a manner, not only a mission. Jesus showed His wounds before He sent anyone.

For women

He breathed on them. The same verb describes God breathing life into Adam. A woman is sent with new breath, not only a task.

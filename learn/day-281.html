Do Not Awaken Love

For men

Love has a right season, and waking it too early is like opening a gift before its time—you spoil the purpose it was meant to fulfill.

For women

A woman who learns to let love sleep until the right hour protects her own heart and the hearts of every man she could carelessly stir.

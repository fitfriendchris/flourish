Recovery Is Holy

Day 112 of 1,095

Then he lay down under the broom tree and fell asleep. Suddenly an angel touched him and said, “Get up and eat.” And he looked around, and there by his head was a cake of bread baked over hot coals, and a jar of water. So he ate and drank and lay down again. A second time the angel of the LORD returned and touched him, saying, “Get up and eat, or the journey will be too much for you.” So he got up and ate and drank. And strengthened by that food, he walked forty days and forty nights until he reached Horeb, the mountain of God.

1 Kings 19:5-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Elijah did not hear the still small voice until he had slept twice and eaten twice. Recovery is not a detour from the mission. It is part of it.

For women

Elijah asked to die under a tree. God answered with bread, water, and sleep — twice — before He said anything dramatic.

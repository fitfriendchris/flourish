Gift of Enough

For men

Agur asks God for a narrow band: not poverty, not riches, just enough. He knows that too much and too little can both destroy a man's soul.

For women

A woman often prays for more or prays for rescue. Agur teaches her to pray for the narrow band of enough, where the soul is neither spoiled by riches nor cornered by poverty.

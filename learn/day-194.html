Thirty, Sixty, a Hundred Fold

For men

The same sower, the same seed, the same field, yet different yields. In investing, your results will differ from your brother's even when you use the same strategy, because timing, temperament, and circumstances are not identical.

For women

A woman often compares households, salaries, vacations, and homes. The parable says the same seed produces different yields because the soil differs. Your story is your soil, and comparison will only make you uproot what is growing.

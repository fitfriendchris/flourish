The Friend Who Opens Doors

Day 256 of 1,095

Then Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul, and when he found him, he brought him back to Antioch. So for a full year they met together with the church and taught large numbers of people. The disciples were first called Christians at Antioch.

Acts 11:25-26 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Barnabas did not need to be Paul’s senior. He simply believed in him when no one else did, connected him to opportunity, and worked beside him. Peer mentors are often the most underrated gift in a man’s life.

For women

Barnabas saw Saul when others saw only danger. A woman who wants to flourish needs peers who recognize her gifts even when she cannot see them herself, and who invite her into places where those gifts can serve.

Giving and Receiving

Day 481 of 1,095

Nevertheless, you have done well to share in my affliction. And as you Philippians know, in the early days of the gospel, when I left Macedonia, no church but you partnered with me in the matter of giving and receiving. For even while I was in Thessalonica, you provided for my needs again and again. Not that I am seeking a gift, but I am looking for the fruit that may be credited to your account. I have all I need and more, now that I have received your gifts from Epaphroditus. They are a fragrant offering, an acceptable sacrifice, well-pleasing to God. And my God will supply all your needs according to His glorious riches in Christ Jesus.

Philippians 4:14-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul thanks the Philippians in accounting language — an account of giving and receiving, paid in full — and then calls the transaction a sacrifice pleasing to God.

For women

Paid in full is a receipt word — the one on ordinary papyrus receipts — and Paul calls the payment a sacrifice pleasing to God.

Arise and Come Away

Day 937 of 1,095

My beloved calls to me, “Arise, my darling. Come away with me, my beautiful one. For now the winter is past; the rain is over and gone.

Song of Solomon 2:10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Winter ends when someone says so. In the Song, the beloved names the new season and invites her into it.

For women

My beautiful one. The call to come away begins with being seen as lovely, and after a long winter she may need to hear it before she can move.

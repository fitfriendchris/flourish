Books on Death Row

Day 338 of 1,095

When you come, bring the cloak that I left with Carpus at Troas, and my scrolls, especially the parchments.

2 Timothy 4:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A dying apostle asked for books. That is the posture of a lifelong learner. A dying apostle asked for books; his mind outlived his circumstances.

For women

A woman's hunger to keep learning honors God even in seasons of limitation. Paul's last request reminds us that a woman's mind is never too full or too tired for one more truth.

Full of the Spirit

For men

Joshua was filled with the spirit of wisdom because Moses had laid hands on him. A leader's final act can equip the next.

For women

The days of weeping ended. Israel grieved Moses for thirty days, and then they followed Joshua. Grief and new leadership both had their time.

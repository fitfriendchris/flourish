Opens No One Shuts

Day 643 of 1,095

To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: These are the words of the One who is holy and true, who holds the key of David. What He opens no one can shut, and what He shuts no one can open. I know your deeds. Behold, I have placed before you an open door, which no one can shut. I know that you have only a little strength, yet you have kept My word and have not denied My name.

Revelation 3:7-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Integration happens under an open door. Christ holds the keys, and when he opens a path across your health, wealth, and relationships, no enemy can close it.

For women

A woman's open doors are often relational — a conversation, a friendship, a season of service, a moment of courage. Christ sets them before her, and no one can shut what he has opened.

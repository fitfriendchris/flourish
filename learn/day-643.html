Day 643 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 92The Integrated Life — One Lord Over Every Ledger

Opens No One Shuts

For men

Integration happens under an open door. Christ holds the keys, and when he opens a path across your health, wealth, and relationships, no enemy can close it.

For women

A woman's open doors are often relational — a conversation, a friendship, a season of service, a moment of courage. Christ sets them before her, and no one can shut what he has opened.

Excellence as Witness

For men

Daniel did not rise because he prayed the most. He rose so. His competence opened the door; his integrity kept him from being thrown out.

For women

Daniel rose in a pagan workplace because his work and his worship were the same life. Competence got him the seat; character kept him in it.

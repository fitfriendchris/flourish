The Lord Is One

Day 647 of 1,095

Jesus replied, “This is the most important: ‘Hear, O Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is One. Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength.’ The second is this: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ No other commandment is greater than these.”

Mark 12:29-31 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Every other calling rests on this foundation. The Lord is One He must be one over every compartment of life. Love Him wholly, and the rest of your calling becomes single-minded rather than scattered.

For women

The shema is not a slogan; it is a call to wholeness. A woman who loves the Lord with all of her heart, soul, mind, and strength will stop compartmentalizing her calling into the hours that feel religious.

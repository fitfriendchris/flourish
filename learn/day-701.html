Unchangeable Purpose

Day 701 of 1,095

So when God wanted to make the unchanging nature of His purpose very clear to the heirs of the promise, He guaranteed it with an oath. Thus by two unchangeable things in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled to take hold of the hope set before us may be strongly encouraged. We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. It enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain, where Jesus our forerunner has entered on our behalf. He has become a high priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.

Hebrews 6:17-20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God's purpose for your life is unchangeable, and he guaranteed it with an oath. That certainty is the an anchor that holds your time, talent, and treasure steady through every storm.

For women

A woman's life is full of change — bodies, schedules, incomes, relationships — but God's purpose for her is unchangeable, guaranteed by his own oath and anchored in Christ. She can hold fast to hope even when everything around her shifts.

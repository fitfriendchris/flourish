A Good Foundation

Day 1047 of 1,095

treasuring up for themselves a firm foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of that which is truly life.

1 Timothy 6:19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Storing treasure for the future is how a man takes hold of that which is truly life. Generosity is not the loss of life but the grip on it.

For women

Ready to share is koinōnikous — generous in fellowship. A woman's wealth becomes real life when it builds community.

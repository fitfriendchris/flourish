I Have Fought the Good Fight

For men

A man at the end of a faithful year does not apologize for the fight. He names it, finishes it, and keeps the faith for the next one.

For women

A woman who finishes a year of faithfulness does not need the crowd's applause. She needs only the quiet certainty that she has kept the faith.

Heals Brokenhearted

For men

Two years have left you with both victories and wounds. Year three says God heals and binds, and the man who has experienced that healing can now point another man to the healer.

For women

After two years you carry both strength and scars. Year three says God binds up the wounds of the brokenhearted, and a healed woman can become a map for another woman's healing.

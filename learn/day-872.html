Day 872 of 1,095

Kindness to the poor is a loan to the LORD, and He will repay the lender.

Proverbs 19:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Malveh YHWH — he is a loan to the LORD. God puts himself on the debtor side of the ledger.

For women

Chonen — from chen, grace. The giving is described with a word that by definition creates no debt in the one who receives it.

Unless the LORD Builds

Day 1054 of 1,095

A song of ascents. Of Solomon. Unless the LORD builds the house, its builders labor in vain; unless the LORD protects the city, its watchmen stand guard in vain.

Psalm 127:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Unless the LORD protects the city. Institutions need God's guarding as much as God's building.

For women

House in Hebrew can mean a dynasty. Building a house is building a line that continues.

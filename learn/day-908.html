Thorns Choke It

Day 908 of 1,095

The seed sown among the thorns is the one who hears the word, but the worries of this life and the deceitfulness of wealth choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.

Matthew 13:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Thorns do not kill the seed. They choke it slowly, and the plant looks alive right up to harvest.

For women

Anxiety and wealth are listed together because they grow together. More money seldom removes the worry it promised to remove.

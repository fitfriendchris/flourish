Charge It To Me

Day 847 of 1,095

But if he has wronged you in any way or owes you anything, charge it to my account. I, Paul, write this with my own hand. I will repay it—not to mention that you owe me your very self.

Philemon 18-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Ellogei — put it on my ledger. Paul stops dictating, takes the pen, and writes a legally binding note into the middle of a letter.

For women

Then, after signing a promissory note, he mentions in passing that Philemon owes him everything — and does not collect.

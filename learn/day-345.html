Seedtime and Harvest

Day 345 of 1,095

As long as the earth endures, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night shall never cease.”

Genesis 8:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God did not promise an endless summer. He promised reliable rhythm. Seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter: every season serves the next, and the cycle itself is mercy.

For women

A woman's hope often lives or dies by whether she believes the seasons will turn. Genesis 8:22 is God's promise that they will. The winter is not the end of the story; it is the prelude to seedtime.

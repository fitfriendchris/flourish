Family Order

Day 622 of 1,095

Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord. Husbands, love your wives and do not be harsh with them. Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is pleasing to the Lord. Fathers, do not provoke your children, so they will not become discouraged.

Colossians 3:18-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul gives four commands to four groups in the household. Each role is directed toward the Lord, and each command is meant to make the home a place of peace rather than a battlefield.

For women

Paul's household commands address wives, husbands, children, and fathers, but mothers shape the atmosphere in which all four commands are lived out. A woman's submission, love, teaching, and gentleness set the tone of the home.

When Friends Hurt You

Day 248 of 1,095

“I have heard many things like these; miserable comforters are you all.

Job 16:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Job's three friends started well by sitting with him in silence; once they started explaining his suffering, they became miserable comforters.

For women

Job's friends became miserable comforters the moment they valued their theology more than his grief; a true friend lets mystery be mystery.

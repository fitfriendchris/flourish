Suffer Righteousness

For men

Righteous suffering is not a sign of failure; it is the place where a man honors Christ, defends hope with gentleness, and becomes a platform others can stand on.

For women

A woman who suffers for righteousness with a holy heart and a gentle word becomes a living defense of the hope that other people need to see.

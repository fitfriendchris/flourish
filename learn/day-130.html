Tracking Without Worshipping

For men

The spreadsheet will not save you, but ignoring it will not either. Tracking is diligence, not devotion. Take this truth into today’s decisions.

For women

A budget you do not review is a promise you do not keep. A budget you worship is a master that steals your joy. Track it, then release it to God. Take this truth into today’s decisions.

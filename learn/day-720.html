Thirsty Come

For men

The last invitation of the Bible is free: thirsty people are invited to the water of life drink freely, and a man who has drunk becomes a voice that invites others.

For women

A woman who has received the water of life freely becomes one of the voices that says 'Come,' drawing thirsty people to Jesus through her words and life.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

The Buried Talent Was the Sin

For men

A man does not increase his master's estate by sitting on it. The servant who buried the talent was not judged for losing money; he was judged for refusing to risk it.

For women

The woman who hides what she has been given is not safer than the one who trades it. Both answer to the same master.

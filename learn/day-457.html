The Faithful Abound

Day 457 of 1,095

A faithful man will abound with blessings, but one eager to be rich will not go unpunished.

Proverbs 28:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The contrast is not rich against poor. It is faithful against hasty — speed is the thing being warned about.

For women

A man of faithfulnesses — the Hebrew is plural. Not one act of integrity but a collection, stacked over years.

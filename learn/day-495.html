Seed and Bread Are Different

Day 495 of 1,095

Now He who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your store of seed and will increase the harvest of your righteousness. You will be enriched in every way to be generous on every occasion, and through us your generosity will produce thanksgiving to God.

2 Corinthians 9:10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God supplies both — bread to eat and seed to sow — and nearly every financial mistake comes from confusing which portion is which.

For women

Bread is to be eaten and seed is to be buried. They come out of the same sack, and telling them apart is most of the discipline.

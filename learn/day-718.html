Grain Dies

For men

A kernel of wheat must die and be buried before it can bear much fruit; a man who wants to bless others must let God bury his self-protective life so that a fruitful one can grow.

For women

The death of self-interest is not the end of a woman's life; it is the doorway to the kind of fruitfulness that feeds the people around her.

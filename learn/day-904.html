If I Have Withheld

Day 904 of 1,095

If I have denied the desires of the poor or allowed the widow’s eyes to fail, if I have eaten my morsel alone, not sharing it with the fatherless—

Job 31:16-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Job's defence lists sins of omission. Eating alone while someone nearby goes hungry is counted as a sin.

For women

A widow's eyes fail from waiting. The sin Job refused was letting someone keep looking for help that never came.

Temple of the Spirit

Day 86 of 1,095

Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore glorify God with your body.

1 Corinthians 6:19-20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

This verse is not about protein shakes. It is about ownership, and the fact that your body now belongs to Someone who paid for it.

For women

Your body is not a thing you own. It is a place where God lives, bought at a price you could not pay.

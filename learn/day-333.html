Sworn to His Own Hurt

For men

A man who keeps his word when it costs him is a man whose promises are worth more than his comfort. Character is built in exactly these unobserved costs.

For women

A woman who keeps her promise when it hurts her is a woman whose love is stronger than her mood, her convenience, or her fear.

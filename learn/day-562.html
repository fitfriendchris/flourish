Be the Another

Day 562 of 1,095

Let another praise you, and not your own mouth—a stranger, and not your own lips.

Proverbs 27:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Turn it round: if another is to do the praising, somebody has to be the another.

For women

A stranger's praise is evidence. A spouse's praise is not evidence — it is covenant. They are different currencies and a marriage needs the second.

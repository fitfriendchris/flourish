Sound Doctrine

For men

Sound doctrine is not a debate trophy; it is a life ordered by truth. Q3 asks whether your beliefs have produced sober minds, dignified speech, and self-controlled relationships.

For women

Sound doctrine must walk. A woman's reverence, self-control, kindness, and speech are the apologetic the watching world cannot argue away.

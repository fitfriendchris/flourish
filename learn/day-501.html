Blessings That Overtake

Day 501 of 1,095

“Now if you faithfully obey the voice of the LORD your God and are careful to follow all His commandments I am giving you today, the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth. And all these blessings will come upon you and overtake you, if you will obey the voice of the LORD your God:

Deuteronomy 28:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The blessings pursue and overtake — it is a hunting verb. But this is Israel's national covenant, and fourteen verses of blessing are followed by fifty-four of curse.

For women

Overtake is a hunting word — the blessings pursue and catch. But the chapter that promises them spends fifty-four verses on the other outcome.

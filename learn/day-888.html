Shut the Door

Day 888 of 1,095

But when you pray, go into your inner room, shut your door, and pray to your Father, who is unseen. And your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

Matthew 6:6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Tameion is the storeroom — the windowless inner room where valuables were kept. Not a prayer closet in a pious sense. The pantry.

For women

Shut the door. Elisha said the same thing to a widow before the oil began to pour.

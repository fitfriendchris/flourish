Life Insurance as Love

For men

Provide is the Greek pronoeo: to think beforehand. Paul does not demand wealth; he demands foresight on behalf of the people under your roof.

For women

Paul's warning reaches every household: if anyone does not provide for her own, she has denied the faith. For many women, provision begins with the foresight that stretches and protects a paycheck.

Wake Sleep

For men

Integration requires wakefulness. A man who is asleep in one ledger will soon lose the others, because the deeds of darkness do not respect boundaries.

For women

A woman's wakefulness often sets the tone of the whole house. She notices when the marriage is drifting, when the children are anxious, when the budget is whispering warnings, and when her own soul has grown dull.

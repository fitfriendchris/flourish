Seed and Bread

Day 842 of 1,095

For just as rain and snow fall from heaven and do not return without watering the earth, making it bud and sprout, and providing seed to sow and food to eat, so My word that proceeds from My mouth will not return to Me empty, but it will accomplish what I please, and it will prosper where I send it.

Isaiah 55:10-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Seed to sow and food to eat. The same grain, and which one it becomes is decided by the person holding it.

For women

An eater is not a lesser category than a sower. The rain comes down for both, and Isaiah does not rank them.

Desire Only God

Day 427 of 1,095

Whom have I in heaven but You? And on earth I desire no one besides You. My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever.

Psalm 73:25-26 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

My flesh and my heart may fail. Asaph says the thing out loud in the middle of a worship song, and the psalm is stronger for it.

For women

She says the failing part out loud, at the settled end of the psalm. The honesty is part of the resolution, not a lapse in it.

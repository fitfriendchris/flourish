FIE: Financial Independence, Eventually

For men

Eventually is a Christian word because it makes room for trust, generosity, and calling along the way instead of postponing everything until a number is reached.

For women

Financial Independence, Eventually, lets a woman build slowly without postponing the life she was called to live today.

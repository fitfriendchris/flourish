FIE: Financial Independence, Eventually

Day 237 of 1,095

Instruct those who are rich in the present age not to be conceited and not to put their hope in the uncertainty of wealth, but in God, who richly provides all things for us to enjoy. Instruct them to do good, to be rich in good works, and to be generous and ready to share, treasuring up for themselves a firm foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of that which is truly life.

1 Timothy 6:17-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Eventually is a Christian word because it makes room for trust, generosity, and calling along the way instead of postponing everything until a number is reached.

For women

Financial Independence, Eventually, lets a woman build slowly without postponing the life she was called to live today.

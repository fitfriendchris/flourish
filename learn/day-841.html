Power to Get

Day 841 of 1,095

You might say in your heart, “The power and strength of my hands have made this wealth for me.” But remember that it is the LORD your God who gives you the power to gain wealth, in order to confirm His covenant that He swore to your fathers even to this day.

Deuteronomy 8:17-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The verse everyone quotes stops one clause early. The purpose of the wealth-getting power is a covenant, and it is named.

For women

You might say in your heart. It is never said out loud, which is why it is never examined.

Think On These

Day 788 of 1,095

Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is right, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is admirable—if anything is excellent or praiseworthy—think on these things. Whatever you have learned or received or heard from me, or seen in me, put it into practice. And the God of peace will be with you.

Philippians 4:8-9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Think on these things is logizesthe — an accounting verb. Paul is telling you which column to enter things in, not what to daydream about.

For women

Read the eight items. Two of them are not moral at all — lovely and commendable are aesthetic and social, and Paul put them on the list anyway.

A Prudent Son

Day 537 of 1,095

Idle hands make one poor, but diligent hands bring wealth. He who gathers in summer is a wise son, but he who sleeps during harvest is a disgraceful son.

Proverbs 10:4-5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Verse five is family language — a prudent son, a son who brings shame. Diligence is not taught by lecture; it is caught by being included in the gathering.

For women

The chapter opens with a wise son and a glad father, and a foolish son and a grieving mother. Verse five is the mother's verse too.

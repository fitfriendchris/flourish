Forgive Because You Have Been Forgiven

Day 263 of 1,095

Then Peter came to Jesus and asked, “Lord, how many times shall I forgive my brother who sins against me? Up to seven times?” Jesus answered, “I tell you, not just seven times, but seventy-seven times! Because of this, the kingdom of heaven is like a king who wanted to settle accounts with his servants.

Matthew 18:21-23 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Peter thought seven times was generous. Jesus answered with a number too large to count and a parable about a debtor who forgot he had been forgiven.

For women

Forgiveness is not earned by the offender; it is released by the one who remembers how much she has been forgiven.

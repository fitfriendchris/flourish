Forgive Because You Have Been Forgiven

For men

Peter thought seven times was generous. Jesus answered with a number too large to count and a parable about a debtor who forgot he had been forgiven.

For women

Forgiveness is not earned by the offender; it is released by the one who remembers how much she has been forgiven.

Guard the Deposit

For men

Guard the deposit guard the treasure entrusted to you, with the help of the Holy Spirit. A Timothy does not guard the truth by his own strength.

For women

He is able to guard what has been entrusted. God guards the deposit even as a woman is asked to guard it.

While He was reclining at the table with them, He took bread, spoke a blessing and broke it, and gave it to them. Then their eyes were opened and they recognized Jesus—and He disappeared from their sight.

Luke 24:30-31 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The two disciples on the road had seven miles of Bible teaching and did not recognise Jesus. They recognised Him at the table.

For women

Their eyes were opened, and He vanished. Some of the best moments at a table are the ones where the teacher is no longer needed.

A Letter of Instruction

Day 230 of 1,095

For I am already being poured out like a drink offering, and the time of my departure is at hand. I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith. From now on there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous Judge, will award to me on that day—and not only to me, but to all who crave His appearing.

2 Timothy 4:6-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul finished his race with a letter. The words he left were worth more than anything in his traveling bag, because they carried his testimony, his charge, and his hope.

For women

A woman's letter of instruction is often the emotional heart of an estate. It carries the stories, apologies, blessings, and explanations the documents cannot hold.

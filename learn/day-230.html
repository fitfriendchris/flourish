A Letter of Instruction

For men

Paul finished his race with a letter. The words he left were worth more than anything in his traveling bag, because they carried his testimony, his charge, and his hope.

For women

A woman's letter of instruction is often the emotional heart of an estate. It carries the stories, apologies, blessings, and explanations the documents cannot hold.

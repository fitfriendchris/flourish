Faith Is the Evidence of Things Not Seen

Day 187 of 1,095

Now faith is the assurance of what we hope for and the certainty of what we do not see.

Hebrews 11:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Every long-term investor lives by faith. He commits real dollars today to a future he cannot see, trusting the evidence of what we hope for.

For women

A woman who plans for a future she cannot yet see is not foolish. She is walking by faith. The long view is a spiritual discipline.

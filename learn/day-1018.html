Examine Yourselves

Day 1018 of 1,095

Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Do you not realize that Jesus Christ is in you—unless you fail the test?

2 Corinthians 13:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The audit has one decisive question: is Jesus Christ in you? Every other measure of three years sits under that one.

For women

Do you not know yourselves? Paul assumes self-knowledge is possible, and a woman can know whether Christ lives in her.

Undivided, Not Flawless

For men

Tom means whole, complete, undivided — not sinless. And the children are blessed after him, walking in a path his feet wore.

For women

One verse earlier: who can say, I have made my heart pure? The chapter rules out claiming purity and still commends walking in integrity.

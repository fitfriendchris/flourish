The One Debt You Keep

Day 498 of 1,095

Be indebted to no one, except to one another in love. For he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law.

Romans 13:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Debt is what turns a bad year into ruin, because obligations do not flex when income does. Paul allows exactly one that is never to be discharged.

For women

Debt does not just add risk. It removes your ability to wait — and waiting is most of what survives a bad year.

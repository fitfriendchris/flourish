Day 646 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 93Calling Confirmed — Gifts, Burdens, and Open Doors

Example

For men

Calling begins before you feel ready. Timothy was young, but Paul did not tell him to wait for credibility. He told him to become an example—first in speech, then conduct, then love, then faith, then purity.

For women

Calling does not wait for perfect conditions. A woman who sets in speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity becomes undeniable in the very spaces where she currently feels overlooked.

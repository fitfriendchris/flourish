Singleness as Prophetic Assignment

Day 276 of 1,095

Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying, “You must not marry or have sons or daughters in this place.”

Jeremiah 16:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God sometimes calls a man to singleness as part of a prophetic assignment. The calling is not easy, but it is not empty either.

For women

Jeremiah was forbidden to marry because his life was meant to be a prophetic sign. Some women are called to singleness for a mission that only a free heart can carry.

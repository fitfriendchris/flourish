Beyond Their Means, of Their Own Accord

For men

No adviser would have approved what the Macedonians did. Paul calls it grace, and the reason it was not recklessness is in the order they did it in.

For women

They gave themselves first to the Lord, and then the money. That order is what makes it grace and not folly.

The Least of These

Day 586 of 1,095

Then the King will say to those on His right, ‘Come, you who are blessed by My Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world. For I was hungry and you gave Me something to eat, I was thirsty and you gave Me something to drink, I was a stranger and you took Me in,

Matthew 25:34-35 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The parent who cannot repay you is the one Jesus asks you to serve as if you were serving him. When the return on your kindness is silence, you have arrived at the place where Jesus measures love.

For women

The hands that feed and bathe a declining parent are the hands that touch Christ. The woman who serves here is building a house that will outlast every loss.

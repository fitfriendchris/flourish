Abundance Grace

Day 399 of 1,095

For if, by the trespass of the one man, death reigned through that one man, how much more will those who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man, Jesus Christ! So then, just as one trespass brought condemnation for all men, so also one act of righteousness brought justification and life for all men.

Romans 5:17-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul does not say life will reign over you. He says you will reign — and the qualification is not effort, it is receiving.

For women

You can work, endure and achieve. You cannot achieve receiving — which is why it is the hardest thing on the list.

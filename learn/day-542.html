Made a Manager Three Times

For men

Potiphar's house, the prison, and Pharaoh's court. Three institutions, the same sentence each time — and Joseph never applied for any of them.

For women

Twice in this chapter the person in charge stops worrying. That is the measure of Joseph — and nobody ever asks who carried his anxiety.

Persuasion Is Not Manipulation

For men

Paul did not manipulate the Corinthians. He persuaded them, and the difference is everything. since we know what it means to fear the Lord is the boundary that keeps your influence clean.

For women

Paul persuades with his conscience open to God and to others. That is the difference between influence and manipulation.

The Strength of a Quiet Spirit

For men

A wife's greatest influence is not her volume; it is her character. Peter shows how respectful, pure conduct can win a husband who does not even believe.

For women

Your beauty does not expire when your skin does. A gentle and quiet spirit is the adornment that outlasts every season.

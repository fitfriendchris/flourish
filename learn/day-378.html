Exhort Daily

Day 378 of 1,095

See to it, brothers, that none of you has a wicked heart of unbelief that turns away from the living God. But exhort one another daily, as long as it is called today, so that none of you may be hardened by sin’s deceitfulness. We have come to share in Christ if we hold firmly to the end the assurance we had at first.

Hebrews 3:12-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

See to it, brothers, that none of you. The watching is assigned outward - somebody else is meant to be looking at you.

For women

The word for exhort and the name Jesus gave the Spirit share a root: called alongside. You are asked to do badly what He does perfectly.

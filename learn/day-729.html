Faithful Creator

Day 729 of 1,095

But if you suffer as a Christian, do not be ashamed, but glorify God that you bear that name. For it is time for judgment to begin with the family of God; and if it begins with us, what will the outcome be for those who disobey the gospel of God? And, “If it is hard for the righteous to be saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?” So then, those who suffer according to God’s will should entrust their souls to their faithful Creator and continue to do good.

1 Peter 4:16-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Entrust your soul to their faithful Creator and keep doing good.

For women

A woman becomes a pillar when she stops being ashamed of suffering for Christ and entrusts her soul to the faithful Creator while she keeps doing good.

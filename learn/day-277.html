Preparing in Singleness

Day 277 of 1,095

One day Ruth’s mother-in-law Naomi said to her, “My daughter, should I not seek a resting place for you, that it may be well with you? Now is not Boaz, with whose servant girls you have been working, a relative of ours? In fact, tonight he is winnowing barley on the threshing floor.

Ruth 3:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Ruth's singleness was not wasted time. It was a season of labor, loyalty, and preparation that made her ready for redemption when the moment came.

For women

Ruth used her single years to become a woman of substance, not a woman of schemes. Preparation, not performance, is the work of singleness.

Preparing in Singleness

For men

Ruth's singleness was not wasted time. It was a season of labor, loyalty, and preparation that made her ready for redemption when the moment came.

For women

Ruth used her single years to become a woman of substance, not a woman of schemes. Preparation, not performance, is the work of singleness.

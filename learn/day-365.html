All Things New

Day 365 of 1,095

And the One seated on the throne said, “Behold, I make all things new.” Then He said, “Write this down, for these words are faithful and true.”

Revelation 21:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man who finishes year one with Jesus does not end a journey. He turns a corner and keeps walking toward the throne that makes all things new.

For women

A woman who finishes year one does not close a book; she turns a page, knowing the Author is make all things new.

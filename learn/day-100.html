You were bought

For men

Your body does not belong to you. That sentence lands as a restriction, but for the Christian it is a liberation: you do not have to optimize, punish, or display a body that was never yours to own.

For women

The culture tells you that your body is the one thing you fully own and the one place where you get to be completely yourself. Paul says the opposite: it was bought, which means the pressure to turn your body into your masterpiece was never supposed to be there.

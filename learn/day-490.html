Provision Means Thinking Ahead

Day 490 of 1,095

If anyone does not provide for his own, and especially his own household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.

1 Timothy 5:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Provide is a word about foresight, not payment. Paul is writing about who the church supports, and his rule is that families think ahead so the church can catch those with nobody.

For women

Provide means to think beforehand. It is foresight, and Paul treats the lack of it as a failure of faith.

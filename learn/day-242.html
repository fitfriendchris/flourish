Love Like Jesus

For men

The bar for husbands is not 'be a nice guy.' It is 'give yourself up for her the way Christ gave himself for the church.' That is the job description.

For women

You were made to be loved into flourishing. A husband's calling is not to rule you but to give himself up for you the way Jesus gave himself for the church.

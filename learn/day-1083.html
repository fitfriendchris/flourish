Our Citizenship

Day 1083 of 1,095

But our citizenship is in heaven, and we eagerly await a Savior from there, the Lord Jesus Christ,

Philippians 3:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Our citizenship is in heaven. Philippi was a Roman colony, and its citizens knew what it meant to live far from the capital.

For women

He will transform our lowly body. The heavenly citizen's hope includes the body, not only the soul.

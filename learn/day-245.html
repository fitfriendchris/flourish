When the Structure Breaks

For men

God's design is permanent, but God also sees brokenness. Jesus holds high the standard and makes room for the wounded.

For women

God's design for marriage is strong, and God's care for the wounded is tender. You do not have to choose between honoring marriage and protecting yourself.

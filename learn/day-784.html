Yet I Will Rejoice

For men

Count the failures. Fig, vine, olive, field, flock, herd — six, and together they are the entire food supply. This is not a bad year.

For women

Look at the line under the last verse. To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. The most desolate declaration in the prophets was set to music and handed to a congregation.

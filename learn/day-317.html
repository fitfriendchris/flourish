Walk Securely

For men

Integrity is not a guarantee of an easy life. It is a guarantee of a stable one. It is the freedom of having no hidden room that could collapse under scrutiny.

For women

A woman who walks in integrity does not live in fear of being exposed because there is no hidden self she is trying to protect.

Love Essential

Day 576 of 1,095

If I speak in the tongues of men and of angels, but have not love, I am only a ringing gong or a clanging cymbal. If I have the gift of prophecy and can fathom all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have absolute faith so as to move mountains, but have not love, I am nothing. If I give all I possess to the poor and exult in the surrender of my body, but have not love, I gain nothing.

1 Corinthians 13:1-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Today Paul turns the same lens from marriage to every Christian household: without love, even the most impressive gifts make only noise.

For women

Today Paul asks the same question of every mother: without love, the most beautiful homemaking is only noise.

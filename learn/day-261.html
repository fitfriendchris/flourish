Go Directly, Then Escalate with Care

Day 261 of 1,095

If your brother sins against you, go and confront him privately. If he listens to you, you have won your brother over. But if he will not listen, take one or two others along, so that ‘every matter may be established by the testimony of two or three witnesses.’ If he refuses to listen to them, tell it to the church. And if he refuses to listen even to the church, regard him as you would a pagan or a tax collector.

Matthew 18:15-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus gives a ladder for handling sin between brothers: private, then witnesses, then the church. The goal is always gaining your brother, not winning the argument.

For women

Jesus protects the dignity of the one who sinned by starting privately, but He protects the community by escalating when repentance is refused. A wise woman follows the ladder without skipping rungs.

A Good Inheritance

Day 352 of 1,095

A good man leaves an inheritance to his children’s children, but the sinner’s wealth is passed to the righteous.

Proverbs 13:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The inheritance a good man leaves is not accidental. It is the overflow of a life aimed at grandchildren.

For women

A good inheritance is more than what a woman leaves behind. It is the kind of woman she becomes while she lives.

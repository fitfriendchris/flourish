Day 639 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 92The Integrated Life — One Lord Over Every Ledger

Wise to Good

For men

Integration demands discernment. Paul wants the Roman church to be sharp toward what is good and untouched by what is evil, because a man who cannot tell the difference will soon let evil into every ledger he keeps.

For women

A woman's discernment is one of the integrating gifts of the household. She often sees first whether a friendship, a purchase, or a habit is good or evil, and her innocence protects everyone under her care.

Ephesians 3:19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul bows his knees, which was not the normal posture. Jews stood to pray. Kneeling is what he does when the request is out of proportion to him.

For women

The word for family in verse fifteen is built out of the word for Father. Paul is making a point about where every household gets its name.

Emotional Purity as Spiritual Discipline

Day 287 of 1,095

But I tell you that anyone who looks at a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

Matthew 5:28 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus raises the standard from external behavior to internal intent. A man who masters his eyes and imagination is a man who can keep a covenant.

For women

Jesus' standard reaches the imagination. A woman who keeps her thoughts, fantasies, and emotional attachments pure is guarding a treasure for the covenant God has prepared.

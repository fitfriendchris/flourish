Day 118 of 1,095

Whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, as for the Lord and not for men,

Colossians 3:23 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Whatever you do covers the spreadsheet and the thing you make at midnight because you cannot stop thinking about it.

For women

Whatever you do is the widest possible phrase. It holds the visible work and the hidden work, the paid work and the unpaid thing you love.

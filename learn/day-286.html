Guard Your Heart

For men

Your heart is the source of your decisions, your loves, and your legacy. Guard it like a city guards its water supply.

For women

A woman's heart is the spring from which her whole life flows. Guarding it is not coldness; it is wisdom that preserves love for its rightful place.

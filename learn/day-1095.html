Surely I Am Coming

For men

The last prayer in the Bible is three words: Come, Lord Jesus. After three years, the course ends where the Book ends — longing for Him.

For women

The last word of the Bible is grace. After every lesson, command, and warning, the final gift is the grace of the Lord Jesus.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

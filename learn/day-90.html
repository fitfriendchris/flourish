Present Your Bodies

For men

Worship is not what you do on Sunday morning. It is what you do with your body on Monday afternoon. It is what you do with hunger, fatigue, desire, strength, and weakness.

For women

Your body is not a support system for your worship. It is the thing you are asked to present as worship. And it is acceptable to God because of Christ, not because you have made it perfect.

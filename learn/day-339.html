Learning Without Compromise

For men

Daniel learned Babylonian wisdom without becoming Babylonian. Excellence and integrity can coexist; Daniel proves it.

For women

A woman can be thoroughly educated and entirely devoted; Daniel proves the two are not enemies. A woman can master the wisdom of her field without adopting its idolatry.

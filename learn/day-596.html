Wounds of a Friend

Day 596 of 1,095

The wounds of a friend are faithful, but the kisses of an enemy are deceitful.

Proverbs 27:6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The curriculum visited this proverb in year one, on day three hundred twenty-three, and watched it at work on day three hundred twenty-eight. Those lessons taught you to tell a faithful wound from a flattering kiss. Year two deepens the same truth by asking what the church does with it: confession, correction, and restoration in a community that has become a clinic for healing.

For women

The curriculum first opened this proverb in year one to teach the difference between a true friend and a flattering enemy. Now year two asks what a church does with that truth: it becomes a place where confession is safe, correction is loving, and restoration is possible.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

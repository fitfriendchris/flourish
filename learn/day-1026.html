One Plants Another Waters

Day 1026 of 1,095

I planted the seed and Apollos watered it, but God made it grow. So neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only God, who makes things grow.

1 Corinthians 3:6-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Neither the planter nor the waterer is anything. Multiplication is threatened most by leaders who think the growth is theirs.

For women

Each will receive wages according to his labour. The waterer's quiet work is counted, even when the planter gets the attention.

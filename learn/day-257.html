Fan the Flame

Day 257 of 1,095

For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God, which is in you through the laying on of my hands. For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control.

2 Timothy 1:6-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul, older and in prison, writes to Timothy to stoke a fire that Timothy has allowed to smolder. Sometimes the younger man has to rekindle the older man’s courage, and sometimes the older man has to remind the younger that the Spirit is still powerful.

For women

Paul, the older mentor, tells Timothy to fan into flame the gift already inside him. A woman can both receive from older mentors and rekindle the fire of those who have grown tired. Mentorship flows both ways.

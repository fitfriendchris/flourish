A Good Man Leaves an Inheritance

For men

The longest horizon in Proverbs is not your own old age. It is the table of your grandchildren. A good man plans for people he may never see.

For women

A good woman plans for the grandchildren she may never meet. Her legacy is not measured by what she kept, but by what she prepared to pass on with love and order.

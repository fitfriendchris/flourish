A generous soul will prosper, and he who refreshes others will himself be refreshed.

Proverbs 11:25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The he who refreshes others will himself be refreshed. It is a farming image, and the point is that the channel is wet as the water passes through.

For women

A channel is wet the whole time the water passes through — not because it kept any, but because it was in the path of something moving.

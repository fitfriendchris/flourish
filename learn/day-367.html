Wait Hope

Day 367 of 1,095

I wait for the LORD; my soul does wait, and in His word I put my hope.

Psalm 130:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The watchman is not hoping the morning comes. He is waiting for something certain, in the dark, before there is any evidence of it.

For women

He repeats it: more than watchmen for the morning, more than watchmen for the morning. Some things are true enough to say twice.

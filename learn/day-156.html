Tentmaking

For men

The apostle who carried the gospel across the Roman Empire did not consider making tents beneath him. Paul used his trade to fund his calling, and a man who wants a side income can learn from that kind of humility.

For women

Priscilla is named alongside Aquila in the tentmaking business. A woman's skill is not a backup plan; it can be the very means that shelters the mission God has given her household.

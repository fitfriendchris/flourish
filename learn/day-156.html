Tentmaking

Day 156 of 1,095

After this, Paul left Athens and went to Corinth. There he found a Jew named Aquila, a native of Pontus, who had recently come from Italy with his wife Priscilla because Claudius had ordered all the Jews to leave Rome. Paul went to visit them, and he stayed and worked with them because they were tentmakers by trade, just as he was.

Acts 18:1-3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The apostle who carried the gospel across the Roman Empire did not consider making tents beneath him. Paul used his trade to fund his calling, and a man who wants a side income can learn from that kind of humility.

For women

Priscilla is named alongside Aquila in the tentmaking business. A woman's skill is not a backup plan; it can be the very means that shelters the mission God has given her household.

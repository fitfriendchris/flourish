Six Days You Shall Labor

For men

The fourth commandment does not begin with rest. It begins with labor: six days you shall labor. God put work into the structure of the week before He made the Sabbath holy.

For women

The Sabbath command starts with work. God did not make rest the whole point; He made it the completion of a faithful week.

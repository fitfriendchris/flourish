Day 659 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 94Leading from the Second Chair — Faithfulness Before Title

Press On

For men

The second-chair man presses on. Paul admits he has not arrived, forgets what is behind and strains forward to the goal.

For women

A woman who presses on does not wait for a title before she runs. She forgets what is behind and strains forward because Christ has already made her His own.

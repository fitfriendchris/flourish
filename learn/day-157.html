Seven Streams

Day 157 of 1,095

Divide your portion among seven, or even eight, for you do not know what disaster may befall the land.

Ecclesiastes 11:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

One paycheck is not a safety net; it is a single point of failure. Ecclesiastes tells the wise man to spread his portions because no one can read next year's weather.

For women

A woman who builds only one income stream is betting her security on a boss she cannot control. Wisdom says to send out several portions, because seasons change and wise hands prepare.

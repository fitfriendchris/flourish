The plans of the diligent bring plenty, as surely as haste leads to poverty.

Proverbs 21:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Diligent is a word about sharpness, not effort. The negotiation is decided by preparation done before anyone sits down.

For women

Diligent is a word for a cutting edge, not for plodding. Preparation is what sharpens the ask.

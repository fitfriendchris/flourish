Joy Trials

Day 696 of 1,095

Consider it pure joy, my brothers, when you encounter trials of many kinds, because you know that the testing of your faith develops perseverance. Allow perseverance to finish its work, so that you may be mature and complete, not lacking anything.

James 1:2-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Trials are not interruptions to your stewardship; they are the testing ground that proves whether your faith is real enough to manage time, talent, and treasure under pressure.

For women

A woman's trials are often hidden — the sick child, the tight budget, the silent marriage, the unreturned text. Counting them as joy is not denial; it is choosing to believe God is making her whole while no one watches.

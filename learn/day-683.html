Day 683 of 1,095

I charge you in the presence of God and of Christ Jesus, who will judge the living and the dead, and in view of His appearing and His kingdom: Preach the word; be prepared in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, and encourage with every form of patient instruction.

2 Timothy 4:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The word must be preached whether the moment is easy or hard. A builder keeps speaking truth with patience because lives depend on it.

For women

A woman preaches the word through her life, her home, and her conversations. Patience and teaching make her voice life-giving rather than harsh.

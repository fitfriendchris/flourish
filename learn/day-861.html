Like His Teacher

Day 861 of 1,095

A disciple is not above his teacher, but everyone who is fully trained will be like his teacher.

Luke 6:40 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Katertismenos — fully trained, fully mended, fully equipped. The word is used of setting a broken bone and of repairing nets.

For women

Katertismenos is the word for a bone set and a net mended. Fully trained means put into working order, not filled with information.

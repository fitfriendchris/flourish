Day 702 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 101The Quiet Victories — Faithfulness No One Sees

Steps Established

For men

Most faithfulness is invisible. Today the victory is not that you triumphed publicly but that you kept going privately — and the LORD held your hand.

For women

A woman's quiet victories are rarely applauded. The LORD does not need applause to establish her steps; he needs her hand in his.

Undivided Devotion

For men

Paul does not say marriage is bad. He says it divides attention. The single man has a window of undivided attention that married life cannot replicate.

For women

Paul wants the single woman to be free from divided anxieties so she can give herself fully to the Lord. That focus is a privilege, not a punishment.

Do Not Grow Weary

Day 1033 of 1,095

Let us not grow weary in well-doing, for in due time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.

Galatians 6:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Grow weary can also mean lose heart or turn coward. Finishing strong is partly a matter of courage.

For women

In due season means its own season. Every good work has its proper harvest time, and not all ripen together.

He Had the Right and Waived It

For men

Paul says plainly that he was entitled to be supported and chose not to be, so that they would have something to imitate rather than something to agree with.

For women

Paul does tell them to withdraw from the one who will not work — and then, in the same breath, forbids treating him as an enemy.

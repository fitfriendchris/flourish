Buy and Hold as Faith

For men

Peter defends God's timing against scoffers who thought the delay meant nothing was happening. A buy-and-hold investor needs the same defense against the market's daily scoffers.

For women

A woman's strength is often endurance. She carries pregnancies, raises children, waits in relationships, and outlasts problems. Buy-and-hold investing asks her to bring that same long-suffering to her money.

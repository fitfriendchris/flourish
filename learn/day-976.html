Honor Your Father

Day 976 of 1,095

Honor your father and mother, so that your days may be long in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

Exodus 20:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

To honour is to give weight. The command does not wait for a parent to deserve it, and it comes with a promise.

For women

Father and mother are named together. Honour for a mother carries the same weight, including for a daughter whose mother was hard to love.

Rest and Scale

For men

In week 23 we saw that your skills and calling are the engine of income. Today we add the harder part: God gives the power, and the goal is not your empire but His covenant.

For women

You cannot build a covenant business on a foundation of exhaustion. Rest is not the opposite of growth; it is part of it.

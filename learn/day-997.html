A Mother in Israel

Day 997 of 1,095

Life in the villages ceased; it ended in Israel, until I, Deborah, arose, a mother in Israel.

Judges 5:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Ordinary life stops when no one protects it. The villages of Israel emptied until someone rose to care for them.

For women

A mother in Israel. Deborah described her leadership in maternal terms, not as an imitation of men.

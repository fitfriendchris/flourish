Properly Before Outsiders

Day 480 of 1,095

and to aspire to live quietly, to attend to your own matters, and to work with your own hands, as we instructed you. Then you will behave properly toward outsiders, without being dependent on anyone.

1 Thessalonians 4:11-12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Make it your ambition to be unambitious — Paul's phrase is a deliberate contradiction, and the reason he gives is commercial: outsiders are watching, and you owe no one.

For women

Paul's reason is not private piety. Outsiders are watching, and your solvency is part of what they see.

Day 775 of 1,095Year 3 · Week 111Steadfast Joy — Emotional Maturity in Every Season

Rejoice Pray Thanks

For men

Give thanks IN every circumstance, for this is God’s will for you IN Christ Jesus. The preposition is en, not huper, and the difference between them is the difference between a cruel instruction and a survivable one.

For women

Rejoice always is first and hardest — and this is the same letter that tells them not to grieve as others do who have no hope, rather than not to grieve.

Hosea: Covenant Love Through Betrayal

Day 290 of 1,095

Then the LORD said to me, “Go show love to your wife again, though she is loved by another and is an adulteress. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites, though they turn to other gods and love to offer raisin cakes to idols.”

Hosea 3:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God told Hosea to though she did not deserve it. That is the whole gospel in one sentence. This is the heart of covenant: keeping your promise when the other person has broken theirs.

For women

God's love does not wait for you to become lovable. It pursues you when you are running the other direction.

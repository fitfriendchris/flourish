Patience Proves Character

Day 283 of 1,095

Since Jacob loved Rachel, he answered, “I will serve you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.” Laban replied, “Better that I give her to you than to another. Stay here with me.” So Jacob served seven years for Rachel, yet it seemed but a few days because of his love for her.

Genesis 29:18-20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jacob's seven years were not wasted time. They were the proving ground where desire was tested by labor, deception, and patience before it became covenant.

For women

Rachel was worth fourteen years of Jacob's labor not because she earned it, but because love that has been tested by time is strong enough to become covenant.

I Will Betroth You

Day 930 of 1,095

So I will betroth you to Me forever; I will betroth you in righteousness and justice, in loving devotion and compassion. And I will betroth you in faithfulness, and you will know the LORD.”

Hosea 2:19-20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God pays the bride-price Himself. In Hosea the five gifts that secure the marriage are all given by the husband.

For women

You shall call me My Husband, and no longer My Baal. Hosea's betrothal changes the name a wife uses for her husband.

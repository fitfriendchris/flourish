Because He Said He Would Not Leave

For men

The cure the writer prescribes for money-love is not a discipline. It is a promise, in the most emphatic negative construction in the New Testament.

For women

Money is often not greed. It is fear in a respectable form — and the writer answers it with a promise, not a budget.

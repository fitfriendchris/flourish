A Blessing Instead

Day 982 of 1,095

Do not repay evil with evil or insult with insult, but with blessing, because to this you were called so that you may inherit a blessing.

1 Peter 3:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

In a house of blessing, reviling stops with the person who refuses to return it. Peter says that refusal is a calling.

For women

That you may inherit a blessing. The one who blesses in return for insult is not only giving blessing but inheriting it.

Day 649 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 93Calling Confirmed — Gifts, Burdens, and Open Doors

First Last

For men

John fell as though dead because the glory was overwhelming. Jesus touched him and spoke first: Fear not. Calling is confirmed when the living one, who holds the keys of Death and of Hades, tells you that you do not have to be afraid.

For women

The right hand that holds the keys of Death and of Hades is the same hand that steadies a trembling woman. She does not need to manufacture courage; she needs to remember who has already touched her and what He has already conquered.

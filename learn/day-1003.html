Planted in the House

Day 1003 of 1,095

Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God.

Psalm 92:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Planted means transplanted. The righteous are moved into God's house and stay long enough to bear fruit in old age.

For women

Ever full of sap and green. A woman planted in God's house can flourish in old age, not merely endure it.

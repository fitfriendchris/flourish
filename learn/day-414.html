Call and I Will Answer

Day 414 of 1,095

Call to Me, and I will answer and show you great and unsearchable things you do not know.

Jeremiah 33:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jeremiah is in a courtyard he cannot leave, in a city under siege, when God says call to me. Nothing about the circumstances changes.

For women

He does not offer release. He offers access — things you could not reach from where you are standing.

Always Be Prepared

Day 1007 of 1,095

But in your hearts sanctify Christ as Lord. Always be prepared to give a defense to everyone who asks you the reason for the hope that is in you. But respond with gentleness and respect,

1 Peter 3:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Preparation for giving an answer begins inside. Peter says to honour Christ as holy in your heart before you ever speak.

For women

With gentleness and respect. The answer is not only what is said but how, and a good conscience finishes the defence.

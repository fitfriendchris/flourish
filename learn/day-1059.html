A House Not Made

Day 1059 of 1,095

For we know that if the earthly tent we live in is dismantled, we have a building from God, an eternal house in heaven, not built by human hands.

2 Corinthians 5:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Even the house that outlives you is a tent compared with the house God is building. Legacy matters, but it is not the final dwelling.

For women

We groan, longing to put on our heavenly dwelling. The longing itself is evidence that the home is real.

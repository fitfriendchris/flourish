More Blessed to Give

For men

Greed wants to keep what it has. Generosity wants to bless what it sees. The one that wins in your life is the one you practice when no one is watching.

For women

A woman who opens her hand finds that her heart opens too. Giving is not loss; it is becoming more like the Giver.

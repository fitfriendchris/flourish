God's Discipline, Our Sonship

For men

God's discipline is not punishment for being bad. It is training because you are His son. Earthly fathers are called to image that same fatherly discipline.

For women

God's discipline proves you are His daughter. A mother's discipline should prove the same thing to her children: you are mine, and I am training you for holiness.

A Stranger, Not Your Own Lips

For men

The next verse completes the pair. Do not boast about tomorrow; do not boast about yourself. Excellence gets announced by somebody else or not at all.

For women

Why a stranger? Because praise from someone with a stake in you is real but it is not evidence. And later the chapter calls praise a crucible.

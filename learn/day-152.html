Working for the Lord

Day 152 of 1,095

Whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, as for the Lord and not for men, because you know that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as your reward. It is the Lord Christ you are serving.

Colossians 3:23-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul wrote these words to people who could not resign. That changes what it means to work heartily, because the audience of the work is no longer the one who controls the worker.

For women

When every human door is closed, the command is still open: work from the soul, because the Lord Christ is your real employer.

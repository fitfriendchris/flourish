A Portion to Seven

Day 475 of 1,095

Cast your bread upon the waters, for after many days you will find it again. Divide your portion among seven, or even eight, for you do not know what disaster may befall the land.

Ecclesiastes 11:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Yesterday: sow even though you cannot see. Today: spread it, because you cannot see. Same ignorance, opposite-looking instruction, and both are wisdom.

For women

Seven, or even eight — the Hebrew way of saying more than you think you need, and the reason given is that you cannot see what is coming.

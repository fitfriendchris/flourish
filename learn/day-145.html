Food and Clothing

For men

Jesus places this teaching in the middle of the Sermon on the Mount, not as a side note about relaxation but as the foundation for how His people handle money, worry, and tomorrow.

For women

Jesus does not say food and clothing do not matter. He says your Father knows you need them, and seeking His kingdom first is the only stable way to receive them.

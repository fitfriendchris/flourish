Fifteen-Year vs. Thirty-Year Paths

For men

A fast path and a slow path can both be faithful, but only if you count the full cost before you start building.

For women

A wise woman counts the full cost of her independence plan before she lays the first stone, because an unfinished tower is worse than a slower one.

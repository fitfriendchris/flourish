Lead Like the Servant

For men

Jesus had all authority and used it to pick up a towel. That is the pattern of leadership in marriage: not domination, but the willingness to serve, wash, and lay down status for the one you love.

For women

The world tells you that power means being served. Jesus says power looks like washing feet. A husband who leads like Christ will be the first to bend, not the last.

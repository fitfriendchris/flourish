Day 614 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 88Guarding the Gates — Purity in a Digital Age

Wives Subject

For men

Peter instructs wives to be subject to their own husbands and to win them by respectful, pure conduct. The passage is not a general theory of male superiority. It is a specific word to wives about influence through holiness.

For women

Peter speaks directly to wives about pure and reverent demeanor as a form of influence. The passage honors a woman's moral authority in the home and calls her to use it through holiness rather than manipulation.

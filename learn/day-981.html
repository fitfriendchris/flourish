Rise Before the Grey

Day 981 of 1,095

You are to rise in the presence of the elderly, honor the aged, and fear your God. I am the LORD.

Leviticus 19:32 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Standing up for the elderly is a physical act of honour, and the command ties it directly to the fear of God.

For women

Honour the face. The Hebrew suggests adorning — treating an old person's face as something beautiful.

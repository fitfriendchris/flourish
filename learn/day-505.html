The Measure You Bring to Hearing

For men

Mark attaches the measure saying to listening, not to giving. The vessel you bring to God's word determines how much of it you take away.

For women

In Mark the measure is what you bring to listening. Two people hear the same sermon and take away the size of their own vessel.

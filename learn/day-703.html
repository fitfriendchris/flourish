Day 703 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 101The Quiet Victories — Faithfulness No One Sees

Wise as Serpents

For men

Faithfulness today may have required discernment, not force. You stayed innocent in a conversation where cunning would have been easier — that is a quiet victory.

For women

A woman's quiet faithfulness is often tested in rooms where manipulation is normal. She remains innocent not because she is unaware, but because she has chosen a better way after seeing the whole board.

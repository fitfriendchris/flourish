Near Brokenhearted

Day 777 of 1,095

The LORD is near to the brokenhearted; He saves the contrite in spirit.

Psalm 34:18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Read the superscription. The most quoted comfort psalm in the Psalter was written by a man immediately after the most humiliating hour of his life.

For women

Near is qarob — the word Ruth uses of the kinsman close enough to have the legal right to redeem. Nearness in Hebrew is not only proximity.

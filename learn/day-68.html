Truth Exchange

Day 68 of 1,095

They exchanged the truth of God for a lie, and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is forever worthy of praise! Amen.

Romans 1:25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul's word is exchanged. Nobody loses the truth by accident — it is traded for something that seemed better at the time.

For women

An exchange means you got something. That is why it happened, and why it is hard to reverse.

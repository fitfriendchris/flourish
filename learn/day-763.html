Steadfast Love Never Ceases

Day 763 of 1,095

Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail. They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness! “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in Him.”

Lamentations 3:22-24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Verse 24 is the one that usually gets left off, and it is a land word. Portion is what the tribe that never got any land was given instead.

For women

Chapters one, two and four are alphabet poems of twenty-two verses. Chapter three has sixty-six — the same grief three times over at every letter. He gave his worst pain the most rigid possible form.

Work Before the Fall

Day 149 of 1,095

Then the LORD God took the man and placed him in the Garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it.

Genesis 2:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Before the serpent, before the fall, before the curse made work hard, Adam had a job. Work was not punishment; it was part of being human.

For women

Eden had work before it had a fall. The question is not whether you were made to work, but whether you are tending the garden God actually gave you.

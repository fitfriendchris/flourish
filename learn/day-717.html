Day 717 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 103Built to Bless — Your Growth Is for Others

Destroy Death

For men

Jesus partook of flesh and blood so that he could destroy the power of death and deliver a man from the fear that keeps him from blessing others.

For women

Because Jesus became flesh and blood and defeated death, a woman can be freed from fear and become a steady blessing to everyone around her.

The Patient Spirit

Day 684 of 1,095

The end of a matter is better than the beginning, and a patient spirit is better than a proud one. Do not be quickly provoked in your spirit, for anger settles in the lap of a fool. Do not say, “Why were the old days better than these?” For it is unwise of you to ask about this.

Ecclesiastes 7:8-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Patience outlasts pride, and a builder trusts the end more than the beginning. Do not romanticize the past or rush the future.

For women

A woman often holds the emotional weather of a home. Patience keeps her from storms of anger and nostalgia that would damage the people she loves.

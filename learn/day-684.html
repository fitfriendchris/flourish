The Patient Spirit

For men

Patience outlasts pride, and a builder trusts the end more than the beginning. Do not romanticize the past or rush the future.

For women

A woman often holds the emotional weather of a home. Patience keeps her from storms of anger and nostalgia that would damage the people she loves.

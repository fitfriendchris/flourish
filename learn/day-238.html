Sovereignty as Stewardship: Owning Time for Kingdom Service

Day 238 of 1,095

Remember this: Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously. Each one should give what he has decided in his heart to give, not out of regret or compulsion. For God loves a cheerful giver.

2 Corinthians 9:6-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The end of financial independence is not leisure. The end is being able to give, serve, and obey without money stopping you first.

For women

A woman who owns her time can pour it into people, causes, and callings that no paycheck could buy. A financially independent woman becomes a refuge because she is free to love without counting the cost first.

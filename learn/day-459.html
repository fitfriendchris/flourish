Moth and Rust

Day 459 of 1,095

Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal. But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where moth and rust do not destroy, and where thieves do not break in and steal. For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.

Matthew 6:19-21 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Read the direction of the last line. The heart follows the treasure, not the other way round — which means you can move it.

For women

You do not have to feel differently before you act differently. Move the money and the heart follows it there.

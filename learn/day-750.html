Goes Before

For men

Year three brings new territory. You do not enter it alone. The LORD who has been with you for two years is already ahead of you, and someone else needs to hear that from your mouth.

For women

The next season is not empty of God. He goes before you into the places you have not seen, and another woman needs to hear that promise from someone who believes it.

Genuine Love

For men

Paul's list of commands is a practical portrait of the church. Genuine love is the thread that runs through every instruction, and every instruction requires showing up in the actual lives of other people.

For women

Paul's commands are for every member of the body. Women often carry the emotional work of rejoicing and weeping with others. Romans 12 calls that work genuine love, not mere sentimentality.

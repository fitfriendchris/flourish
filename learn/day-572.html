You Do Not Have Because You Do Not Ask

For men

James finishes the diagnosis with two failures: not asking at all, and asking as a consumer. Both of them run a marriage into quarrels.

For women

The harshest word in the letter is followed by the tenderest: he yearns jealously over the spirit he made to dwell in us. But he gives more grace.

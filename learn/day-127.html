Count the Cost

For men

Jesus does not say “follow your heart.” He says sit down and count the cost before you start building. A budget is not restraint; it is the honesty that makes any good thing possible.

For women

A tower half-built is not a symbol of hope; it is a warning. Jesus asks you to measure your materials before you promise the outcome, because faithfulness finishes what faith starts.

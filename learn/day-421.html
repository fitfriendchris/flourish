Doorkeeper

For men

The sons of Korah were temple gatekeepers. When they write that they would rather be doorkeepers, they are describing their actual job — and calling it than a thousand days elsewhere.

For women

He is not enduring the doorway. He prefers it — and he is comparing it to the best of everything else.

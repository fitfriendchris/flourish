Under-Rower and House Manager

Day 506 of 1,095

So then, men ought to regard us as servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God. Now it is required of stewards that they be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul picks two images for himself: the man on the lower bank of oars who cannot see where the ship is going, and the household manager who runs everything and owns nothing.

For women

A steward ran the whole estate and owned none of it. That combination is the entire job description.

The Fast God Chooses

For men

Isaiah confronts a people who treat fasting as a religious performance. The fast God chooses is something else entirely: loosening oppression, feeding the hungry, sheltering the homeless, and refusing to hide from your own flesh.

For women

Isaiah's word is for the whole community, but women are often the ones who first notice hunger, loneliness, and nakedness in the people around them. The fast God chooses calls that noticing into action.

Provoke One Another

For men

Stir up is paroxysmos — the word for provocation, used elsewhere of a sharp quarrel. And it is preceded by a verb meaning study the person first.

For women

Three let-us statements in order: draw near, hold fast, consider one another. The third does not work without the first.

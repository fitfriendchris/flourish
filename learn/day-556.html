Provoke One Another

Day 556 of 1,095

Therefore, brothers, since we have confidence to enter the Most Holy Place by the blood of Jesus, … let us draw near with a sincere heart in full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled to cleanse us from a guilty conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.

Hebrews 10:19; 10:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Stir up is paroxysmos — the word for provocation, used elsewhere of a sharp quarrel. And it is preceded by a verb meaning study the person first.

For women

Three let-us statements in order: draw near, hold fast, consider one another. The third does not work without the first.

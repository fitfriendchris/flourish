Love Pure Heart

For men

Paul tells Timothy why the church exists: not for controversy, not for status, but for love. That love is not sentimental; it flows from a pure heart, a clear conscience, and a sincere faith.

For women

Paul's charge to Timothy is for the whole church, and women are often the ones who quietly keep love moving in a community. But love that looks pure on the outside can still come from mixed motives.

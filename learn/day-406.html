Fruit Sanctification

Day 406 of 1,095

But now that you have been set free from sin and have become slaves to God, the fruit you reap leads to holiness, and the outcome is eternal life. For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Romans 6:22-23 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul asks a yield question about your worst years, and he assumes there was a yield. Nobody keeps doing something that returns nothing.

For women

Wages is a soldier's word — regular pay, issued on a schedule. That is why the old thing still holds. It is paying you something real.

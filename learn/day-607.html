Day 607 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 87Forgiveness as Warfare — Cutting the Root of Bitterness

Guidance

For men

Bitterness grows in isolation. The proverb says a people falls lack of guidance, and a man trying to forgive alone is a people of one about to fall.

For women

A woman can drown in her own version of a story. The proverb says safety comes from with many counselors, which means forgiveness often begins when you let someone else speak into your pain.

Eternal Life Know

Day 438 of 1,095

Now this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom You have sent.

John 17:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus defines eternal life as knowing rather than lasting. It is a relationship in progress, not a length of time.

For women

You cannot know anyone at speed. Every relationship that went deep went deep in unhurried, unremarkable time.

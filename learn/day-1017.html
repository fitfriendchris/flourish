Lift Up Your Eyes

For men

The disciples said four months until harvest. Jesus said look up, the fields, for they are ripe now — and the Samaritans were walking toward them.

For women

The Samaritans came because a woman told them. The harvest that walked across the field was the fruit of her testimony.

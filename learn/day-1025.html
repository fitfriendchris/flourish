Two by Two by Two

Day 1025 of 1,095

Then Jesus called the Twelve to Him and began to send them out two by two, giving them authority over unclean spirits.

Mark 6:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus sent in pairs from the beginning. Multiplication starts with partnership, not solo heroics.

For women

Priscilla and Aquila worked as a pair. A woman's partnership in mission can be with her husband, a friend, or a sister in Christ.

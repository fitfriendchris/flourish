First Fruits, Not Leftovers

For men

The first check you write is a public statement about who owns the rest of your money. If God is first, the rest of the budget has a chance to fall into proper order.

For women

A woman who gives first is not proving her devotion; she is protecting her heart from the lie that security comes from keeping everything.

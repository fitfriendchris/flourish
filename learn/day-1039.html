Your Timothy

Day 1039 of 1,095

Let no one despise your youth, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Let no one despise your youth is also a word to the older man. A Paul who looks down on his Timothy has already failed him.

For women

So that all may see your progress. A younger woman's growth is meant to be visible, and the older woman can help others notice it.

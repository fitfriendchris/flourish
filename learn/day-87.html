Gnosticism's Shadow

For men

The early church gave a name to the idea that bodies do not matter. The name was heresy. And John gave the church a test: any spirit that refuses to confess Jesus came in the flesh is not from God.

For women

The spirit that says your body does not matter is the same spirit John warned the church against. It has a name, and the name is heresy.

Day 610 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 87Forgiveness as Warfare — Cutting the Root of Bitterness

Finds Wife

For men

A wife is a good thing and a source of favor. Bitterness turns a good thing into a battleground, and forgiveness is how a man protects what God has given him.

For women

A woman often receives the message that her value depends on being found. The proverb says something better: the one who finds her obtains favor from the Lord. Her relationships are gifts, and forgiveness is how she guards the good things God has given her.

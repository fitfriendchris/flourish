A Time for Everything

For men

Ecclesiastes does not promise every season will feel fair. It promises every season is accounted for. A man who accepts his season stops fighting the calendar and starts asking what God is doing in it.

For women

A woman who fights every season becomes exhausted. A woman who reads the season becomes wise. Ecclesiastes gives you permission to stop performing the wrong season and start living the one you are actually in.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

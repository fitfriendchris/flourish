Hidden in the Meal

Day 839 of 1,095

He put before them another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed that a man took and planted in his field. Although it is the smallest of all seeds, yet it grows into the largest of garden plants and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and nest in its branches.” He told them still another parable: “The kingdom of heaven is like leaven that a woman took and mixed into three measures of flour, until all of it was leavened.”

Matthew 13:31-33 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Two parables, one point, opposite directions. The seed grows up where you can see it. The leaven works inward where you cannot.

For women

A woman took leaven and hid it. Enkrypsen — concealed it on purpose, in forty litres of flour, and waited.

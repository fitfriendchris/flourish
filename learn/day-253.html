Four Generations Deep

Day 253 of 1,095

You therefore, my child, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus. And the things that you have heard me say among many witnesses, entrust these to faithful men who will be qualified to teach others as well.

2 Timothy 2:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Mentorship is not a single conversation. It is a chain: Paul taught Timothy, Timothy taught faithful men, and those men taught others. Your life is meant to be a link in that chain.

For women

Faith travels through people. The woman who discipled you, the friend who pointed you to Christ, the older woman who showed you how to love your family — each is a link in the same four-generation chain Paul describes.

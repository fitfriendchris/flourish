Able to Teach Others

Day 990 of 1,095

And the things that you have heard me say among many witnesses, entrust these to faithful men who will be qualified to teach others as well.

2 Timothy 2:2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Faithful comes before able. Paul's criteria for the next teachers put character ahead of skill, and the teaching itself must be public enough to be tested.

For women

Entrust is a banking word. The truth is a deposit, and the next generation is where it must be placed.

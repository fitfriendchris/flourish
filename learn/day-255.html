See What the Leader Cannot See

For men

A real mentor tells you the truth before the crash. Jethro saw Moses burning out and named it. A mentor who only flatters you is not a mentor; he is a fan.

For women

A woman often carries invisible weight — schedules, emotions, relational repairs — until someone names it. A mentor like Jethro sees the load and helps her set it down before she breaks.

Then I Will Teach

Day 812 of 1,095

Restore to me the joy of Your salvation, and sustain me with a willing spirit. Then I will teach transgressors Your ways, and sinners will return to You.

Psalm 51:12-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Then. The teaching is downstream of the joy, not of the knowledge — and David had the knowledge the whole time.

For women

And sinners will return to you. The outcome David expects from his teaching is not about David at all.

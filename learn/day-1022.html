Each Will Bear His Own

Day 1022 of 1,095

Each one should test his own work. Then he will have reason to boast in himself alone, and not in someone else. For each one should carry his own load.

Galatians 6:4-5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man can only audit his own work. Comparison with a neighbour produces pride or despair, and neither is an honest review.

For women

Each will one should carry his own load. The word is a backpack, not a crushing weight. A woman can carry her own pack without carrying everyone's.

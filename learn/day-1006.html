A Chosen Race

Day 1006 of 1,095

But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s own possession, to proclaim the virtues of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light.

1 Peter 2:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation — all for a purpose: to proclaim. Identity is given in order to be declared.

For women

Once you were not a people. The identity Peter describes is built on mercy, and a woman can proclaim from that humility.

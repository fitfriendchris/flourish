Pray All

Day 711 of 1,095

First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgiving be offered for everyone— for kings and all those in authority—so that we may lead tranquil and quiet lives in all godliness and dignity.

1 Timothy 2:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Honest assessment includes your prayer life. If you are not praying for the people and powers around you, you are not yet treating the weak pillar with the first tool God gave.

For women

Honest assessment must include prayer. If you are not praying for all people, including the ones you dislike, your weak pillar is being addressed without the first tool God gave.

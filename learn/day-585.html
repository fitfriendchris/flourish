Leave and Cleave

For men

Leaving is not abandonment; cleaving is not exclusion. Honor sits between them. The covenant with your spouse is the gravity well around which every other loyalty orbits.

For women

A wife's first we is her husband; from that secure center she can love both families without being torn apart.

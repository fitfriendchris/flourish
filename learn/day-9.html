Day 9 of 1,095Year 1 · Week 2The Armor of God

Breastplate of Righteousness

For men

The breastplate covered the organs you cannot live without. Righteousness is not decoration; it is what keeps a hit from being fatal.

For women

Righteousness is not about looking good. It is armour over the part of you that cannot take a direct hit.

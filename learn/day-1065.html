A Sabbath Rest Remains

Day 1065 of 1,095

There remains, then, a Sabbath rest for the people of God. For whoever enters God’s rest also rests from his own work, just as God did from His.

Hebrews 4:9-10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Whoever enters God’s rest also rests from his own work, just. The deepest Sabbath is resting from self-justification.

For women

Strive to enter that rest. The paradox is that entering rest requires effort — the effort of trust.

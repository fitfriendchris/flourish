Spirit Guide

Day 435 of 1,095

However, when the Spirit of truth comes, He will guide you into all truth. For He will not speak on His own, but He will speak what He hears, and He will declare to you what is to come.

John 16:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Spirit's title in this verse is a road word — He leads you along a way. Guidance here is a journey, not a verdict.

For women

We want a map because a map can be studied alone. A guide has to be walked with, and has to be asked again.

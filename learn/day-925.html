In the Presence of Enemies

Day 925 of 1,095

You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies. You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows.

Psalm 23:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God sets a table while the enemies are still in the room. Discipleship does not wait for the threat to leave.

For women

You anoint my head with oil. God's honour arrives at the table, in view of those who meant harm.

Day by Day, in Two Places

For men

Luke says day by day twice, and names two settings: the temple courts and homes. The architecture is repetition, alternating between public and domestic.

For women

Devoted is proskartereo — to adhere stubbornly, to persist. And all four things have the definite article: the teaching, the fellowship, the bread, the prayers.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

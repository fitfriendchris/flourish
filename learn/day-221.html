The Land Rests Too

Day 221 of 1,095

Then the LORD said to Moses on Mount Sinai, “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land that I am giving you, the land itself must observe a Sabbath to the LORD. For six years you may sow your field and prune your vineyard and gather its crops. But in the seventh year there shall be a Sabbath of complete rest for the land—a Sabbath to the LORD. You are not to sow your field or prune your vineyard. You are not to reap the aftergrowth of your harvest or gather the grapes of your untended vines. The land must have a year of complete rest. Whatever the land yields during the Sabbath year shall be food for you—for yourself, your manservant and maidservant, the hired hand or foreigner who stays with you, and for your livestock and the wild animals in your land. All its growth may serve as food.

Leviticus 25:1-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Sabbath year turns the soil itself into a teacher: even the ground needs a season when it is not pushed to produce.

For women

In the Sabbath year, the land's rest reorganized the whole household. A woman who manages a home knows that some systems cannot keep producing without a season of repair.

Save Sinners

Day 689 of 1,095

This is a trustworthy saying, worthy of full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the worst. But for this very reason I was shown mercy, so that in me, the worst of sinners, Christ Jesus might display His perfect patience as an example to those who would believe in Him for eternal life.

1 Timothy 1:15-16 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The vision is not renewed by your improvement. It is renewed by the fact that Jesus came into the world to save sinners, and you are still one of them.

For women

The vision is not renewed by pretending you have outgrown your need for mercy. It is renewed by remembering that Jesus came to save sinners, and his patience with you is the example others need.

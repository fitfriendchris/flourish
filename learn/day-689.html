Save Sinners

For men

The vision is not renewed by your improvement. It is renewed by the fact that Jesus came into the world to save sinners, and you are still one of them.

For women

The vision is not renewed by pretending you have outgrown your need for mercy. It is renewed by remembering that Jesus came to save sinners, and his patience with you is the example others need.

Full Price at the Gate

Day 198 of 1,095

So Ephron’s field at Machpelah near Mamre, the cave that was in it, and all the trees within the boundaries of the field were deeded over to Abraham’s possession in the presence of all the Hittites who had come to the gate of his city. After this, Abraham buried his wife Sarah in the cave of the field at Machpelah near Mamre (that is, Hebron) in the land of Canaan. So the field and its cave were deeded by the Hittites to Abraham as a burial site.

Genesis 23:17-20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Abraham refuses a gift. He pays full price at the city gate in front of witnesses because he wants the title clear. The first recorded real estate transaction in the Bible is a lesson in public clarity.

For women

Women are often pressured to keep deals soft and relational. Abraham at the gate says that the kindest thing you can do in a major transaction is make the terms plain while everyone is still smiling.

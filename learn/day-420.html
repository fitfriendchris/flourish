My son, if you accept my words and hide my commandments within you, … then you will discern the fear of the LORD and discover the knowledge of God.

Proverbs 2:1; 2:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Four verses of conditions and then a reversal: after all that digging, Solomon says the LORD gives wisdom. Both halves are true and neither cancels the other.

For women

Six conditions, then a gift. The digging never buys it; it shows that you wanted it enough to be given it.

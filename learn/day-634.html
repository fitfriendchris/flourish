Honor Wives

Day 634 of 1,095

Husbands, in the same way, treat your wives with consideration as a delicate vessel, and with honor as fellow heirs of the gracious gift of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered.

1 Peter 3:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Honor is not romance on demand; it is a man studying his wife as a scholar studies a sacred text, because his prayers ride on how he treats her.

For women

To be understood is not an extra in a marriage; a relationship cannot breathe without it. A wife who is honored flourishes, and a husband who honors her discovers that God answers prayer.

Overseer Noble

For men

Paul calls be an overseer a noble task, and then he surrounds it with a list of character tests. A man who wants to lead the church must first learn to lead himself, his home, and his temper.

For women

Paul writes about overseers, but the whole congregation has a stake in the character of its leaders. Women are often the first to see whether a leader is gentle at home, greedy in secret, or quarrelsome in conflict.

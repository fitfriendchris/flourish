The Threefold Cord

For men

Solomon argues that isolation is a poverty, not a freedom. Two are better than one because companionship multiplies reward, recovers failure, and provides warmth. The final image points beyond any human pair to a cord of three strands.

For women

Solomon says two are better than one, and cord of three strands is not quickly broken. A woman needs real companionship too, and the strongest form of it includes God as the third strand.

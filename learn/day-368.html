The Fear of Man

Day 368 of 1,095

The fear of man is a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is set securely on high.

Proverbs 29:25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A snare is baited. Nobody walks into one because they were forced — they walked in because something in it was wanted.

For women

You will not feel afraid. You will feel reasonable, agreeable, and easy to be around — and something will be quietly deciding what you say.

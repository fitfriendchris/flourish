God's will, not a formula

For men

You have probably opened the Bible hoping for a verse that picks your job, your city, or your spouse. Paul has something more useful in mind: a renewed mind that can see clearly.

For women

You may have learned to look for God's will as if it were a hidden code: the right verse, the right feeling, the right open door. Paul says the starting place is much closer to home: your own mind, renewed by mercy.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

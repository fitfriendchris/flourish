Increase Abound

For men

Paul prays that the Thessalonians' love would increase and abound. This is not a modest improvement. It is a request for overflowing, excessive, visible love that establishes hearts in holiness before the coming of the Lord.

For women

Paul's prayer for increasing love lands with special force on women, because women often set the emotional ceiling of a home or community. A woman's love can either shrink to her own circle or abound for all.

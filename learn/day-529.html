You Had No Opportunity

For men

Paul thanks them for a gift and immediately protects them from feeling accused. Contentment is what lets a worker receive help without making anyone feel guilty.

For women

He says he has learned contentment and then, in the very next line, calls their gift kind. Contentment that cannot receive anything is a wall, not a virtue.

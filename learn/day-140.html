Wise Store Up

For men

Getting out of debt is only half the war. Staying out means becoming the kind of man who stores up instead of devours. Proverbs 21:20 is about the other side of freedom.

For women

A woman can pay off debt and still live with a famine mindset, or she can store up with wisdom and rest. Proverbs 21:20 invites her to build a dwelling that has treasure, oil, and peace inside it.

Rejoice in Her

Day 928 of 1,095

May your fountain be blessed, and may you rejoice in the wife of your youth: A loving doe, a graceful fawn—may her breasts satisfy you always; may you be captivated by her love forever.

Proverbs 5:18-19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man will be intoxicated by something. Solomon uses the same verb for delight in a wife and for being led astray, and asks him to choose.

For women

At all times. The command to delight has no expiry date, and it was written knowing that bodies change.

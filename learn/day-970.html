Pay Careful Attention

For men

Pay attention to yourselves first, then to the flock. A guard who is careless with his own soul cannot protect anyone else.

For women

The Holy Spirit made you overseers. A woman's sphere of care — her home, her group, her friendships — is also a flock entrusted to her.

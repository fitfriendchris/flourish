Toil Adds Nothing to It

Day 523 of 1,095

The blessing of the LORD enriches, and He adds no sorrow to it.

Proverbs 10:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The second clause can also be read: and toil adds nothing to it. Both readings are ancient, and together they change what giving actually costs you.

For women

'Etseb is the Eden word. In Genesis three it is used of the woman's pain — and this proverb says God's blessing does not carry it.

The Verse Is About Confrontation

For men

Two or three gathered is not a promise about small prayer meetings. Read the eight verses before it: it is attached to going to someone who has wronged you.

For women

Peter asks how often to forgive in the very next breath. Matthew 18 requires confrontation and unlimited forgiveness in one chapter.

Two Sentences and a Divided Church

For men

James quotes the actual words. The whole sin of partiality is carried in a seating instruction — eight words, and a church has been split.

For women

Verse five turns it round: God chose the poor to be rich in faith. The assembly's seating plan runs opposite to God's own selection.

Unashamed Delight

For men

The Bible does not blush when it speaks about a husband delighting in his wife's body. Song of Songs treats married desire as a gift from God, not a shameful appetite to be suppressed.

For women

Your body is not a bargaining chip, and it is not dirty. In marriage it is a garden locked for one beloved, and Scripture celebrates the desire that belongs only inside the covenant.

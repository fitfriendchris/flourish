Day 726 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 104Year Two Commencement — The Builder Becomes a Pillar

Rock or Sand

For men

The builder becomes a pillar only when his life is founded on doing the words of Jesus. Hearing is not enough. The commencement test is the storm, and the storm reveals the foundation.

For women

A woman often builds her house with careful attention to how things look. The commencement call is to build on the rock of doing Jesus' words, because the storm will test the foundation, not the facade.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

Not By But For

Day 817 of 1,095

For we are God’s workmanship, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance as our way of life.

Ephesians 2:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The verse opens with gar. It is the ground of the two verses that just abolished works, and the preposition changes from by to for.

For women

Paul is writing to people he has just described as having no hope and without God in the world. The workmanship claim lands on the ones with no pedigree.

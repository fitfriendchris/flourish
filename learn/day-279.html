Contentment Now, Not Later

Day 279 of 1,095

I am not saying this out of need, for I have learned to be content regardless of my circumstances. I know how to live humbly, and I know how to abound. In any and every situation I have learned the secret of being filled and being hungry, of having plenty and having need. I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength.

Philippians 4:11-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul learned contentment; he did not wait for his circumstances to provide it. A single man who learns this now will bring a gift, not a burden, into any future marriage.

For women

Paul learned to be content before his circumstances changed. A woman who learns that secret is free to wait wisely instead of settling anxiously.

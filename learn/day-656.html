All Things Possible

Day 656 of 1,095

Jesus looked at them and said, “With man this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.”

Matthew 19:26 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The second-chair man must believe that promotion is not impossible, and that waiting is not wasted, because with God all things are possible.

For women

A woman who believes that with God all things are possible stops trying to force doors open and starts knocking with confidence while she keeps serving well.

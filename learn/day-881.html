What Do You Mean

Day 881 of 1,095

When your children ask you, ‘What does this service mean to you?’ you are to reply, ‘It is the Passover sacrifice to the LORD, who passed over the houses of the Israelites in Egypt when He struck down the Egyptians and spared our homes.’” Then the people bowed down and worshiped.

Exodus 12:26-27 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The child asks you are to reply, and the Hebrew is blunter: what is this service TO YOU. The question is aimed at the parent.

For women

Ki, not im. Not IF your children ask — when. The practice was designed to provoke the question.

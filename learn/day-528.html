Whoever Gathered Much Had Nothing Left Over

Day 528 of 1,095

For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though He was rich, yet for your sake He became poor, so that you through His poverty might become rich.

2 Corinthians 8:9 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul reaches for the manna to explain the collection: gathered much, nothing left over; gathered little, no lack. That is what excellent work is for.

For women

Paul had the authority to command this collection and explicitly refuses to use it. He tests love instead of compelling it.

Honesty Is the Best Persuasion

For men

Jesus does not forbid all oaths. He forbids the whole machinery of padding, promising, and swearing that people use to make lies sound trustworthy.

For women

A yes that needs a hundred qualifiers is not really a yes. Jesus calls us to a life where simple words carry real weight.

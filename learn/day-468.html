Gathered by Hand

Day 468 of 1,095

Dishonest wealth will dwindle, but what is earned through hard work will be multiplied.

Proverbs 13:11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The Hebrew says wealth from vapor dwindles, but wealth gathered by hand grows. A negotiated raise is the by-hand kind, and it compounds on every year that follows.

For women

Vapor money vanishes; money gathered by the handful grows. A raise is a handful — and it changes every year that comes after it.

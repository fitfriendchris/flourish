The Tower Becomes a Foreclosure

Day 200 of 1,095

Which of you, wishing to build a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost to see if he has the resources to complete it? Otherwise, if he lays the foundation and is unable to finish the work, everyone who sees it will ridicule him, saying, ‘This man could not finish what he started to build.’

Luke 14:28-30 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Back in week nineteen we used this same passage for budgeting: count the cost before you build. Today the tower is a thirty-year mortgage, and a half-built house is a foreclosure. The same principle scales all the way up.

For women

A woman is often told that a home is love made visible, and it is. But a house payment that swallows the budget is not love. It is a tower that will not be finished, and the unfinished tower harms the very family it was meant to shelter.

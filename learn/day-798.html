Help Comes

Day 798 of 1,095

A song of ascents. I lift up my eyes to the hills. From where does my help come? My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth.

Psalm 121:1-2 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A Song of Ascents, sung walking up to Jerusalem. The hills are the ones on the road, and they were where the bandits and the shrines both were.

For women

The psalm changes voice after verse two. Someone else starts speaking, and everything after that is second person — spoken over the traveller rather than by him.

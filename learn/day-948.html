As for Me and My House

Day 948 of 1,095

But if it is unpleasing in your sight to serve the LORD, then choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve, whether the gods your fathers served beyond the Euphrates, or the gods of the Amorites in whose land you are living. As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD!”

Joshua 24:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A household's direction is set by a decision someone makes out loud. Joshua made his at the end of his life, and made it for his house.

For women

Joshua set up a stone as a witness. A household's decision to serve God can be marked so that it is remembered.

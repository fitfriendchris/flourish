And There Is No Partiality

For men

The passage does not stop at the slave. Two verses on it turns to the master, and the phrase in between cuts both ways: there is no partiality.

For women

In a culture whose household codes addressed only the head of the house, Paul speaks first and directly to the people with the least power.

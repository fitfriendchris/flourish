Dust and Breath

Day 85 of 1,095

Then the LORD God formed man from the dust of the ground and breathed the breath of life into his nostrils, and the man became a living being.

Genesis 2:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Your body is not a rental. God shaped it from dirt and filled it with His own breath, and He has never called that combination anything less than good. It is the soil from which the rest of your life grows.

For women

You are not a soul trapped in a body. You are dust and breath held together by the deliberate hand of God. And that changes how you live in it every single day.

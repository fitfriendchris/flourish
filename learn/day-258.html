When the Chariot Comes

For men

Every mentorship ends. The question is whether you leave with grief and gratitude, or with resentment and dependence. Elisha stayed with Elijah until the chariot came, then picked up the mantle and walked on alone.

For women

Elisha refused to abandon Elijah, yet when the time came elisha also picked up the mantle and moved forward. A woman must know how to stay faithfully and how to leave gracefully, neither clinging nor running away.

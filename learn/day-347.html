Come Away and Rest

Day 347 of 1,095

And He said to them, “Come with Me privately to a solitary place, and let us rest for a while.” For many people were coming and going, and they did not even have time to eat.

Mark 6:31 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Jesus did not call the apostles away from mission because they were lazy. He called them away because they were exhausted. A man who cannot rest is a man who will eventually break, no matter how noble the cause.

For women

A woman often gives away every margin to the needs around her. Jesus sees the coming and going and says, 'Come away.' Rest is not selfish; it is the invitation of a Shepherd who knows His sheep are worn out.

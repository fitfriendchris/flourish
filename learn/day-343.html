Grow in Grace and Knowledge

For men

Growth is not an option; it is the final command of Peter's second letter. Grace is the soil; knowledge is the light; growth is the fruit.

For women

A woman's growth is not measured by perfection but by how deeply she roots herself in grace. A woman's growth is never complete this side of eternity, and grace makes that good news.

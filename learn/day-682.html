Seems Right

Day 682 of 1,095

There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death.

Proverbs 14:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The path that looks smart can end in destruction. A builder tests every road against God's Word, not his own instincts.

For women

A woman is often the first to sense when a path only seems right. Her discernment protects the household from roads that end in death.

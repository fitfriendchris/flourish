Cast Your Bread upon the Waters

Day 185 of 1,095

Cast your bread upon the waters, for after many days you will find it again.

Ecclesiastes 11:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Faith in the market begins with faith in the God who makes the market possible. Casting bread on the waters is an act of trust, not recklessness.

For women

A woman who gives, invests, or sows is not throwing her bread away. She is sending it ahead of her on waters only God can see to the other side.

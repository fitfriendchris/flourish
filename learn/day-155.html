Field First

Day 155 of 1,095

She appraises a field and buys it; from her earnings she plants a vineyard. She girds herself with strength and shows that her arms are strong. She sees that her gain is good, and her lamp is not extinguished at night.

Proverbs 31:16-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Most side incomes fail before they start because a man wants the harvest while skipping the fieldwork. The woman in Proverbs 31 does not stumble upon a vineyard; she considers, buys, plants, strengthens, watches, and waits.

For women

A woman who waits for perfect conditions will never plant. The Proverbs 31 wife shows that a side income begins with consideration, not impulse, and finishes with perseverance, not luck.

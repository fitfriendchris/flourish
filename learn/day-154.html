Ambition Sanctified

For men

Ambition needs direction. Paul does not tell the Thessalonians to stop wanting things; he tells them to want the right things: quiet, responsibility, and independence.

For women

Paul does not crush ambition; he sanctifies it. The goal is not to disappear; it is to become the kind of person whose life needs no explanation.

Day 54 of 1,095

You will keep in perfect peace the steadfast of mind, because he trusts in You.

Isaiah 26:3 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Isaiah gives the cause at the end of the sentence: because he trusts in you. Everything before it depends on that clause.

For women

You cannot stay your mind on someone you do not trust. Focus is downstream of confidence.

Who to ask, who to ignore

Day 81 of 1,095

For lack of guidance, a nation falls, but with many counselors comes deliverance.

Proverbs 11:14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man who makes every decision alone is not brave. He is isolated. The proverb says safety comes from many counselors, not from stubborn independence.

For women

You may be carrying a decision quietly because you are afraid of what people will think, or because the person you would normally ask is too close to the choice. The proverb says safety is in many counselors, not in secrecy.

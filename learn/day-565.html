The Word for Mending Nets

Day 565 of 1,095

Finally, brothers, rejoice! Aim for perfect harmony, encourage one another, be of one mind, live in peace. And the God of love and peace will be with you.

2 Corinthians 13:11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Aim for restoration is katartizo — the verb for mending fishing nets and setting a broken bone. Repair is the ordinary work, not the emergency.

For women

The God of love and peace — the only place He is given that title, and it is assembled out of the very things He has just asked for.

Jesus Withdrew

For men

Luke uses the imperfect tense: Jesus kept withdrawing. Solitude was not a retreat from need; it was the rhythm that made His ministry sustainable.

For women

Jesus did not wait until every crisis was managed before He stepped away. He withdrew in the middle of the need, and the need was still there when He returned.

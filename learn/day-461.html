Not Work Eat

For men

Not willing. Paul's sentence turns on that word, and dropping it turns a rebuke of the idle into a weapon against the unemployed.

For women

It addresses a decision, not a circumstance. It has nothing to say to someone who cannot find work, and it has been used against them anyway.

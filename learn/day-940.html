Outdo One Another

For men

Be devoted to one another in brotherly love. Outdo yourselves in honoring one another honour means leading the way. In a marriage, the one who honours first sets the pace for both.

For women

Philostorgos is family affection. Marriage needs the warmth of close relatives, not only the heat of romance.

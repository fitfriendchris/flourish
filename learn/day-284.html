Desire Without Wisdom Destroys

For men

Samson had every advantage—Nazirite calling, supernatural strength, and divine purpose—but his appetite made every decision for him.

For women

A woman who ignores wisdom because she is flattered by attention is walking the same road Samson walked; attraction without discernment destroys the very life it promises to fulfill.

Built on the Rock

Day 1053 of 1,095

Therefore everyone who hears these words of Mine and acts on them is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. The rain fell, the torrents raged, and the winds blew and beat against that house; yet it did not fall, because its foundation was on the rock.

Matthew 7:24-25 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The same storm hit both houses. What survives is not what avoided the storm but what was built on hearing and doing.

For women

Luke says the wise man dug deep. Foundations are hidden work, and most of the labour is never seen.

Rhythms of Grace

For men

Your body runs on rhythms, not willpower. Ecclesiastes says that pattern is built into creation, not invented by productivity apps.

For women

There is a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing. The same wisdom applies to work and rest: not everything belongs to every season.

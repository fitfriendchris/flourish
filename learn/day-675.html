Making All New

Day 675 of 1,095

And the One seated on the throne said, “Behold, I make all things new.” Then He said, “Write this down, for these words are faithful and true.” And He told me, “It is done! I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. To the thirsty I will give freely from the spring of the water of life. The one who overcomes will inherit all things, and I will be his God, and he will be My son.

Revelation 21:5-7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Failure does not have the last word because the One on the throne is still writing. The long road back is not a repair job; it is an early draft of the new creation.

For women

A woman often bears the weight of broken things — broken bodies, broken budgets, broken bonds. The promise that God is make all things new gives her permission to grieve what was and to hope for what will be.

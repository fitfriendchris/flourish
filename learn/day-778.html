Walk Spirit

Day 778 of 1,095

So I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. For the flesh craves what is contrary to the Spirit, and the Spirit what is contrary to the flesh. They are opposed to each other, so that you do not do what you want. But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.

Galatians 5:16-18 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Walk, and you will not gratify. The first half is the command and the second half is a consequence — Paul never tells you to attack the second half directly.

For women

Verse 17 says the opposition runs both ways. The Spirit also keeps you from doing what you want, which means the conflict is not evidence that something has gone wrong.

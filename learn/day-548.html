Day 548 of 1,095

And we urge you, brothers, to admonish the unruly, encourage the fainthearted, help the weak, and be patient with everyone. Make sure that no one repays evil for evil. Always pursue what is good for one another and for all people.

1 Thessalonians 5:14-15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul gives three different responses to three different people, and only one instruction that applies to everyone. Pressure is when we collapse them into one.

For women

We urge you, brothers — the whole church. Admonishing, encouraging and holding are assigned to the congregation, not to the staff.

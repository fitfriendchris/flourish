A Wise Master Builder

Day 1056 of 1,095

By the grace God has given me, I laid a foundation as an expert builder, and someone else is building on it. But each one must be careful how he builds.

1 Corinthians 3:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul called himself a skilled master builder by grace. The same verse warns every builder to must be careful how he builds.

For women

Someone else is building on it. A foundation layer rarely sees the finished building, and she must lay it anyway.

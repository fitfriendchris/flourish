Persistent Prayer

Day 30 of 1,095

Then Jesus told them a parable about their need to pray at all times and not lose heart:

Luke 18:1 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Luke says why Jesus told the parable before he tells it: so that they would not lose heart. Persistence is a heart problem before it is a discipline problem.

For women

Jesus links praying always with not losing heart, as though they are the same act described twice.

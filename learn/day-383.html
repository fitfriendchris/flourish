Kindness Repentance

For men

Paul aims this at the person doing the judging. The kindness is offered to the moralist, which is a harder thing to receive than mercy offered to the obvious sinner.

For women

Patience is not a verdict. It is time, and Paul says it is being spent on leading you somewhere.

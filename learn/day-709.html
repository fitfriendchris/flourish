No Other Name

Day 709 of 1,095

Salvation exists in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved.”

Acts 4:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Honest assessment begins with the most important question: what are you actually trusting to save you? Name the name, or admit the substitute.

For women

Peter says this to the men who had the power to jail him. The exclusivity of the name is not a debating point here — it is what he says when it costs him.

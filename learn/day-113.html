Very Good

Day 113 of 1,095

And God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.

Genesis 1:31 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Before anything was useful, it was good. Not good for something. Just good — and that verdict includes the things you enjoy.

For women

Very good is a verdict, not a usefulness test. The world was declared beautiful before it was declared useful.

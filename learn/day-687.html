Let Justice Roll

Day 687 of 1,095

But let justice roll on like a river, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.

Amos 5:24 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Justice and righteousness are not occasional events. They must flow like a river through every part of a builder's life.

For women

A woman often creates the current of justice and kindness in her home and community. Righteousness flows through her ordinary choices.

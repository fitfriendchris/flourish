Her Deed Becomes the Brand

For men

The woman with the alabaster jar did not write a mission statement. She acted, and her action became the story that outlasted her.

For women

The woman with the alabaster jar broke something costly and public. Her courage became the brand that Jesus made sure the world would remember.

Aaronic Blessing

For men

You have heard this blessing for 730 days. Year three is the time to speak it over someone else as faithfully as you need it spoken over you. Receive the blessing, then speak it over someone who needs it.

For women

The Aaronic blessing is for you first. Receive it fully, then become the woman who can pass it on. Be a channel of blessing to your household and your friends this week.

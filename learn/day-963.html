He Ran to Him

Day 963 of 1,095

So he got up and went to his father. But while he was still in the distance, his father saw him and was filled with compassion. He ran to his son, embraced him, and kissed him.

Luke 15:20 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The father ran. In that culture a patriarch did not run, and the loss of dignity was part of the welcome.

For women

While he was still in the distance, his father saw him. Seeing from a distance means someone was still looking down the road.

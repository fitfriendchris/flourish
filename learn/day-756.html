Face Like Flint

Day 756 of 1,095

Because the Lord GOD helps Me, I have not been disgraced; therefore I have set My face like flint, and I know that I will not be put to shame.

Isaiah 50:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The resolve is downstream of the help. Read the therefores in order — most of us try to manufacture the flint first.

For women

He offers his cheek and sets his face in the same breath. Hardness of purpose and hardness of heart are not the same thing, and it is very easy to trade one for the other.

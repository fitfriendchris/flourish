Go Home and Tell

Day 1012 of 1,095

But Jesus would not allow him. “Go home to your own people,” He said, “and tell them how much the Lord has done for you, and what mercy He has shown you.”

Mark 5:19 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The healed man asked to go with Jesus and was told to go home. Sometimes the most important mission field is the one you would rather leave.

For women

Go home to your own people,” He said. The Greek says your own people, and a woman's first mission field is often the circle that knows her best.

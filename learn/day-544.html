The Leaf Falls When the Flow Stops

For men

Chapter eleven surrounds this proverb with sayings about circulation — give freely and grow richer, withhold and suffer want. The leaf falls when the flow stops.

For women

The leaf is verse twenty-eight. Two verses later the same chapter has a tree of life — and a tree is what other people live off.

And He said to them, “Watch out! Guard yourselves against every form of greed, for one’s life does not consist in the abundance of his possessions.”

Luke 12:15 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The rich fool's only problem was a good harvest. Read his speech and count how many times he says my — that is the whole diagnosis.

For women

He would not be used as leverage in a family money fight. And covetousness here is pleonexia — more-ness, the wanting itself.

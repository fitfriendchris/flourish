Citizenship Heaven

For men

Your real country is not the one on your passport. That changes how you read every disappointment, every hope, and every plan for next year.

For women

Your real home is not the house, the city, or the country you live in. It is the kingdom whose citizenship you already hold, and whose Savior is coming for you.

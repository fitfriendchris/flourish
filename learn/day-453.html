God Of Hope

Day 453 of 1,095

Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe in Him, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Romans 15:13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God of hope is an unusual title, and Paul uses it at the end of an argument about people who could not agree.

For women

Hope is the last item in the chain and it is an overflow. You do not start by generating it.

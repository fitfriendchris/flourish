Hides His Eyes

Day 497 of 1,095

Whoever gives to the poor will not be in need, but he who hides his eyes will receive many curses.

Proverbs 28:27 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Solomon does not describe a man who refuses. He describes a man who looks away, which is how almost all ungenerosity actually happens.

For women

Almost nobody refuses. They arrange not to see, and it feels like nothing at all.

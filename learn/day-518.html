The Hilarious Giver

For men

The word behind cheerful is hilaros. And bountifully is literally with blessings — the sowing itself is described as a blessing before anything comes back.

For women

Reluctantly is ek lypes — out of grief. And Paul builds an entire logistical system to make sure nobody gives because he is standing there.

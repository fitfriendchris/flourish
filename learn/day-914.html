Like a Nursing Mother

Day 914 of 1,095

On the contrary, we were gentle among you, like a nursing mother caring for her children. We cared so deeply that we were delighted to share with you not only the gospel of God, but our own lives as well. That is how beloved you have become to us.

1 Thessalonians 2:7-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul described his leadership as nursing. Gentleness is not the opposite of strength; it is strength under care.

For women

Affectionately desirous. Paul was not merely responsible for the Thessalonians. He longed for them.

They Shall Build

Day 1058 of 1,095

They will rebuild the ancient ruins; they will restore the places long devastated; they will renew the ruined cities, the desolations of many generations.

Isaiah 61:4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

They shall build — and they are the ones who once mourned. God's rebuilders are people He has first comforted.

For women

Desolations of many generations. Some damage runs through families for decades, and God's builders repair what was passed down.

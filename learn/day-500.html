No One Can, Not No One Should

For men

Jesus states an impossibility, not a rule. The verb is to be a slave to — and a slave, by definition, has exactly one owner.

For women

A hired man can hold two jobs. A slave belongs to one household — and Jesus chose the slavery word deliberately.

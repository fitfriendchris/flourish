Let no unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building up the one in need and bringing grace to those who listen. And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in whom you were sealed for the day of redemption. Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, outcry and slander, along with every form of malice. Be kind and tenderhearted to one another, forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you.

Ephesians 4:29-32 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul lists the weapons bitterness uses — wrath, anger, clamor, slander — and then gives the replacement: kind, tenderhearted, forgiving speech. A man must choose which arsenal he carries today.

For women

Bitterness hardens a woman's heart until her words become weapons. Paul says to put away bitterness and put on kindness, tenderheartedness, and forgiveness — the exact garments a wounded heart resists wearing.

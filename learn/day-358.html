Pressing Toward the Goal

For men

A man finishes a year the same way he runs a race: eyes forward, weight dropped, lungs burning toward the next mark.

For women

A woman who finishes a year well does not stop to count her trophies; she lifts her eyes to the next invitation Jesus is holding out.

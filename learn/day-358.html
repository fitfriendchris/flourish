Pressing Toward the Goal

Day 358 of 1,095

Brothers, I do not consider myself yet to have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: Forgetting what is behind and straining toward what is ahead, I press on toward the goal to win the prize of God’s heavenly calling in Christ Jesus.

Philippians 3:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

A man finishes a year the same way he runs a race: eyes forward, weight dropped, lungs burning toward the next mark.

For women

A woman who finishes a year well does not stop to count her trophies; she lifts her eyes to the next invitation Jesus is holding out.

Ruth and Boaz at the Gate

For men

Boaz did not keep his commitment private. He ratified it in public, before elders and witnesses, because covenant needs accountability.

For women

A covenant needs witnesses. Boaz did not marry Ruth in secret; he stood before elders and asked them to hold him to his promise.

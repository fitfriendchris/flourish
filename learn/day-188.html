Though the Earth Gives Way

For men

Markets crash, companies fail, and portfolios bleed. The man who knows God as refuge does not pretend the earth is stable; he refuses to fear when it gives way.

For women

A woman who has built her security on God can watch the mountains move into the sea and still stand. Her refuge is not her account balance.

Calling Lowly

For men

God builds with the lowly. A builder does not need worldly credentials; he needs a calling from God and the gratitude to obey it.

For women

A woman's calling is often hidden in the ordinary. God chose the lowly, and her faithful, unseen work shames the world's boasting.

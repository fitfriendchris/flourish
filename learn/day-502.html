He Used the Budget to Explain the Cross

For men

We have taken the tower into budgeting three times. Notice which direction Jesus was arguing: He used counting the cost to explain discipleship, because counting was the obvious part.

For women

There are two parables here, not one. The second is about a king who works out he cannot win and sends for terms while the enemy is still far off.

Friend Loves

For men

Fair-weather friends disappear when the season turns. A covenant friend shows up in adversity because that is the reason he was born.

For women

A woman's friendships can become echo chambers or armor. The friend who loves at all times enters the adversity with you, and she is the reason you do not fall alone.

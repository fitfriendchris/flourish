How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to Your word. With all my heart I have sought You; do not let me stray from Your commandments. I have hidden Your word in my heart that I might not sin against You.

Psalm 119:9-11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The long war is fought from memory as much as from momentary insight. A soldier who has to look up every order in the heat of battle is already losing.

For women

A woman in a long war needs Scripture in her heart, not just on her shelf. The temptation will not wait for her to find the right page.

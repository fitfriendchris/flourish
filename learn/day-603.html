Examine Faith

Day 603 of 1,095

Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Do you not realize that Jesus Christ is in you—unless you fail the test?

2 Corinthians 13:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul tells a church that has been grading him to test itself instead. Examine yourselves is the sentence people reach for last and he puts it first.

For women

Year two asks how a woman shows up for others only after she has first been honest with God about her own faith.

Encourage

Day 589 of 1,095

Therefore encourage and build one another up, just as you are already doing.

1 Thessalonians 5:11 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Encouragement is not flattery; it is building a person up with truth and hope. Your children are learning how to treat you by watching how you treat them.

For women

The words you speak over your mother or father become part of the house they live in. The words you speak today become part of tomorrow's family memory.

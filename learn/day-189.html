The Farmer Waits for the Precious Fruit

For men

The farmer does not dig up the seed to see if it is growing. He waits for the early and late rains. A man who invests patiently is doing the same work.

For women

A woman who awaits the precious fruit is not passive. She is steady. She has learned that some returns only come after both the fall and spring rains.

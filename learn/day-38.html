Day 38 of 1,095

Now when Daniel learned that the document had been signed, he went into his house, where the windows of his upper room opened toward Jerusalem, and three times a day he got down on his knees, prayed, and gave thanks to his God, just as he had done before.

Daniel 6:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The most important phrase is the last one: as he had done before. The decree changed nothing because the habit predated it.

For women

Daniel did not decide what to do under pressure. He had decided years earlier, and pressure only revealed it.

A Root, Not The Root

For men

Paul's sentence is more precise than the quotation. the root of all kinds of evil. By craving — and the two verbs describe being led astray by something outside and impaled by your own hand.

For women

Plunge is bythizo — the verb for sinking a ship. Not tripping. Going down with everything aboard.

Her Husband Trusts Her

Day 933 of 1,095

The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he lacks nothing of value. She brings him good and not harm all the days of her life.

Proverbs 31:11-12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Trust is the most valuable thing a husband can give his wife, and the proverb says it produces gain.

For women

She brings him good and not harm all the days of her life. The measure of a long marriage is consistency across decades.

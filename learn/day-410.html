Walk Faith

Day 410 of 1,095

For we walk by faith, not by sight.

2 Corinthians 5:7 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Sight is not only what your eyes report. It includes how the situation currently feels, which is the harder half to walk past.

For women

Faith is not a feeling that replaces the evidence. It is walking while the evidence is still unimpressive.

Rejoicing Is the Harder Half

For men

Suffering together is common. Rejoicing at another's honour is rare, and it fails first in speech — the congratulation with a qualifier attached.

For women

God deliberately over-honours the parts that lack honour. The compensation is designed into the body, not left to chance.

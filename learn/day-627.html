Children Obey

For men

Paul addresses children directly. Obedience is not merely a household rule. It is a commandment with a promise, tied to the way God's people flourish in the land.

For women

Paul speaks to children, and mothers are often the ones who translate this command into daily life. A woman who understands that obedience is worship can teach it without turning the home into a battlefield.

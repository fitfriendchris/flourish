Small Over Years

Day 158 of 1,095

For who has despised the day of small things? But these seven eyes of the LORD, which scan the whole earth, will rejoice when they see the plumb line in the hand of Zerubbabel.”

Zechariah 4:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You want the side income to replace your salary in six months. The exiles needed twenty years to finish the temple. Small things are not the problem; impatience is.

For women

A woman often measures herself against someone else's finished story and despises her own beginning. Zechariah says the small step is not nothing; it is the very thing the eyes of the Lord are watching over.

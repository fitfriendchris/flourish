Shadow Almighty

For men

Dwells is a residence verb, not a visiting verb. Everything the psalm goes on to promise hangs off that one participle.

For women

A shadow needs two things: that the sun is up, and that you are close enough. In that climate it was not a poetic comfort. It was whether you could keep working through the afternoon.

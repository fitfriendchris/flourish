Image Invisible

Day 668 of 1,095

The Son is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation. For in Him all things were created, things in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities. All things were created through Him and for Him. He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together.

Colossians 1:15-17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Christ is the original of every image you project. If your platform does not point back to him, it is a billboard for a counterfeit god.

For women

Every image you project is either a window to Christ or a mirror to yourself. A woman's platform becomes a pulpit when her people see Jesus through her ordinary days.

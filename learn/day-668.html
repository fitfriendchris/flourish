Day 668 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 96Stewarding Influence — Your Platform Is a Pulpit

Image Invisible

For men

Christ is the original of every image you project. If your platform does not point back to him, it is a billboard for a counterfeit god.

For women

Every image you project is either a window to Christ or a mirror to yourself. A woman's platform becomes a pulpit when her people see Jesus through her ordinary days.

The full lesson is in the app. The teaching, three things to do today, a prayer and questions to talk through (Days 1–7 are free; the rest come with membership).

Discipline the body

For men

Paul does not describe a serene spirituality. He says he beats his body into submission - a violent image, because the flesh does not negotiate politely when it has made itself comfortable.

For women

Paul's language sounds harsh to modern ears, but the heart behind it is mercy. He is fighting his body so that his body does not disqualify him from the calling he loves.

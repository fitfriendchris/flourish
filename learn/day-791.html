Fear Prolongs

Day 791 of 1,095

The fear of the LORD prolongs life, but the years of the wicked will be cut short.

Proverbs 10:27 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

This is a proverb, not a promise, and knowing the difference is what keeps it from breaking you the first time a godly man dies at fifty.

For women

The verb is yasiph — adds. And the proverb is describing something that accumulates, which means the unit of measurement is not years so much as days.

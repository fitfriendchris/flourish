Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives; he who seeks finds; and to him who knocks, the door will be opened.

Matthew 7:7-8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Read two verses further and the scene turns out to be a kitchen — a child asking his father for something to eat.

For women

Knocking happens at a door, which means the scene is somebody outside a house wanting in. Read it from the other side of the door for once.

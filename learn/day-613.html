Examine Your Ways

For men

Jeremiah writes from a ruined city, and he does not blame the Babylonians first. He calls the survivors to examine their own ways and to turn back to the LORD. The same discipline is the starting point for purity.

For women

Jeremiah's call to examine our ways is not a call to self-loathing. It is a call to honest inspection so that we can turn back to the LORD. For a woman, this is the starting point of a guarded heart.

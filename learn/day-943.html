I prayed for this boy, and since the LORD has granted me what I asked of Him, I now dedicate the boy to the LORD. For as long as he lives, he is dedicated to the LORD.” So they worshiped the LORD there.

1 Samuel 1:27-28 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The name Samuel carries the word asked. What a parent prays for, a parent has to be willing to give back.

For women

Each year she made him a little robe. Giving a child to God did not end her mothering. It changed its form.

Day 663 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 95The Discipline of Endurance — Year Two Perseverance

Fight Faith

For men

Endurance is not passive waiting; it is active fighting. Year two asks you to pursue, fight, and take hold of eternal life while everything around you suggests you could coast.

For women

Year two requires a fighting faith, not a fragile one. A woman endures by fleeing what destroys her, pursuing what makes her like Christ, and taking hold of eternal life in the daily details.

We Have Obtained an Inheritance

For men

No earthly will can secure what matters most. In Christ, the inheritance is already obtained, and the heir is already safe.

For women

A woman who knows she has already obtained an inheritance in Christ can plan her earthly estate with open hands. She does not clutch what she cannot keep.

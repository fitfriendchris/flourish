Small Beginnings

Day 137 of 1,095

For who has despised the day of small things? But these seven eyes of the LORD, which scan the whole earth, will rejoice when they see the plumb line in the hand of Zerubbabel.”

Zechariah 4:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Your smallest debt payment is not nothing. It is the first stone of a rebuilt house. Zechariah 4:10 was written for people who were embarrassed by how little they had to show.

For women

A woman often feels that small progress does not count because the gap is still so large. Zechariah 4:10 says the eyes of the Lord are on the small thing, and He does not measure progress the way shame does.

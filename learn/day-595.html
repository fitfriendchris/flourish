Remember Leaders

For men

The author tells the church to remember its leaders, examine the results of their lives, and copy their faith. Gratitude is not nostalgia. It is discipleship.

For women

The command to remember is a command to stay connected to the people through whom God has fed you. For women, this includes mothers, mentors, teachers, and older sisters in the faith who have spoken the word of God into ordinary moments.

Day 594 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 85The Healing Fellowship — Confession and Restoration

Children God

For men

John writes to people who feel like outsiders in the world. His answer is not a strategy for fitting in. It is a declaration of adoption. You are not merely tolerated by God; you are his child.

For women

John writes to believers who feel misunderstood by the world. His answer is not a technique for popularity. It is the truth that you already belong to the Father, and that belonging will one day transform you completely.

Arrows in the Warrior’s Hand

For men

Children are not a project, a status symbol, or a burden. They are a heritage and a weapon — arrows placed in the hands of parents who will one day launch them.

For women

You are not only raising children. You are shaping arrows that one day will leave your hand and fight battles you will not see.

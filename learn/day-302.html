Arrows in the Warrior’s Hand

Day 302 of 1,095

Children are indeed a heritage from the LORD, and the fruit of the womb is His reward. Like arrows in the hand of a warrior, so are children born in one’s youth. Blessed is the man whose quiver is full of them. He will not be put to shame when he confronts the enemies at the gate.

Psalm 127:3-5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Children are not a project, a status symbol, or a burden. They are a heritage and a weapon — arrows placed in the hands of parents who will one day launch them.

For women

You are not only raising children. You are shaping arrows that one day will leave your hand and fight battles you will not see.

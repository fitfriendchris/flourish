The Wife of Your Covenant

Day 288 of 1,095

Yet you ask, “Why?” It is because the LORD has been a witness between you and the wife of your youth, against whom you have broken faith, though she is your companion and your wife by covenant.

Malachi 2:14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God was a witness at your wedding. That means marriage is not a private arrangement you can dissolve when it becomes inconvenient.

For women

God does not see a wedding as a social event. He sees a covenant, and He stands as witness to every promise spoken there.

Living Stones

Day 1055 of 1,095

you also, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house to be a holy priesthood, offering spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.

1 Peter 2:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

You are being built. The passive verb means the building is God's work, and the stones are living.

For women

A holy priesthood offering spiritual sacrifices. Every living stone is also a priest, and a woman's offerings are part of the house.

Built, Established, Filled

Day 563 of 1,095

By wisdom a house is built and by understanding it is established; through knowledge its rooms are filled with every precious and beautiful treasure.

Proverbs 24:3-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Three verbs and three faculties: wisdom builds, understanding makes firm, knowledge fills the rooms. Most couples stop after the first.

For women

The house-building passage runs straight into war and adversity. It is filled because of what is coming, not in spite of it.

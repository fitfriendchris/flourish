The Secret

For men

Paul did not write this from a book tour. He wrote it from prison, and the secret he learned was not how to get more, but how to want rightly in plenty and hunger alike.

For women

The same verse that gets printed on coffee mugs was written by a prisoner. The secret Paul learned was not how to achieve more, but how to be at peace with less or with much.

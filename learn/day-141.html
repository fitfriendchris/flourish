The Secret

Day 141 of 1,095

I am not saying this out of need, for I have learned to be content regardless of my circumstances. I know how to live humbly, and I know how to abound. In any and every situation I have learned the secret of being filled and being hungry, of having plenty and having need. I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength.

Philippians 4:11-13 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Paul did not write this from a book tour. He wrote it from prison, and the secret he learned was not how to get more, but how to want rightly in plenty and hunger alike.

For women

The same verse that gets printed on coffee mugs was written by a prisoner. The secret Paul learned was not how to achieve more, but how to be at peace with less or with much.

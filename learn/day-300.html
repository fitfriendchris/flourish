Enjoy This Life

For men

Ecclesiastes calls your wife your portion in life and in your labor. The grind will not remember your name, but the woman beside you will, and the shared life is the reward God gives under the sun.

For women

Enjoying life with the husband you love is not frivolous. It is the portion God has given you in the middle of work, loss, and ordinary days that will never come again.

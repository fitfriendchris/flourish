Savage Wolves

Day 969 of 1,095

I know that after my departure, savage wolves will come in among you and will not spare the flock. Even from your own number, men will rise up and distort the truth to draw away disciples after them.

Acts 20:29-30 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The most dangerous wolves come from inside. The tell is that they draw disciples after themselves rather than after Christ.

For women

Paul warned with tears for three years. Guarding a community is not suspicion; it is grief that refuses to stay silent.

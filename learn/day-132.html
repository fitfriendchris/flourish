Annual Review

For men

A budget that is never reviewed becomes a mirror no one looks into. Set a date, gather the numbers, and turn back to the LORD with what you see. Take this truth into today’s decisions.

For women

An annual review is not a punishment; it is the discipline of return. The woman who examines her ways is the woman who can walk away from what does not belong in her future.

Fiery Trial

Day 645 of 1,095

Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery trial that has come upon you, as though something strange were happening to you. But rejoice that you share in the sufferings of Christ, so that you may be overjoyed at the revelation of His glory. If you are insulted for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests on you.

1 Peter 4:12-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Integration is tested by fire. A man who is one person under one Lord will eventually face pressure, and that pressure is not strange — it is the test that proves the integration is real.

For women

A woman's fiery trial often burns in the places she loves most — the marriage, the child, the body, the reputation. Peter calls her beloved and says the fire is a test that reveals what is real.

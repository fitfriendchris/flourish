When You Have Turned

Day 811 of 1,095

Simon, Simon, Satan has asked to sift all of you like wheat. But I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith will not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.”

Luke 22:31-32 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Satan asked for all of them. Jesus prayed for one. The pronouns change in the middle of the sentence and the English cannot show it.

For women

Sterison — strengthen, make firm. It is the word Peter reaches for in his own letter, decades later, about the God who does it.

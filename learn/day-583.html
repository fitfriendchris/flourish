Imitators God

For men

Imitation is the highest honor an adult child can give—not flattery, but copying the Father who loved first.

For women

A daughter imitates the Father by walking in love toward the mother who may never say thank you. Your words and your presence carry the fragrance of Christ when nothing else can.

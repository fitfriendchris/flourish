And the streets of the city will be filled with boys and girls playing there.”

Zechariah 8:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The prophet's picture of a redeemed city is not bigger buildings or better politics. It is children playing safely in the streets.

For women

Children playing in the streets is a prophecy, not a sentimental extra. It means safety, peace and enough margin for joy.

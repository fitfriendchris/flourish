Power Witnesses

Day 692 of 1,095

But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.”

Acts 1:8 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The vision is not renewed by finding the right location. It is renewed by receiving the right power and becoming a witness wherever you already are.

For women

The vision is not renewed by moving to a better place. It is renewed by being filled with power and becoming a witness right where you are.

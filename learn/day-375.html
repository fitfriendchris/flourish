Day 375 of 1,095Year 2 · Week 54The Furnace of Suffering — Refined, Not Consumed

Confess Forgive

For men

Confess is homologeo - to say the same thing. Not to grovel, not to explain. To agree.

For women

Faithful and just. Not faithful and merciful - just. Forgiveness is described here as God being right, not God being soft.

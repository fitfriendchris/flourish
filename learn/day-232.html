What Financial Independence Actually Means

Day 232 of 1,095

Instruct those who are rich in the present age not to be conceited and not to put their hope in the uncertainty of wealth, but in God, who richly provides all things for us to enjoy.

1 Timothy 6:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Financial independence is not early retirement; it is the practical ability to choose your work instead of being owned by the paycheck that pays your bills.

For women

Financial independence gives a woman the practical freedom to say yes to her calling and no to pressure that does not fit the life God has given her.

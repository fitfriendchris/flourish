Strength and Grey Hair

Day 893 of 1,095

The glory of young men is their strength, and gray hair is the splendor of the old.

Proverbs 20:29 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Two halves, two currencies, and Solomon does not rank them. He says what each one has.

For women

Grey hair is not compensation for lost strength. It is a second asset, and it arrives without being noticed.

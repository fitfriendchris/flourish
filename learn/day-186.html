Give a Portion to Seven

For men

Diversification is not modern theory. Solomon commanded it: spread what you have across seven or eight ventures because you do not know which day will bring disaster.

For women

A woman who gives divide your portion among seven is not scattered. She is wise. She knows the future is hidden and refuses to bet everything on one hope.

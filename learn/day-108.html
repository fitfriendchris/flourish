Jesus Sleeps in Storms

For men

The disciples panicked because Jesus was sleeping in a real storm. His sleep was the lesson: trust is an embodied practice.

For women

The cushion is the detail that stings. Jesus was not just resting; He was resting well enough to sleep through chaos.

The Crown of Glory

Day 1077 of 1,095

And when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the crown of glory that will never fade away.

1 Peter 5:4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The crown of glory comes when the Chief Shepherd appears. Every under-shepherd answers to Him.

For women

Crown of glory for shepherds. Women who shepherd children, groups, and friends are promised it too.

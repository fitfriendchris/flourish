Anxious Is a Tell

Day 466 of 1,095

Then Jesus said to His disciples, “Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will wear.

Luke 12:22 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Anxiety changes what you accept. Jesus attacks it at the root — not with technique, but with the fact that your Father already knows what you need.

For women

He does not tell them to calm down. He tells them what is true — your Father knows — and lets the fear die of accuracy.

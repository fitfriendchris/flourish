Till I Die I Will Not Put Away My Integrity

Day 321 of 1,095

I will never say that you are right; I will maintain my integrity until I die.

Job 27:5 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Job's friends accused him of hidden sin. Job refused to agree with a lie just to end his suffering. Integrity under fire is refusing to let pain, pressure, or popularity buy your conscience.

For women

A woman of integrity does not let suffering rewrite the truth about who she is in God. It is the quiet courage to remain the same woman in suffering that you were in strength.

The Jubilee Reset

Day 122 of 1,095

So you are to consecrate the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty in the land for all its inhabitants. It shall be your Jubilee, when each of you is to return to his property and to his clan.

Leviticus 25:10 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

God built a reset button into the economy. Every fifty years, no family was allowed to stay ruined forever.

For women

God did not leave mercy to chance. He scheduled it. Every fifty years, the trumpet announced that no family would be ruined forever.

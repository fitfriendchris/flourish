Nourishes and Cherishes

Day 932 of 1,095

In the same way, husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself. Indeed, no one ever hated his own body, but he nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ does the church.

Ephesians 5:28-29 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Nourish and cherish are the words for raising a child and warming a nest. Paul asks husbands to do both for a lifetime.

For women

He who loves his wife loves himself. A wife can let herself be cared for without guilt, because her flourishing is his.

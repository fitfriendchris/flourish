Prosper Be Healthy

Day 719 of 1,095

Beloved, I pray that in every way you may prosper and enjoy good health, as your soul also prospers. For I was overjoyed when the brothers came and testified about your devotion to the truth, in which you continue to walk. I have no greater joy than to hear that my children are walking in the truth.

3 John 2-4 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

John prays for Gaius's total flourishing — body, soul, and circumstances — because a man built to bless must walk in the truth with his whole life, not just his religious words.

For women

A woman who walks in the truth with body, soul, and life aligned becomes a stable platform for the people God has placed under her care.

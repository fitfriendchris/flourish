What Enough Looks Like

For men

Paul draws the line at food and clothing. Not because those are the only goods, but because contentment lives at the level of need, and everything above that is grace.

For women

Paul says food and clothing are the baseline of enough. A woman who cannot rest until every desire is met is not living below her means; she is living above her soul.

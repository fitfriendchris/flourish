The prudent see danger and take cover, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty.

Proverbs 27:12 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

The danger a business actually walks into is usually ethical, and the prudent man builds a place to hide before he needs it.

For women

The simple is not the fool — he is the one who has decided nothing in advance and meets pressure with an open mind.

Avalanche Math

For men

The avalanche method will save you the most money. That is what the math says. Proverbs 14:8 says the prudent man knows his own way well enough to decide whether the math is the only thing that matters.

For women

A woman is often told to be sensible, but Proverbs 14:8 says wisdom is more than sensible — it is self-aware. The right debt method is the one that fits your real life, not the life a financial influencer assumes you have.

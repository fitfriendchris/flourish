What God Has Joined

Day 294 of 1,095

So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let man not separate.”

Matthew 19:6 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Marriage is not a human invention. It is a divine joining, and no human court has the authority to unjoin what God has joined.

For women

The permanence of marriage is not a prison sentence. It is the security that makes deep trust possible.

Ask, Serve, Stay

For men

Elisha did not wait for a mentorship application to open. He was plowing when Elijah threw the cloak, and he responded by leaving everything and serving. A man who wants a mentor must move from admiration to action.

For women

Elisha left a secure life to follow Elijah. A woman who wants to grow must also be willing to leave the familiar — her own assumptions, her isolation, her pride — and place herself under the influence of someone further along in faith.

Day 455 of 1,095

But Moses told the people, “Do not be afraid. Stand firm and you will see the LORD’s salvation, which He will accomplish for you today; for the Egyptians you see today, you will never see again. The LORD will fight for you; you need only to be still.”

Exodus 14:13-14 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Stand firm, God says in verse fourteen. Go forward, He says in verse fifteen. Both are the instruction.

For women

You have only to be silent may be the hardest sentence in the passage.

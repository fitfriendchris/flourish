Word Truth

Day 407 of 1,095

Sanctify them by the truth; Your word is truth.

John 17:17 · Berean Standard Bible

For men

Year one taught you that your identity is spoken by God. Year two takes that truth into the parts of your mind that still believe another story. Today the prayer of Jesus becomes the scalpel: sanctify them by the truth; Your word is truth.

For women

Year one taught you that your name is spoken by God. Year two asks what happens when the old names still echo in your mind. Today Jesus prays that truth would sanctify you, separating the wound from the woman.

Refund & Return Policy

Effective: 29 August 2026

If Flourish is not right for you, we would rather give your money back than keep it.

Bought inside the iPhone app? Apple processes those payments, so Apple issues the refund. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot reverse an Apple charge ourselves, but write to us and we will help you through it.

Membership (bought on the web)

  • 14 days, no questions asked, on your first subscription charge.
  • Cancel any time from Me → Membership. You keep access to the end of the period you paid for.
  • Renewal charges are not normally refunded, but tell us if a renewal caught you by surprise — we will look at it.
  • Lifetime is refundable within 30 days of purchase.

Physical items

  • 30 days to return unused items in their original condition. Return shipping is yours.
  • Damaged, defective or wrong item: free replacement or a full refund including shipping. Send a photo and we will sort it out.

Digital downloads

14 days, no questions asked.

Gifts to the ministry

Gifts are final once processed. If you gave by mistake or entered the wrong amount, contact us within 48 hours and we will refund it.

How to ask

Email support@flourishapp.online with your order number or Stripe receipt. Refunds return to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days once issued.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 August 2026 · Applies to: the Flourish web app and the Flourish iOS app

Flourish is a daily devotional and life-training app. This policy explains exactly what we hold, why, and how you get it back or get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. Your reading progress lives on your device and only reaches us if you sign in. If you join a church inside Flourish, post a prayer request, RSVP to an event or message a pastor, that is held on our servers too — and some of it is deeply personal, so section 2 explains exactly how it is treated. Payment details never touch our servers. There are no advertising trackers, no data brokers, and nothing is sold — ever.

2. The personal parts, and how they are treated

Flourish is a church app, so some of what it holds is what European law calls special category data (GDPR Article 9) and Apple calls sensitive information:

  • Your religious affiliation — belonging to a church, checking in to a service, joining a ministry.
  • What is in a prayer request — people write about illness, bereavement, money trouble, addiction and family breakdown. We do not ask for any of that, and we do not analyse it, but it is what people write and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.
  • Pastoral messages, for the same reason.

We process this only because you chose to give it — you joined a church, you wrote the request, you sent the message (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a), explicit consent). You can withdraw by deleting the content or your account, and it is deleted for real: see section 6.

  • A prayer request marked private is visible only to you and your church's leaders. Marked pastoral only, only to the pastoral team. Only church-wide ones appear on the wall.
  • An anonymous request hides your name from other members. It is still linked to your account in our database, so that you can delete it — a truly unlinked post is one you could never take back.
  • None of it is used for advertising, sold, shared with another church, or used to train any model.
  • We never write a prayer request, a message or a church name into our server logs or crash reports.

3. What we never collect

  • No card numbers. Stripe and Apple handle payment; we receive only a confirmation and the last four digits on a receipt.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no data sale or sharing for advertising. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track you.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Flourish never asks your device for any of them.
  • No health data collected as health data — we have no health fields, ask no health questions, and run no analysis over what you write. If you choose to write about your health in a prayer request, that is your words in your post, treated under section 2.
  • No third-party analytics pixels.

4. Children

Flourish is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content, but it is designed for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Families are welcome to read together under a parent's account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to privacy@flourishapp.online and we will delete it promptly.

5. Payments

Card processing is performed by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1) on the web, and by Apple for purchases made inside the iOS app. Their handling of your payment data is governed by their own privacy policies. We receive only what we need to fulfil the order.

6. Your rights, and how to actually use them

Under the GDPR (EU/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California) and similar laws elsewhere, you may access, correct, export, delete, or restrict our use of your data, and you will never be treated differently for asking.

  • In the app: open Me → Privacy & data to download everything we hold or delete your account outright. Deletion is immediate and does not require emailing anyone.
  • By email: privacy@flourishapp.online. We reply within 30 days.

Deleting your account erases your identity from our records. Completed order and gift records are anonymised rather than destroyed, because financial records must be retained for tax purposes — after deletion they can no longer be connected to you.

7. How long we keep things

  • On-device progress: until you delete the app or clear it.
  • Account and membership: until you delete your account.
  • Financial records: 7 years, anonymised after account deletion, as tax law requires.
  • Prayer requests, messages and church membership: until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account removes them from the church wall too — a deletion that leaves your prayer request in front of your congregation is not a deletion.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Crash reports: until the fault is fixed, then cleared. They hold no account id or email.

8. Security, and what the crash reporter refuses to send

Everything travels over TLS. Sign-in uses one-time email links, so there is no password to be stolen or reused. Server credentials are held as environment variables and are never shipped to your browser or bundled into the app; a build check refuses to ship a bundle containing one. The crash reporter sends the error and the screen name only: it deliberately omits your account id, your email, and the part of the address after the #, because that is where church and prayer identifiers live. The server strips anything token-shaped again before storing it.

9. International transfers

We operate from the United States, and our processors (Supabase, Stripe, Railway, Apple) may process data in the US and EU under Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Scripture

Scripture quotations are from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which has been dedicated to the public domain.

11. Changes and contact

Material changes are announced in the app 14 days before they take effect. Questions: privacy@flourishapp.online.

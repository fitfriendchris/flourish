Quick answers

Restore a membership

In the app, open Me → Account → Sign in / restore purchase, or tap Restore a purchase on the membership sheet. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or the same email (web) you bought with.

Cancel or change a membership

Bought in the iPhone app: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Flourish; Apple manages these. Bought on the web: Me → Membership. Either way you keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

Purchases made in the iPhone app are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For web purchases, see our refund policy.

My sign-in code hasn't arrived

Codes usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or promotions folder, and use the code from the newest email.

Delete my account

In the app: Me → Privacy & data → Delete my account. It takes effect immediately, as our privacy policy describes.

Add my church

Search for your church on the Church tab. If it says it is not on Flourish yet, invite your pastor, or send us a message with the topic Churches.
